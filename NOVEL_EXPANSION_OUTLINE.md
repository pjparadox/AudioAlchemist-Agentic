# NOVEL_EXPANSION_OUTLINE.md (REVISED)
**Target Length:** 80,000 words
**Structure:** 4 Books, 52 Chapters.

## BOOK I: THE COVENANT (Chapters 1-13)
*Focus: The Mundane World vs. The Encroaching Shadow*

**Chapter 1: The Predator** (Erin)
*   *Ghost Injection:* The Van Hopper trial closing arguments. Inside Erin's head as she manipulates the jury. The physical sensation of lying.

**Chapter 2: The Victory Lap** (Erin)
*   The celebration at the bar. The hollowness of success. Erin's loneliness amidst the crowd.
*   *Sensory:* The smell of expensive gin and cheap ambition.

**Chapter 3: The Hangover** (Erin)
*   *Ghost Injection:* Erin's apartment. Her morning routine. The silence of her life. The cat (Jasper) reacting to... nothing.

**Chapter 4: The Summit** (Erin)
*   The meeting with Karl Gunderson. The Senior Partner offer. The "Devil's Bargain" metaphor made literal.

**Chapter 5: The Descent** (Erin)
*   The drive to the jail. Transition from the glass tower to the concrete underworld. The sensory assault of the prison intake.

**Chapter 6: The Saint in Hell** (Moore)
*   *Ghost Injection:* Father Moore's perspective inside the cell. His prayer routine. The demonic harassment he endures even now.

**Chapter 7: First Contact** (Erin/Moore)
*   The meeting. The clash of worldviews. Erin's skepticism vs. Moore's certainty. The physical details of Moore's hands.

**Chapter 8: The Girl from the North Country** (Emily - Flashback)
*   *Ghost Injection:* Emily leaving home for college. The innocence. The landscape of the farm. The feeling of being watched.

**Chapter 9: The Dormitory** (Emily - Flashback)
*   College life. The freedom. The first subtle signs (smells, cold spots).

**Chapter 10: 3:00 AM** (Erin)
*   The first supernatural event in Erin's apartment. The stopped clock. The smell of sulfur.
*   *Horror:* The psychological terror of the mundane turning wrong.

**Chapter 11: The Prosecution** (Ethan Thomas)
*   *Ghost Injection:* Thomas at church. His genuine faith. Why he hates Moore (he sees him as a heretic).

**Chapter 12: The Strategy** (Erin/Karl)
*   War room. Planning the defense. "Reasonable Doubt" vs "The Truth".

**Chapter 13: The Rain** (Atmosphere)
*   The weather turns. The city is besieged by a storm that mirrors the spiritual warfare.
