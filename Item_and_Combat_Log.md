
### Lady Thunderlord Into Hades (Chapters 20-25)

**Items**
*   **Mangled Dolly:** Starts **Winter Dungeonland** quest. Hard difficulty. Rewards include Logout Re-enabled or Legendary evolving gear.
*   **Mantle of the Undying Chronomancer:** (Epic Cloth). +Stam/Int/Dex. Use: Reduce cooldowns by 24h.
*   **Deadscream's Wraps:** (Epic Leather). +Dex/Stam/Str. Use: Invisibility.
*   **Leg Plates of the Undying:** (Epic Plate). +Stam/Str. Special: Regenerate 1 HP per 4 attacks (Set bonus improves this).
*   **Book of Death and Rebirth:** (Quest Item). Used to transform Mirae.
*   **Dragon Heartstone:** (Quest Item). Offered to Sune.

**Mechanics**
*   **Nativity Pestilence:** Rodekin infection. Infects target. If they survive, they become a Wererat.
*   **Relic Creation:** Destroying objects with strong emotional/magical value sends them to Hades as Relics. (e.g. Burning cash shop books).
*   **Undying:** Subrace. Immune to death (unconscious). High regen.
*   **Thunderlord:** Title/Class. Divine spells.
