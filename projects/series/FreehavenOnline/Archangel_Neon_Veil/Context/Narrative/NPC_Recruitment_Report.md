# NPC Recruitment Assessment: Archangel Project

**Date:** 2026-01-20
**Subject:** Candidate Selection for Synthetic Instantiation (Phase 2)
**Objective:** Identify 1-3 Non-Player Characters (NPCs) from the *Freehaven Online* data cluster to join the current *Neon Veil* asset team (Devin, Mei, Yuki, Alana, Rook). Candidates must demonstrate high utility, unique skill sets not currently represented, and psychological profiles compatible with the "Real World" operation.

## Selection Criteria
1.  **Utility:** Does the asset offer a capability the current team lacks?
2.  **Adaptability:** Can the asset function outside of high-fantasy logic?
3.  **Personality:** Will they synergize or interestingly conflict with the current roster?

---

## Top Candidates

### 1. Alfred (The Butler)
*   **Origin:** Cupcake Keep Staff.
*   **Role:** Logistics / Support / Base Management.
*   **Justification:** The current team is chaotic (Rook), specialized (Alana), or focused on fieldwork (Devin/Mei). They lack a dedicated "Base Mom" or Quartermaster. Alfred is programmed for absolute loyalty and efficient management of property.
*   **Real World Application:** In a synthetic body, Alfred would manage the Elysium safehouse, handle procurement (legal and illegal), cook (unless Pehrom is available), and maintain the team's gear. He provides a stabilizing influence for Rook ("Master Rook, please do not eat the toaster").
*   **Title:** *The Steward*.

### 2. Aztram (The Researcher)
*   **Origin:** Dragonswood Keep (Zarentu's Court).
*   **Role:** Research / Arcane Analyst / "Scientist".
*   **Justification:** Alana covers "Tech" and "Code", but Aztram covers "Deep Theory" and "Magic". In the real world, this translates to theoretical physics, advanced chemistry, or understanding the "Source Code" of reality (The System). He is accustomed to researching impossible problems (The Logout Button).
*   **Real World Application:** Working with Alana to decode the "Hunter's" code or the nature of the "Founder". He represents the bridge between Magic and Science.
*   **Title:** *The Arcanist*.

### 3. Lord Zarentu (The Patron)
*   **Origin:** Dragonswood Keep (Dragon Lord).
*   **Role:** Heavy Hitter / financier / Strategist.
*   **Justification:** If the team needs a "Nuclear Option" or a high-level strategist. Zarentu is ancient, patient, and politically savvy. However, his ego might clash with Rook's.
*   **Real World Application:** Corporate takeover, high-level negotiation (above Devin's paygrade), or combat support (Polymorph).
*   **Title:** *The CEO*.

---

## Recommendation

**Primary Choice: Alfred**
The team desperately needs logistical support. Rook is eating the furniture; Alana is rewiring the building; Yuki is haunting the infirmary. Alfred brings order to domestic chaos.

**Secondary Choice: Aztram**
To support Alana in the "Tech/Magic" overlap.

**Reserved: Zarentu**
Save for a later phase when the stakes escalate to global corporate warfare.

## Rejected Candidates
*   **Sten:** Too inexperienced. Prone to exploding.
*   **Herald Brime:** Skill set (Amplification) is redundant with modern PA systems.
*   **Nothgoran:** Redundant with Rook (The Muscle).
