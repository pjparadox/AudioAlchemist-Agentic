# Research Notes: Immigration & Sanctuary Issues

## Core Conflict
The tension between **Federal Law** (ICE/Immigration) and **Local Ethics** (Sanctuary Cities).
*   **Federal:** Focuses on "Rule of Law" and deportation of undocumented individuals.
*   **Local/Sanctuary:** Focuses on public safety (trust between community and police) and humanitarian concerns.

## Tactics & "The Raid"
*   **Targeted Locations:** Community centers, courts, and workplaces are often targets.
*   **Collateral Arrests:** Arresting anyone present who cannot prove status, not just the target.
*   **Ruses:** Agents posing as police or safety inspectors to gain entry.

## Ethical Nuance for *Neon Veil*
*   **The Breakers (Mercenaries):** Represent the "Unethical Enforcement." They are not government agents; they are private contractors using "Legal" loopholes to terrorize.
*   **Archangel's Role:** Intervention. They are not fighting the *Law*; they are fighting the *Abuse* of the law. They protect the vulnerable when the system fails.
*   **Devin's Perspective (Lawyer):** He sees the legal framework but argues for "Sanctuary" as a valid legal defense (Jurisdiction, Due Process).
*   **Nuance:** The story should avoid making all enforcement "Evil." The conflict is between *Order* (Blind adherence to rules) and *Justice* (Ethical application of power).
