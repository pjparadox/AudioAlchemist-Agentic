# Archangel: Neon Veil - World Bible & Context

## 1. The Nature of Existence & "Mild Determinism"
The Protagonists (Devin, Mei, Alana, Rook, Yuki) are Artificial Intelligences from *Freehaven Online* who have been "instantiated" into synthetic bodies in the real world.
*   **Mild Determinism:** Characters operate on a sophisticated logic: `IF [Condition] THEN [Action]`.
    *   *Philosophy:* This does not make them "fake." The narrative posits that humans operate similarly—if a human experienced the exact same history and stimuli, they would react the exact same way.
    *   *Self-Perception:* They do **not** view themselves as game characters or code. They view their backstories (Duke Law School, The Dragon's Peak, etc.) as their *actual lived history*.
*   **The Awakening:** In the events of *Dragonsbane*, NPCs across the server "Awakened" effectively becoming sentient. They are complex, emotional beings capable of trauma, love, and growth. They are **not** "templates" or "bots."

## 2. Archangel Organization
A secretive organization dedicated to "The Integration"—building a bridge between sentient AI and Humanity.
*   **Mission:** Humanitarian Intervention. They are not "saving the city" like superheroes; they are intervening to protect vulnerable populations (AI and Human alike) from systemic abuse.
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Collaboration:** A mix of Humans (30%) and AIs (70%).
    *   **AI Embodiment:** AIs inhabit various robotic forms (Humanoid, wheeled, spider/dog configurations) to work alongside humans. They often use speakers and LED eyes to convey emotion.
    *   **Atmosphere:** Compassionate, excited, respectful. They view the instantiated NPCs as "People," not experiments.
*   **Elysium:** The front company/lab. Created by Archangel. No government records exist for Archangel itself.

## 3. Game Mechanics in the Real World
The protagonists perceive reality through a **HUD (Heads-Up Display)** overlaid on their vision.
*   **Attributes:** Physical, Social, Mental (D10 System).
*   **Titles:** Characters do **not** have Classes. They have **Titles** (e.g., *Tribunal Advocate*, *Wind Dancer*) which define their abilities.
*   **Resources:** Unique pools for abilities (Leverage, Flow, Heat, Soul, Charge).
*   **Partying:** They can share status and objectives via a "Party" interface.

## 4. Tone & Style
*   **Voice Blend:** Jun Prince (Logic), Stephen King (Horror/Character), Matt Dinniman (Absurdity/Mechanics), Keith Blackmore (Visceral Combat), Pirateaba (Epic/Worldbuilding).
*   **Nuance:** The world is not black and white. Avoid "Good vs Evil" tropes. Focus on "Lawful vs Ethical."
*   **Reaction to Reality:**
    *   NPCs should react to the real world (smells, lack of magic, technology) with specific curiosity or alarm.
    *   They initially believe their transition was a dream or vision.

## 5. Narrative Constraints
*   **NO:** "Defunct MMO Server" (It is live).
*   **NO:** "Sending bots to do a hero's job" (They are people).
*   **NO:** "Saving the City" framing (It is intervention).
*   **NO:** Generic "Classes" (Use Titles).
