# Synthetic Body Mechanics (Archangel: Neon Veil)

The synthetic bodies of the Neon Veil protagonists are designed to mimic the Freehaven Online game mechanics to facilitate empathy with the player experience.

## The Interface (HUD)
*   **Visual Overlay:** Characters perceive a Heads-Up Display (HUD) overlaid on their vision.
*   **Elements:**
    *   **Health/Resource Bars:** Visual representation of their physical integrity and energy (Mana/Stamina).
    *   **Targeting Reticles:** Auto-focus on threats or points of interest.
    *   **System Messages:** Text alerts for "Mission Updates", "Loot", or "Errors".
    *   **Party Frames:** Status of teammates (Health, Location).

## Progression: Titles
*   **No Classes:** Like Freehaven, the synthetic bodies do not have rigid classes.
*   **Titles as Roles:** Progression is tracked via "Titles".
    *   **Current Titles:** Reflect their current capabilities (e.g., *Wind Dancer*, *Tribunal Advocate*).
    *   **Potential Titles:** Goals to be unlocked (e.g., *Dragon Lord* - blocked by hardware limitations).
*   **Effect:** Titles unlock "Skill Trees" or specific sub-routines (e.g., *Techno-Shaman* unlocks "Interface" ability).

## Resource Systems
Each protagonist has a unique resource bar modeled after game mechanics:
*   **Devin:** **Leverage** (Social/Legal Power). Generated by identifying crimes, flaws, or secrets. Spent to "Command" or "De-escalate".
*   **Mei:** **Flow** (Momentum). Generated by movement and dodging. Spent for critical strikes or impossible acrobatics.
*   **Yuki:** **Soul** (Life Force). Generated by proximity to death/spirits (or blood). Spent for healing or necrosis.
*   **Alana:** **Charge** (Battery). Generated by proximity to electricity/data. Spent to hack or overload tech.
*   **Rook:** **Heat** (Rage/Fire). Generated by combat or eating spicy food. Spent for strength feats (or fire breath, if fixed).

## Partying & Empathy
*   **The Link:** The team is "Partied". They share a subconscious data link.
    *   **Shared XP:** Successes grant "XP" (Dopamine hits) to the whole group.
    *   **Location Tracking:** They can always find each other on their internal maps.
*   **Empathy Engine:** The system rewards them for acting like "Players" (Looting, exploring, questing) to align their synthetic psychology with the human minds they are meant to protect/understand.
