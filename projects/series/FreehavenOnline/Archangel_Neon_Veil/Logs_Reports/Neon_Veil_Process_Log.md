## Loop 8: 16th Degree Adversarial Audit (Chapter 4)
**Agent:** Thoth (High Inquisitor) -> Jules (Executioner)
**Date:** 2026-01-20
**Input:** Chapter 4 Draft ("The Ghost in the Machine")

**Critique (Bestseller or Burn It):**
*   **Tone:** The opening scene is too "sitcom". Rook eating ghost peppers and building a blender feels like filler. Cut the slapstick. The stakes need to be higher immediately.
*   **Sensory Details:** The description of the data drive is weak. "Screaming like drowning children" is a good start, but *push* it. Make the sound vibrate in their teeth. Make the light from the drive hurt their eyes.
*   **Pacing:** The hacking scene is too easy. "Root access to your mom"? No. Make Alana struggle. Make her sweat. The code should fight back like a living thing (because it is).
*   **The Attack:** Spider-bots are cliché. Make them nastier. Are they made of recycled surgical tools? Do they scream when they attack?
*   **The Husk:** "Cyber-Frankenstein" is lazy. Describe the horror of the biology. The smell of rotting meat inside the chrome. The sound of wet lungs in a metal chest.
*   **Character Voice:**
    *   **Rook:** Too buffoonish. He is a *Prince*. His arrogance should be regal, not childish. His "Lamp-Blender" needs to be a weapon, not a joke.
    *   **Yuki:** Good, but make her more unsettling. Her hunger should be terrifying to the others.
    *   **Devin:** "Litigation" as an attack is clever, but explain *how*. Is he finding a flaw in the Hunter's logic? Or the corporate structure?
*   **The Twist:** The "Hunter has Devin's face" twist is solid, but the "Warehouse Explodes" cliffhanger is cheap. End on the revelation. The explosion obscures the emotional impact.

**Reforging Directives:**
1.  **Cut the Sitcom:** Remove the "bagpipes falling into a woodchipper" description. It undermines the tension.
2.  **Visceral Hacking:** Alana doesn't just type; she *bleeds* code. The drive burns her fingers.
3.  **Rook's Hoard:** Reframe the "Lamp-Blender" as a crude attempt at Artificing. He is trying to regain power, not just making trash.
4.  **The Husk:** Describe the "Recycled Human" aspect more vividly. Yuki's reaction needs to be visceral horror/hunger.
5.  **Cliffhanger:** End on the face reveal. Cut the explosion. Let the silence of the betrayal hang.

**Verdict:** REVISE IMMEDIATELY.
