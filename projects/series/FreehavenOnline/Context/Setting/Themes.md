# Setting & Themes

## The Intervention
The narrative arc is framed as an "Intervention" to save the city/population, not just a superhero fight.

## The Digital Divide (Theme)
The core conflict echoes real-world wealth and technology inequality.
*   **The Augmented (Early Adopters):** Wealthy, integrated with high-bandwidth Neural Links. They have access to the "Premium" version of reality (AR overlays, instant data, health monitoring).
*   **The Disconnected (Late Adopters/Legacy):** Poor, elderly, or biologically incompatible humans. They rely on "Analog" tech or low-bandwidth connections. They are being pushed out of society, denied services, and policed by private security firms (like **The Breakers**).

## Real World Parallels
*   **ICE / Immigration:** The policing of the Disconnected mirrors real-world immigration enforcement. "Checking Papers" becomes "Checking Bandwidth." Detainment camps are "Processing Centers."
*   **Washington Statutes:** The story references real Washington State laws (RCW) and Federal statutes.
    *   **RCW 9A.84.020 (Failure to Disperse):** Used by Breakers to kettle crowds.
    *   **42 U.S. Code § 1983:** Used by Devin to threaten lawsuits against state actors.

## The Breakers
Private military contractors hired to "Keep the Peace" (suppress the Disconnected).
*   **Tactics:** Sonic suppression, kettling, "preventative detention."
*   **Tech:** High-end, military-grade gear that Alana views as "Bloated Code."
