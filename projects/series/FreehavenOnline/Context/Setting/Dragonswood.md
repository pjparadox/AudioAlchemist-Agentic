# Dragonswood

**Type:** Region / Fortress (Keep).
**Ruler:** Lord Zarentu (White/Prismatic Dragon).
**Location:** Haven Online.

## Features
*   **Dragonswood Keep:** Zarentu's home.
*   **Windroost:** Nearby mountain area, home to wind dragons/whelps.
*   **Arena:** Site of the Dragonswood Tournament.

## Significance
*   Site of the alliance between Toxic Muffins and Zarentu.
*   Location where Cimmerian (Black Dragon) attacked.
*   Place where Frobod received his Draco-Pegasus.
