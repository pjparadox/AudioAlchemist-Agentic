# Durolan

**Type:** City (Capital of Mages).
**Location:** Haven Online.
**Features:**
*   Based on real-world GPS maps (reskinned).
*   Magic brooms sweep streets.
*   **The Smiling Otter:** Inn. Keeper: Jardis. Barmaid: Mia.
*   **Digitization Station:** Stone igloo structure with Angel guards.

## Significance
*   Home base for the Toxic Muffins (Cupcake Keep is located here/nearby).
*   Site of the first "Digitization" protests.
