# Rurodin

**Race:** Ke'bla Elf.
**Role:** Leader of the Ke'bla Elves / Master Baker.
**Location:** Elenandrial (Taiwan).
**Relationships:** Husband of **Helsidol**, Father of **Tiasol**.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Enthusiastic, emotional (about family), proud.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I'm a grandfather! I'm a grandfather!"
    *   "It would be good to know if I'm right in thinking the boy is twenty five or thirty." (Avoiding questions).

## History
*   Led the exile of the Ke'bla. Reunited with his daughter via portrait. Baked the cookies to save Winterfest.
