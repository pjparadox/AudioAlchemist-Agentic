# Lao Fan

**Race:** Goh Tchu (Were-Panda).
**Role:** Leader of the Goh Tchu Clan.
**Location:** Taiwan (Asian Haven).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Deep, rumbling, wise, pacifist.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "We don't eat people. Just imagine."
    *   "Child of Rat... Or do you prefer Plaguebringer?"
    *   "Have you eaten?"

## Lore
*   Allied with the Ke'bla. Removed the "enchantment" (debuff/restriction) from Melpomine and Annette.
