# Perolon

**Race:** San'ta Elf.
**Location:** Winterland.
**Relationships:** Husband of **Alvawynn**, Father of **Reilynn**.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Angry (initially), depressed, then overjoyed.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "We're not doing it!" (Winterfest).
    *   "I want some porridge." (Excusing himself to cry).
    *   "It was her favorite part." (Tinsel).

## History
*   Refused to celebrate Winterfest after Santa killed his daughter. Reunited with her ghost.
