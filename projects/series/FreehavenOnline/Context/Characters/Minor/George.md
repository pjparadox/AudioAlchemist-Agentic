# George

**Type:** Once Man Amalgamation (Boss).
**Location:** Ashfall Crypt.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Screaming, agony.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "Kill me! Kill me please!" (Faces on his body).
    *   "ROAAAAARRRRRRRR!"

## Lore
*   Made of screaming human faces. Defeated by Mel's party, freeing the souls (who then registered in Ashe March).
