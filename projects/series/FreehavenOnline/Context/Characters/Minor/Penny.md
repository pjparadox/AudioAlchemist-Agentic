# Penny

**Race:** Ghost.
**Role:** Clerk at Ashe March Town Hall.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Bureaucratic, efficient, dry humor.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "If you don't remember how you died, see me for form 2b."
    *   "Paperwork makes the world go round."
    *   "Will you be pressing charges?" (Regarding Zan's decapitation).

## Lore
