# Zan

**Race:** Ghost (Formerly Human/Vampire).
**Role:** Hunter.
**Location:** Ashe March (Hades).
**Relationships:** Husband of **Marcy**.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Desperate (as Vampire), Relieved/Happy (as Ghost).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I was just... so... hungry." (As Vampire).
    *   "Marcy... I'm here. I didn't go to Tartarus."
    *   "I liked the frelling vampires better." (When Leroy broke his hands).

## History
*   Hunter who went to Hades to find his wife. Turned into a vampire by Dorian. Begged for death to save his soul. Decapitated by Frobod. Resurrected as a Ghost. Hates Disco.
