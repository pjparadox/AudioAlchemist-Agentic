# Krynn Icebeard

**Race:** Dwarf.
**Role:** Trader in Arctica.
**Companions:** **Direfoxes** (Mounts), **Ice Golems** (Guards).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Hearty, boisterous, trader mentality.
*   **Accent:** Scottish/Dwarven Brogue ("Ye," "Me").
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "Hellooooo down there."
    *   "You wound me! How can you not know the likes of Thoram Icebeard?"
    *   "Who do you think I am? My foxes are the best foxes in... all of Arctica."
