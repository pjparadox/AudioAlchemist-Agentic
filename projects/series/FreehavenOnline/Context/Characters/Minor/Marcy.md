# Marcy

**Race:** Ghost (NPC).
**Location:** Ashe March (Hades).
**Relationships:** Wife of **Zan**.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Loving, protective, sensible.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "Damn fool. We're long lived."
    *   "I won't have it! Not if there's even a chance you're damning yourself."

## History
*   Resident of Ashe March. Sent the party to find Zan. Refused to let him live as a vampire if it meant damnation. Punch-hugged him when he returned as a ghost.
