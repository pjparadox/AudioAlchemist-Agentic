# Character Development Log

Tracks the emotional arcs, turning points, and worldview shifts of characters per chapter.

## Major Characters

**Justine / Melpomine**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 1:** Designing monsters at work. Feels professional satisfaction but hint of loneliness (talks to AI).

*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 11:** Takes charge of the Keep. Handles Alfred's "Fourth Wall" breaks. Admits to Alfred that she is Stabbies' sister (Legacy Account).
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 14:** Gambles aggressively on Frobod. Uses game mechanics (Mount Cloning) to "Hack" the joust.
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 16:** Wins Single Combat. Shows mercy to Sedid (Handkerchief). Taunts Krogen to make him reckless.
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 18:** Sacrifices herself (Throws the match) for Frobod. Shows selflessness/leadership.

*   **Hades - Prologue:** Grief stricken by Mirae's "Death". Hope reignited by the "Online" status. Determined to find her.
