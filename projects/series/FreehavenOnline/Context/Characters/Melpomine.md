# Melpomine (Justine)

**Role:** Protagonist, Guild Leader of Toxic Muffins.
**Real Name:** Justine Nicks.
**In-Game Names:** Melpomine, Stabbies (inherited), Scootiwutams (alias), MarineLu (alias).

## Appearance
*   **Melpomine:** Half-Elf. Lithe, strong lean muscles, long blonde ponytail. Green eyes.
*   **Scootiwutams:** Dwarf Female. Red full plate armor with gold flecks. Nose ring. Pigtails. Stone colored skin.
*   **MarineLu:** Human (Taiwanese). Martial Artist.

## Personality
*   **Real World:** Game designer (Intuitive Programmer). Creates monsters and mounts (Elechoppers).
*   **Motivation:** Inherited her deceased brother Jordan's account to keep his memory alive. Seeks to reconnect with his old guildmates.
*   **Traits:** Competent, protective, resourceful. Uses "Bite" (Lightning's Bite staff) or "Exsanguinator" (Sword).

## History
*   **Background:** Brother Jordan died 8 years prior to *Dragonsbane*. She tricked customer support into giving her access to his account.
*   **Dragonsbane:** Formed the new Toxic Muffins. Won the Dragonswood Tournament (tie with Frobod). Forged alliance with Lord Zarentu.
*   **Relationships:**
    *   **Jordan:** Deceased brother. Original owner of "Stabbies".
    *   **Frobod:** Romantic interest.
    *   **Mirae:** Best friend (lost to digitization).
    *   **TakoStar:** Close friend.

## Abilities
*   **Tanking:** Experienced tank (Scootiwutams).
*   **Martial Arts:** Proficient with staff (Bite) and unarmed combat (MarineLu).
*   **Magic:** Can use elemental magic (Thunder Bridge).
*   **Developer Perks:** Access to "Developer Edition" items like the Elechoppers (spirit-powered motorcycles).
