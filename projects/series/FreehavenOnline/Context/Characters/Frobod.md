# Frobod

**Role:** Grand Champion, Main Tank/DPS.
**Real World:** Father to a young daughter. From Las Vegas.

## Appearance
*   **Race:** Human.
*   **Class:** Death Knight (mix of fighter and necromancy).
*   **Gear:** Charcoal black plate armor ("Deadplate"). Chest piece shaped like a skull. Wields a massive greatsword.
*   **Mount:** Black Draco-Pegasus (Gift from Zarentu).

## Personality
*   **Motivation:** Desperate to log out and return to his daughter.
*   **Traits:** Stoic, fierce, protective. Prone to anger when trapped.
*   **Relationships:**
    *   **Melpomine:** Romantic interest.
    *   **Daughter:** Primary motivation.

## Abilities
*   **Combat:** Heavy melee hitter. Uses death magic (dark energy bolts).
*   **Titles:** Grand Champion of Dragonswood.
