# SalemLilly (Lilly)

**Real Name:** Lilly Sutherland
**Role:** Dagger Hex Summoner (Hybrid Rogue/Mage)
**Race:** Goblin
**Background:** Olympic Figure Skater.
**Relationship:** Married to **Pehrom** (Rob).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Competitive, aggressive, capitalist, proud.
*   **Accent:** Boston/Jersey (Heavy).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I hate her." (About Tiasol).
    *   "If this were realistic, we'd be able to take one of those ice daggers."
    *   "Fifty percent for me, and you can divide the rest." (Negotiating contract).

## Abilities
*   **Dagger Hexing:** Casts spells via dagger strikes (e.g., Cinderburst).
*   **Taming:** Can bind creatures (requires low health + binding circle).
*   **Summoning:**
    *   **Stone Throwing Devil:** Red skin, horns.
    *   **(Fluxed) Stone Throwing Bunny:** Pink rabbit, eats carrots.
*   **Skating:** Professional level.

## Items
*   **Ultima Blades of the San'ta:** Epic Skate Blades. +Dex/Cha.
*   **Evolving Helm:** +5 Taming, Halves Negotiation Time.

## Story Arc
*   **Winter Dungeonland:** Joined the party to log out and see family (expecting to return). Tamed the Flux Bunny. Challenged Tiasol to a skate-off.
