# Pahrem (Leah)

**Real Name:** Leah
**Role:** Healer (Priestess)
**Race:** Goblin
**Accent:** Boston/Jersey (Voice filter).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Snarky, tough, "No nonsense."
*   **Phonetics:** "Ova here," "Gonna," "Ain't," "G'nades."
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "What am I chopped liva?"
    *   "I got your heals and a crap ton of g'nades!"
    *   "Don't suck the root. Ya gotta bite' em' with everything ya got..." (Quoting Pehrom).

## Abilities
*   **G'nades:** Goblin Alchemy explosives. Healing, Fire, Acid.
*   **Smite:** Holy damage.
*   **Resurrection:** Can revive players.

## Items
*   **Mantle of the Undying Chronomancer:** Epic Cloth Shoulders. Reduces cooldowns.
*   **Low Hades Garb of the Healer:** Epic Cloth Chest. Boosts healing.
*   **Turkeyshoot Cornucopia:** Creates buff food.

## Titles/Achievements
*   **Turkanator:** Defeated Turkakodzilla in 1, 3, and 5 person challenges.

## Story Arc
*   **Hades:** Vital healer for the group.
*   **Winter Dungeonland:** Participated in Turkeyshoot. Accompanied the party to Arctica.
