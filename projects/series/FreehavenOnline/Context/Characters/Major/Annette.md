# Annette

**Race:** Human -> **Rodekin** (Wererat).
**Role:** Priestess/Chosen of Winter.
**Guild:** Member of **Toxic Muffins**.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Initially traumatized, then eager to learn, protective ("Rat Mother").
*   **Conversational Style:** Curious about magic. Defends her "family" (Jason/James).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I didn't eat your chicken!"
    *   "I want this." (Choosing to become Rodekin).
    *   "I'm Jewish. Is that okay?" (Asking about Santa quest).

## Magic
*   **Healing Arrows** (Flux Mutation): Shoots allies to heal them.
*   **Spirit Communion:** Can commune with spirits (Magpie, Winter).

## Background
*   Rescued from a vampire dungeon in Hades. Chose to become a Rodekin for protection.
