# Melpomine (Justine)

**Real Name:** Justine
**Role:** Guild Master of Toxic Muffins
**Race:** Half-Elf (Main)
**Class:** Monk / Thunderlord (Paladin of Sune)

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Professional, authoritative but accessible, slightly cynical, "Gamer" savvy.
*   **Keywords:** "Agro," "Mob," "Spec," "DPS," "Buff up."
*   **Conversational Style:** Often explains game mechanics to NPCs (e.g., explaining "Classes" to Zarentu). Uses sarcasm to defuse tension.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I wave my middle finger in the air and shout 'INFERNO!!!' then pump my fist in the air and say 'Let's ride.'"
    *   "We don't need to discuss what would have happened to your friend here... and the city of Durolan."
    *   "I'm Justine." (To Frobod).

## Alts / Forms
*   **Lilly:** Magical Human / Wererat (Rodekin).
    *   **Traits:** Rage vs Calm meter. Can burn rage for speed.
    *   **Forms:** Nezumi (Rat), War Form, Rattadon.
    *   **Magic:** Rite of Gratitude, Rite of Seeking Stone (requires spirit favor).
    *   **Voice:** Chittery/Growly in war form.
*   **Scootiwutams:** Dwarf Paladin. Used for resurrection (has mana).
    *   **Voice:** Scottish Brogue (Roleplayed).

## Background
*   **VWI Developer:** Former employee of Virtual World Innovations. Knows the "code" behind the world.
*   **Creations:** Created the **Elechoppers** (Elemental Motorcycles) and **Once Men** (Monsters).
*   **Family:** Brother **Jordan** (Deceased). Sees the guild as her new family.

## Key Equipment
*   **Bite:** Elemental Staff. AoE Taunt. Sparkles with electricity.
*   **Fists of the Thunderlord:** Epic Leather Gloves. Lightning/Sonic damage.
*   **Darksteel Chakram:** Thrown weapon. Reforms on wrist.
*   **Effigy of Protection:** Heku magic item (one-time death prevention).
*   **Elechoppers:** "Big Red" (Fire). Can fly.

## Story Arc
*   **Dragonsbane:** Won the tournament. Allied with Lord Zarentu.
*   **Hades:** Led the rescue of Mirae. Became a **Thunderlord** (Paladin of Sune) after being incinerated by Sune and resurrected. Rescued Zan.
*   **Winter Dungeonland:** Led the party to Arctica. Learned **Skating**. Used Rodekin alt to help Annette. Defeated Great Uncle Winter. Stuck in game (Frobod took the logout). Kissed Frobod goodbye.
