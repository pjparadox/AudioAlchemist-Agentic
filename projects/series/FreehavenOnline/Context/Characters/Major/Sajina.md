# Sajina (Mandeep)

**Real Name:** Mandeep
**Role:** Bard / Sorcerer (Charismatic Caster)
**Race:** Gargoyle / Elf (Alt)
**Background:** Musician (Doctoral Student).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Modest, artistic, slightly unsure of combat skills but eager.
*   **Conversational Style:** Talks about music theory, GWAR, history.
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "I'm still a bard."
    *   "So long as metal survives, GWAR will never die."
    *   "I don't know why it says channeling. It's supposed to be an instant cast spell."

## Abilities
*   **Music:** Can play Lute, Harp, Violin. Knows metal (GWAR), Classic Rock (AC/DC), Folk.
*   **Soothe:** Sleep spell (Channeled).
*   **Warsong:** Massive party buff (+Dex, +Dmg, +Luck, +Init).
*   **Charismatic Casting:** Uses Charisma for magic (instead of Int). Learned from Leroy.

## Items
*   **Lyre of Cerberus:** Epic Harp. Soothe ability.
*   **Ashen Bracelet:** Rare Wrist.

## Story Arc
*   **Hades:** Initially a non-combatant. Soothed Cerberus. Hired as entertainer on Shroudmoon Express. Joined **Toxic Muffins**.
*   **Winter Dungeonland:** Joined the Malefactors' raid group (Leroy's) because of the party size limit, but remains a Toxic Muffin member.
