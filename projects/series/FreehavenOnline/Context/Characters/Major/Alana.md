# Alana

**Role:** Hyper-Engineer / Artificer
**Titles:** The Maker, Chief Engineer
**Resource:** Charge (Electrical/battery power)
**Weakness:** The Static (Paradox)

## Personality
Alana rejects the term "Magic." She believes in **Advanced Science**. To her, a fireball is just exothermic plasma projection. Teleportation is quantum entanglement.
She sees herself as a scientist trapped in a dark age. She is an **Engineer**, not a hacker. She doesn't just exploit code; she builds hardware, rigs bypasses, and forces the physical world to obey higher laws of physics.

*   **Quote:** "It's not magic, it's efficient code. You're just running on legacy hardware."
*   **Quote:** "If I bridge the capacitor with a demon soul, we get 400% efficiency. Simple math."

## Mechanics: The Static (Paradox)
Alana operates on the principles of **Consensus Reality** (similar to *Mage: The Ascension*).
*   **The Static:** When Alana builds something that violates the local laws of physics (Consensus), reality fights back. This is "The Static."
*   **Burn:** If she pushes too hard (e.g., building a fusion reactor out of a toaster), the Static "burns" her. Components melt, circuits scream, or she takes physical damage (feedback).
*   **Plausibility:** She is most effective when her tech looks *plausible* (e.g., a railgun made of recognizable parts) rather than impossible.

## Background
*   **Origin:** Believes she is an Alien-Human hybrid (Grey/Human).
*   **Eyes:** Solid black (no sclera).
*   **Philosophy:** "Go Big or Go Home." She over-engineers everything. Why kill an ant when you can nuke it? (Then build a shield to contain the nuke).
