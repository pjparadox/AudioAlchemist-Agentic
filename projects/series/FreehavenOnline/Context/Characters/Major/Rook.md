# Rook Vhal

**Role:** DPS / Bruiser.
**Race:** Dragon (Synthetic Body).
**Resource:** Heat.

## Personality
*   **Hoarding:** Not obsessed with generic gold. Covets **Beauty, Quality, and Excitement**. Hoards well-crafted items, things that shine with artistic merit, or trophies of battle.
*   **Temperament:** Generally laid back and easygoing.
*   **Wrath:** Hard to provoke, but terrible when unleashed. Becomes ruthless and obsessed.
*   **Delusion:** Believes he is a Dragon Lord stuck in a polymorph.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Rumbling, arrogant but appreciative of quality.
*   **Keywords:** "Shiny," "Exquisite," "Hoard," "Heat."
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "This craftsmanship... acceptable."
    *   "I am Cinder Vhal."

## History
*   Instantiated NPC. Awakening involved destroying a room to find his "Hoard" (High quality furniture).
