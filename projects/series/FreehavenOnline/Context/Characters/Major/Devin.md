# Devin Quell

**Role:** Tribunal Advocate / Social Tank
**Titles:** The Counsel, Esquire
**Resource:** Leverage
**Weakness:** The Letter of the Law (Cannot violate his own code)

## Personality
Devin acts as the "Face" of the party. He is a high-functioning sociopath (mild determinism) who operates on **IF/THEN** logic.
He views combat as a legal proceeding. Every attack against him is a "Tort." Every enemy action is a "Violation."
He plans to level up by **taking a case to court and winning.**

*   **Quote:** "Objection. Hearsay."
*   **Quote:** "You have the right to remain silent. I suggest you exercise it before I garnish your wages."

## Mechanics: Leverage
Devin generates **Leverage Points** by identifying legal or procedural violations during conflict.
*   **Gain Leverage:**
    *   *Witnessing a crime:* "RCW 9A.40.040 - Unlawful Imprisonment" (+1 Leverage).
    *   *Scanning an enemy:* "RCW 9A.84.010 - Riot" (+1 Leverage).
    *   *Finding Evidence:* "Incriminating Data" (+3 Leverage).

*   **Spend Leverage:**
    *   **Injunction (Cost: 2):** Forces an enemy to stop an action (CC/Stun).
    *   **Objection (Cost: 1):** Interrupts an enemy spell or ability (Counter).
    *   **Settlement (Cost: 5):** Forces a non-violent resolution or retreat.
    *   **Garnish (Cost: 3):** Debuffs enemy stats (e.g., "Garnishing Strength").

## Goals
*   **The Courtroom Arc:** Devin intends to find a client, build a case, and take it to a real-world court. He believes winning a trial is the ultimate XP gain.
