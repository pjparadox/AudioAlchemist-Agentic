# Mirae (Amy)

**Real Name:** Amy
**Role:** Healer (Archer/Nature Magic)
**Race:** Sea Elf / **The Undying** (Mummy)
**Status:** Digitized (Real body deceased). Stuck in game.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
*   **Tone:** Optimistic, kind, "Big Sister" energy (even though she's younger).
*   **Conversational Style:** Emotionally intelligent. Often comforts others (e.g., comforting the Doll in Flux).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "It's our first holiday together in my new life."
    *   "I can't die! How cool is that?"
    *   "Be brave little one, and let Winter inside."

## Traits
*   **The Undying:** Subrace acquired in Hades.
    *   **Immortality:** Cannot die. Drops to 0 HP and goes unconscious. Regenerates.
    *   **Heku Magic:** Can create Effigies (e.g., Protection).
    *   **Death's Insight:** Free travel to/from Hades.
    *   **Alive:** Counts as "double alive" for life detection.

## Relationships
*   **Tako (Chris):** Boyfriend. Protective but independent.
*   **Melpomine:** "Big Sister" figure.
*   **Sten:** Goblin boy she befriended in Hades.

## Key Equipment
*   **Green Magic Arrows:** Healing Arrows (learned/adapted).
*   **Elechopper:** Earth (Green).

## Story Arc
*   **Dragonsbane:** Digitized to escape quadriplegia. Presumed dead.
*   **Hades:** Found "alive" in Hades. Rescued by Toxic Muffins. Transformed into The Undying via the *Book of Death and Rebirth*.
*   **Winter Dungeonland:** Helped redeem Great Uncle Winter. Used Heku magic to save Mel.
