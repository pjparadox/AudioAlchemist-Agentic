# Pehrom & Pahrem

## Pehrom
*   **Role:** Guild Chef.
*   **Race:** Goblin.
*   **Real World:** Lost both arms and part of a thigh in a shark attack (diving accident).
*   **Traits:** Passionate about cooking. New Jersey accent.
*   **Ability:** Discovered how to make VR food taste real.

## Pahrem
*   **Role:** Healer (Replaced Mirae).
*   **Race:** Goblin Female.
*   **Style:** Green skin, purple hair. Khaki robes with dark halo.
*   **Relation:** Pehrom's in-game sister.
*   **Abilities:** Alchemy (Acid grenades), Healing.
