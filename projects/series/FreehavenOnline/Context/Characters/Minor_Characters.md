# Minor Characters (Freehaven Online Series)

This document lists minor characters, NPCs, and background figures appearing in the *Freehaven Online* series.

## NPCs (Haven Online / Freeworld)

### Dragonswood & Durolan
*   **Lord Zarentu:** White/Prismatic Dragon Lord of Dragonswood Keep. Can shape-shift into a human (Roman General style). Powerful, wealthy, an ally to the Toxic Muffins.
*   **Aztram:** Half-Elf Wizard. Researcher for Zarentu. Created the "Logout" research project.
*   **Herald Brime:** Zarentu's announcer. Casts amplification spells.
*   **Alfred:** NPC Butler at Cupcake Keep. Loyal, capable of managing guild property.
*   **Sten:** Goblin Squire. Laurelade's dance partner. Exploded by Cimmerian's trap (likely to respawn).
*   **Jardis:** Innkeeper of *The Smiling Otter* in Durolan. Named NPC (Hotfix update). Collects hero autographs.
*   **Mia:** Barmaid at *The Smiling Otter*. Named NPC.
*   **Nothgoran:** Dragon Warrior (Charcoal Drake). Boss of the Windroost. Participated in the tournament.
*   **Lady Lord Sedin:** Tournament champion (Dragon).
*   **Lord Maadzan:** Tournament champion (Dragon).
*   **Lady Ashtara:** Tournament champion (Dragon).
*   **Archibald & Lyle:** Gate guards at Dragonswood Keep.

### Antagonists
*   **Cimmerian:** Ancient Black Dragon. Leader of the "Death Cult". Wants to usher in a new era of dragon kind. Caused the "Rip of Worlds".
*   **Raika:** Yellow Lightning Dragon (Windroost Boss).
*   **Ridgewood Bandits:** Generic enemies.

### Hades (Book 3)
*   **Aluwyn:** Old Cleric. Knowledgeable about the Underworld rivers.
*   **Rauel:** Mage at the Lodge of Arcanum. Can make portals.
*   **Marulan:** Dismissive NPC at the Lodge.
*   **Lorak the Messenger:** Red-skinned demon messenger. Delivered letter from Leroy Jensen.

## Players (Freehaven Online)

### Toxic Muffins (Extended)
*   **Pehrom:** Goblin Chef. Real-life amputee (lost arms to shark). Discovered "Taste" in VR.
*   **Pahrem:** Goblin Healer. Pehrom's in-game sister. Replaced Mirae as healer. Alchemy expert (Grenades).
*   **MarineLu:** Melpomine's martial artist alt.
*   **Stabbies:** Melpomine's deceased brother's character (Human Rogue).

### Others
*   **Leroy Jensen:** Pro-gamer. Member of "The Malefactors". Friend of Jordan (Stabbies).
*   **Elondriel:** Twilight Elf. "Soc" gamer. Fishing buddy.
*   **LadyPoints:** Twilight Elf. "Soc" gamer. Elondriel's partner.
*   **Biskon:** Half-Orc. Fisherman.
*   **Ashlynn:** Human female. Protestor at the Digitization Station. Warned Melpomine about deaths.
*   **Ceris:** Elf male. Protestor.
*   **Payneskill:** Player who died soloing ground troops. Resurrected by Mirae.
*   **Sleeve:** Human jouster. Tried to steal Frobod's gold.

## Real World (Mentioned)

*   **Timothy Buck:** Father of a victim (seen on news).
