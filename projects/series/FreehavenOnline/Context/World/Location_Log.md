# Location Log

Tracks locations visited, their descriptions, and associated lore.

## Haven / Freehaven Online
**Durolan (City of Mages)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 2:** Stone buildings, mage towers (skyscrapers). Magic brooms sweeping streets. Purple crystal mailboxes.
*   **Lodge of Arcanum (Hades Ch 1):** Meeting place for mages. Portal chamber with "Borromean link" floor pattern.
**The Pond (Durolan)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 2:** Peaceful. Water spiders (Fairies) eaten by bass. Justine's ritual spot.
**Athelon (Human Capital)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 4:** Overcrowded spawn point. NPC Marketplace.
**Smiling Otter Inn**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 5:** Rustic. Smells of beef stew (which tastes like warm water). Meeting place for the party.
**Ridgewood**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 5:** Farming area outside Athelon. Dirt roads. Bandit territory.
**Windroost (Dungeon)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 6:** Mountain cave. Full of eggs.
*   **Interior:** Hatchery (slime/goo), Boss Room (Supermarket sized, open balcony).
**Cupcake Keep (Guild Hall)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 9:** Located in Durolan.
*   **Exterior:** Overgrown garden, target dummies, moat. Gate has a giant cupcake charred into it.
*   **Interior:** Gloomy/Gothic initially. Living room has a holographic TV.
*   **Expansion (Ch 11):** Generates new rooms for members (e.g., Mirae's Hot Tub).
*   **Kitchen (Ch 20):** Pehrom's domain. Smells of real food.
**Durolan Town Square**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 10:** Open market. Site of Archangel's announcement.
*   **Digitization Station (Ch 20):** Stone igloo with angel statues. Players enter to upload consciousness.
**Dragonswood Keep**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 14:** Tournament grounds. Festive atmosphere (tents, minstrels).
*   **Arena:** Dirt track for jousting. Noble's box.
*   **Dining Hall (Ch 15):** Long table where Zarentu eats chickens.
*   **The Gullet (Ch 18):** Secret Dungeon inside Cimmerian's stomach. Pink, veiny, bouncy walls. Smells of death.
**Fire and Soul (Tavern)**
*   **Dragonsbane - Ch 10:** Soc tavern in Durolan. Elf musician playing lute.
**Temple of Sune**
*   **Hades - Ch 3:** Rustic. Wind chimes. Granite altar with lightning bolt stone.
**The Road to Hell (Dungeon)**
*   **Hades - Ch 6:** Southwest Desert (Real World location). Cave entrance.
*   **Interior:** Bioluminescent mushrooms (Blue/Violet). Underground river/waterfall.
*   **River Lethe:** Crossing point.
