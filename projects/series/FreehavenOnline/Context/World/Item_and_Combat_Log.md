## Mechanics

**Aliases**
*   **Description:** Allows a player to swap "Identities" (Appearance, Name, Race, Reputation).
*   **Constraint:** Requires being out of sight OR alerts nearby NPCs/Players to the deception.

**Player+ (Icon)**
*   **Description:** A blue cross icon indicating the player is "Incapacitated" in real life (e.g., Quadriplegic).
*   **Significance:** Community marker for players with disabilities.

**Merge Stats**
*   **Description:** Experience points from ALL characters on an account (Freeworld + Haven) are pooled. The highest stat becomes the base, points are refunded/added.
*   **Result:** Long-time players (like Justine) become overpowered immediately.

**Thunder Bridge (Spell)**
*   **Origin:** Freeworld.
*   **Description:** Turns the caster/party into lightning to travel instantly to a known location.
*   **Cost:** High Manna. Causes nausea.
*   **Reaction:** Haven guards view it as "Alien Magic".

**Logout Lock**
*   **Description:** Logout button greyed out. Customer Support disabled.
*   **Penalty:** Forceful removal causes brain damage/death (per Archangel).

**Taste (New Mechanic)**
*   **Description:** Initially, game food tastes like water.
*   **Correction:** Cooking manually (real world techniques) or using specific skills unlocks flavor. Discovered by Tako/Mirae.

**Digitization**
*   **Description:** Permanent upload of consciousness to the game.
*   **Risk:** 10-20% failure rate (Death).
*   **Reward:** "Pioneer" Title. Immortality.

**Permadeath (NPC/Player?)**
*   **Event:** Groth (Orc Player) killed by Zarentu. Status marked as "Dead" (not respawning).
*   **Implication:** Violence in "Safe Zones" or by admin-level NPCs might be permanent.

**Lost Status**
*   **Description:** Players who die during Digitization appear "Online" but "Unknown".
*   **Quest:** *The Lost Champion* aims to restore them.

**Bardic Magic**
*   **Spell:** *The Dead Can Dance*. Forces undead to dance. Radius increases with more musicians.
*   **Requirement:** Charisma + Performance.
