# Game Mechanics

## Core System: D10 Attributes
Freehaven Online uses a D10 (ten-sided die) system.
*   **Resolution:** To determine the outcome of an action, the system rolls a number of D10s equal to `Attribute + Ability`.
*   **Success:** Every die that rolls a 6 or higher is a "Success".
*   **Damage:** Excess successes (beyond the first) are often added to the base damage of an attack.
*   **Resistance:** Defenders roll `Stamina + Endurance` (or relevant resistance stats). Each success negates one point of incoming damage.

## Attributes & Stats
Attributes are the core statistics of a character. They can only be raised via "Point Breaks".
*   **Physical:** Strength, Dexterity, Stamina.
*   **Social:** Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance.
*   **Mental:** Perception, Intelligence, Wits.

## Health & Resources
*   **Health:** Base 10 for most humanoids. Damage subtracts directly from this pool.
*   **Death:** Reaching 0 health results in death. Characters respawn at their "Bind Point" with a temporary "Death Penalty" debuff (15 minutes).
*   **Manna:** Mystical energy used to power spells.
*   **Regeneration:** Health and Manna regenerate over time, accelerated by food ("Hearty meal") or rest.

## Progression: Point Breaks
*   **No Levels:** There are no traditional levels (1-100).
*   **Point Break (Ding):** Instead of leveling, players earn "Point Breaks".
*   **Mechanism:** A Point Break awards 10 points to distribute among attributes.
*   **Cost:** Raising a stat becomes exponentially more expensive as it gets higher.

## Identity & Titles (Class Replacement)
*   **Aliases:** A single account can have multiple "Aliases" (identities), each with unique names, appearances, and reputation scores (e.g., Melpomine vs. Scootiwutams).
*   **Titles:** "Classes" do not strictly exist. Instead, players define themselves by "Titles" earned through gameplay (e.g., *Dragonslayer*, *Grand Champion*, *Paladin of Helios*).
    *   Titles can confer stat bonuses or social status (e.g., Grand Champion grants +2 Dance).
    *   Neon Veil Protagonists use this system to define their roles without rigid class structures.

## Group Mechanics
*   **Partying:** Players can form a "Party".
    *   **Shared HUD:** Party members see each other's health/manna bars in the UI.
    *   **Communication:** "Party Chat" allows telepathic-like communication over distance.
    *   **Loot:** Loot can be distributed via "Rolls" (D10) or assigned by the leader.
    *   **Mount Sharing:** Party members can ride each other's mounts (if multi-passenger).

## Unique Systems
*   **Digitization:** Uploading consciousness. Success = "Pioneer" title. Failure = "Lost" (trapped in Hades).
*   **Logout Block:** Current crisis prevents logging out.
*   **Taste:** New update allows realistic taste (discovered by Pehrom).
*   **Reputation:** Numerical value determining NPC reactions. unlocking higher tiers (Neutral -> Friend -> Honored -> Revered -> Exalted) grants discounts and access to special items.

## Items
*   **Elechoppers:** Spirit-powered motorcycles (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Ether). Flying mounts.
*   **Limited Edition (LE):** Rare items (pets, mounts) from events.
*   **Developer Edition (DE):** Staff items that copy stats from other items.
