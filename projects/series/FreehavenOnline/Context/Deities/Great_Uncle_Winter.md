# Great Uncle Winter (Santa)

**Role:** Holiday Deity of Winterfest.
**Location:** San'ta Vale (Arctica).
**Lore:** Turns evil/corrupted if he doesn't get cookies.
**Abilities:**
*   **Missile Toes:** Foot ballistas.
*   **Enlarge:** Grows massive.
**History:** Was corrupted by a lack of cookies (rats ate them). Killed Reilynn. Redeemed by the Toxic Muffins and Ke'bla cookies.
