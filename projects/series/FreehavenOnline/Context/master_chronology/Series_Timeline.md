# Series Timeline

## The Golden Age
*   **Launch:** Haven Online and Freeworld Online launch.
*   **Jordan's Death:** Jordan Nicks (Stabbies) dies.
*   **Justine's Takeover:** Justine (Melpomine) takes over Jordan's account 6 months later.

## Book 1: Dragonsbane
*   **The Merge:** Haven and Freeworld merge. Players cannot log out.
*   **Toxic Muffins Reborn:** Melpomine recruits TakoStar, Mirae, Frobod, Laurelade.
*   **Dragonswood Tournament:** Guild wins. Frobod named Grand Champion.
*   **Cimmerian's Attack:** Black Dragon attacks the feast. Zarentu fights back.
*   **Digitization Crisis:** Digitization stations open. Players start disappearing ("Lost").
*   **Mirae's Sacrifice:** Mirae attempts digitization and becomes "Lost".
*   **Alliance:** Toxic Muffins align with Dragonswood.

## Book 2: Winter Dungeonland
*   **Holiday Event:** Saving "Santa" (Great Uncle Winter).
*   **Quest:** Focusing on reuniting families.

## Book 3: LadyThunderlord Into Hades
*   **Rescue Mission:** Entering Hades to find the "Lost" players (Mirae).
*   **LadyThunderlord:** Amy joins the quest.
*   **Resolution:** Rescuing souls from the Underworld.

## The Archangel Event (Neon Veil)
*   **Premise:** "Archangel" organization instantiates NPCs into synthetic bodies in the real world.
*   **Project Neon Veil:**
    *   **Subjects:** Devin, Mei, Yuki, Alana, Rook.
    *   **Location:** Elysium (Synthetic Body Lab).
    *   **Status:** Characters believe their fake backstories.
