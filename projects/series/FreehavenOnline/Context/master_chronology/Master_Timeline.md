# Freehaven Online - Master Chronology

This file tracks the absolute timeline of events across the series (Dragonsbane, Winter Dungeonland, LadyThunderlord into Hades, and Archangel: Neon Veil).

## Format
**[Book Title] - [Chapter #]**
*   **In-Game Event:** Event description.
*   **Real-World Event:** Event description (if applicable).
*   **Key Revelation:** Who learned what?

---

## Timeline

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 1**
*   **Real-World Event:** Justine (Game Designer) works with Sal (AI) to design the "Once Man" monster and "Elechopper" mounts for the upcoming expansion.
*   **Key Revelation:** "Intuitive Programming" allows design via acting/speech rather than code.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 2**
*   **Real-World Event:** Justine reflects on her brother Jordan's death (Heart attack at 19, 8 years ago). She used her Korean license ("JP Nicks") to claim his account.
*   **In-Game Event (Haven):** Justine logs into Jordan's account ("Spur27"). Finds his character "Stabbies" (Human) and "Scootiwutams" (Dwarf Paladin). Noticed active buffs cast by him 2 years ago (frozen by logout).
*   **Key Revelation:** The "Toxic Muffins" guild has 1 member (Jordan). His friends list is empty (wiped by merge to her account).

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 3**
*   **Real-World Event:** Night before the "Freehaven" merge.
*   **In-Game Event (Haven):** Justine visits the lake one last time. Summons "Ethereal Flutterdrake (LE)" and names it "Doom".
*   **Interaction:** Meets Elondriel and LadyPoints (Elves) who accuse her of buying the account ("Credit Card Player").
*   **Emotional Moment:** The 2-year-old buffs expire. Justine destroys the anniversary letter. Feels release.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 4**
*   **Real-World Event:** The Merge goes live. Justine logs in.
*   **In-Game Event (Freehaven):** System merges all characters (Haven + Freeworld) into one "Player" pool. Stats are combined (resulting in overpowered attributes).
*   **Character Creation:** Justine creates "Melpomine" (Half-Elf).
*   **Mechanic:** "Aliases" allow switching appearances/identities (e.g., to Scootiwutams).
*   **Action:** Melpomine flees the crowded spawn point. Meets TakoStar (Half-Elf Rogue) in Athelon.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 5**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine (as Scootiwutams) accepts a "Limited Edition Guild Quest" from the Toxic Muffins interface: *Dragonsbane - Part 1* (Kill 50,000 Dragons).
*   **Recruitment:** Recruits TakoStar, Frobod (Death Knight), Laurelade (Gnome Mage), and Mirae (Sea Elf Healer) to the guild.
*   **Revelation (Mirae):** Mirae reveals she is a quadriplegic in real life ("Player+" icon). Justine reveals she is a Dev (partial truth) and inherited the LE account.
*   **Action:** The party decides to farm dragons at "Three Winds".

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 6**
*   **In-Game Event:** The party flies to Windroost on Justine's Elechoppers.
*   **Action:** Combat outside the dungeon. Justine learns "Mounted Combat".
*   **Lore:** Elechoppers have cosmetic weather effects (Rain, Aurora, etc.).

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 7**
*   **In-Game Event:** Windroost Dungeon Run. Justine "pulls the room" (Chaos). Frobod/Tako die and are resurrected.
*   **Boss Fight:** Tilashia (Dragon Hybrid) and Raika (Lightning Dragon).
*   **Loot:** Frobod gets "Windroost Hatchling" (Epic Pet). Justine gets "Lightning's Bite" (Staff). Mirae/Laurelade trade wand/ribbon.
*   **Quest Update:** Party receives "Tournament Invitation" (LE Quest) for Lord Zarentu's "Contest of Champions".

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 8**
*   **In-Game Event:** Return to Athelon. "Hotfix Installing" notification appears (Unusual duration).
*   **Guild Growth:** Justine promotes Mirae to Officer. Mirae recruits 37 "Player+" members (Incapacitated players).
*   **The Awakening (Subtle):** NPCs (Jardis, Mia) display memory and personality. Jardis asks to be remembered.
*   **Crisis:** **Logout Button Disabled.** Customer Support Disabled. Frobod panics about missing his daughter's birthday.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 9**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine and Frobod use "Thunder Bridge" (Freeworld Spell) to travel to Durolan (City of Mages).
*   **Interaction:** Durolan Guards (Dryslyn, Jenda) react with suspicion to "Alien Magics". Justine switches to "Stabbies" alias (Exalted Rep) to defuse situation.
*   **Location:** "Cupcake Keep" (Guild Hall). Run by "Alfred" (Cupcake), a chaotic magical butler.
*   **Feature:** Holographic TV plays real-world news (CNN).
*   **Emotional Moment:** Justine comforts Frobod about being trapped.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 10**
*   **In-Game Event:** "Archangel" appears as a giant hologram in the town square.
*   **The Announcement:** "Logout disabled by design." "Forceful removal = Brain Damage/Death." "Immortality (Digitization) available soon."
*   **Objective:** To logout, players must unlock the feature via Quests.
*   **Reaction:** Frobod focuses on the "Tournament Quest" as the escape key.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 11**
*   **In-Game Event:** Party regroups at Cupcake Keep. Alfred reveals he knows about the "Real World".
*   **Mechanic:** Guild members get "Recall Stones" (Teleport to Keep every 6 hrs).
*   **Social:** TakoStar arrives with a "Cabbit" pet. Mirae loves it.
*   **Revelation:** Mirae argues *for* Digitization (Immortality) vs Frobod's desire to return to his daughter.
*   **Feature:** Guild Hall grows new rooms for new members. Mirae gets a hot tub (Officer perk).

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 12**
*   **Real-World News:** TV confirms ~18,000 trapped players. 6 deaths from forced removal.
*   **Debate:** Frobod calls Mirae selfish for wanting to stay. Mirae reveals the option to digitize is real.
*   **Action:** The group decides to pursue the Tournament Quest to find the "Logout Key".
*   **Travel:** They fly Elechoppers to Dragonswood.
*   **Interaction:** Justine warns the team to treat NPCs ("Havenfolk") with respect due to the Awakening.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 13**
*   **In-Game Event:** The team goes hunting for food (since game food tastes like water).
*   **Action:** Laurelade accidentally burns down part of the forest with "Cinderburst". Justine puts it out with water magic.
*   **Revelation:** Tako cooks a rabbit. It tastes like "Butt Crack". Mirae cooks it properly (using real world knowledge, not game skill). It has flavor.
*   **Lore:** "Data-Bite" technology allows taste, but was unreleased until now.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 14**
*   **In-Game Event:** Arrival at Dragonswood Tournament.
*   **Registration:** Frobod (Joust), Justine (Single Combat), Mirae (Archery), Laurelade (Offensive Magic), Tako (Single Combat).
*   **Gambling:** Justine bets 1000g on Frobod to win the Joust (3:1 odds). Frobod bets 200g on Justine.
*   **Strategy:** Justine clones her "Boom Buggy" speed (600%) to Frobod's mount.
*   **Joust:** Frobod destroys the competition (including "Xephrus", a dragon knight) using the speed hack.
*   **Conflict:** Xephrus accuses him of cheating. Zarentu (Lord of the Keep) verifies the mount is "Unaugmented" (Technically true). Xephrus is forced to kneel.
*   **Reward:** Frobod wins. Justine gets a "Rose Ring of the Champion" (Charisma/Dance buff) and is forced to wear it by the crowd.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 15**
*   **In-Game Event:** Downtime at the festival.
*   **Guild Update:** The 37 new recruits are farming Windroost. Dragon kill count: 3,673/50,000.
*   **Interaction:** Sleeve (The Bookie) tries to run away with the money. Frobod uses his "Champion's Boon" to have Zarentu arrest him.
*   **Meeting Zarentu:** The team meets Lord Zarentu. He is eating 8 whole chickens.
*   **Dialogue:** Justine explains "Freeworld" and "Elechoppers" to Zarentu. He is intrigued but warns them not to be the cause of the "Rip between Worlds".
*   **Lore:** Zarentu opposes the "Extra-Planar Dragon" (The antagonist of the quest).

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 16**
*   **In-Game Event:** Single Combat Tournament.
*   **Justine vs Maadzon:** Justine uses "Shadow Step" and electricity to defeat the heavy tank.
*   **Justine vs Sedid:** Sedid (Blue skinned elf/mage) uses illusions. Justine uses "Flow Like Water" (Dodge) and "Blind" (Throwing dirt) to win.
*   **Justine vs Groth:** Groth (Orc Player) cheats by disarming Justine. Turns into a Mountain Lion (Druid?). Justine uses "Fists of the Thunderlord" (Unarmed Lightning/Sonic dmg) and "Move Like Ice" (Slow/Freeze) to beat him.
*   **Conflict:** Groth curses and tries to attack Justine after the match. Zarentu has him executed by meteor. Groth is permadeath (Status: Dead).
*   **Justine vs Krogen:** Krogen (Dragon Knight) uses fire sword. Justine taunts him ("I think I'll like him when he's angry"). Krogen cheats by turning into a Dragon. Zarentu disqualifies him.
*   **Victory:** Justine wins Single Combat. Frobod wins the Joust.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 17**
*   **In-Game Event:** The party strategizes for the Grand Melee (Group Combat).
*   **Social:** They discuss what food they miss (Pizza, Burritos). Mirae admits she misses "Lychees" (Asian Fruit).
*   **Moment:** Justine hugs Mirae. They bond over missing the real world.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 18**
*   **In-Game Event:** The Grand Melee. Toxic Muffins vs 5 Dragon Knights.
*   **Action:** Justine kites the enemy team ("Form B"). Laurelade uses Blizzard (Ice). Tako uses daggers. Frobod tanks.
*   **Friendly Fire:** Laurelade accidentally electrocutes Justine (Crit fail on control).
*   **Victory:** Toxic Muffins win.
*   **Bonus Round:** Zarentu demands a "Grand Champion" match between Frobod and Justine.
*   **The Throw:** Justine throws the match (Letting Frobod impale her) so he can get the "Special Gift" to help him see his daughter.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 19**
*   **In-Game Event:** Awards Ceremony.
*   **Reward:** Frobod gets a "Draco-Pegasus" (Flying Mount). He is furious (Wanted a Logout Key).
*   **Zarentu's Boon:** Frobod asks to "Go Home". Zarentu tasks Aztram (Wizard) to research "Adventurer Origins" and "The Rip".
*   **Alliance:** Justine negotiates a 1-year alliance between Toxic Muffins and Dragonswood Keep.
*   **Enchantments:** Party members get access to Zarentu's enchanters. Justine commissions "Kicking Boots".

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 20**
*   **In-Game Event:** Return to Durolan.
*   **Discovery:** "Digitization Stations" (Stone Igloos) appear in the city. Protestors warn that people are dying (10-20% failure rate).
*   **Guild Update:** Pohrum (Pehrom's brother) successfully digitized. Earns title "Pioneer".
*   **Kitchen:** Pehrom (Goblin Chef) is cooking real food (Boar Burgers) using real-world physics. It has TASTE.
*   **News:** TV reports 100+ deaths. "Forceful removal" is killing players.
*   **Quest Progress:** "Discover Identity" objective complete (Cimmerian). Kill count: 5,002/50,000.

**Dragonsbane - Chapter 21**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine goes to sleep in the game.
*   **Cliffhanger:** (Implied) The next morning?

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Prologue**
*   **Timeline:** Two weeks later.
*   **Status:** Mirae is confirmed **Dead/Lost**. She failed digitization.
*   **Event:** Mirae's status changes to "Online" with an "Oroboros" icon. Location: "Unknown".
*   **Location:** Digitization Station. Justine meets Magnus (Gnome) who is looking for Jonah (Half-Gnome).
*   **Hope:** Justine and Tako realize Mirae might be stuck in the system, not dead.

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 1**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine meets with Marulan, Rauel, and Aluwyn (Mages) in Durolan.
*   **Item:** "Essence of the Lost Champion" (from Cimmerian's chest) can be used to restore a Lost soul.
*   **Quest:** *The Lost Champion - Part 2* (LE Quest). Objectives: Enter Hades, Find Soul, Feed Essence, Ritual.
*   **Conflict:** Marulan calls it Necromancy. Justine ignores him (-500 Rep).
*   **Mechanic:** Hades is a new zone. Entrances correspond to Rivers (Lethe/Styx). Justine chooses Lethe (Oblivion).

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 2**
*   **Interaction:** Justine receives a message from **Leroy Jensen** (Pro Gamer/Malefactors Guild). He knew Jordan.
*   **Lore:** Jordan's death was a heart attack. Leroy confirms he was a Toxic Muffin.
*   **Meeting:** Leroy visits Cupcake Keep. Meets Pehrom (Chef). Eats "Giant Lambox" steak.
*   **Alliance:** Leroy joins the party for the Hades quest. Offers mentorship in tanking skills.
*   **Recruitment:** Pehrom promotes Pahrem (Goblin) to healer for the group.

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 3**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine visits the Temple of Sune (God of Travel/Lightning).
*   **Quest:** *Thunderlord*. Requirement: Travel to a far land and sacrifice a trophy. Restriction: Weapon > 35 inches.
*   **Item:** "Meditation Stone (Sune)".
*   **Buff:** "Blessed" (+1 Dex, +1 Stellar Navigation).

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 4**
*   **Training:** Leroy teaches Justine "Shield of Harmony" (Magic Resist), "Aegis" (Invulnerability), and "Haven Arcane/Elemental Magic".
*   **Lore:** Mentorship skill requires teaching others.
*   **Party:** Leroy joins as an alt named "Butt-Trumpet" (Hobling) to avoid paparazzi.
*   **Surprise:** Alfred restores the portrait of Jordan in the master bedroom.

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 5**
*   **In-Game Event:** Justine visits Aztram at Dragonswood Keep.
*   **Upgrade:** Aztram enchants her boots -> "Swiftstrike Kickers of the Thunderlord" (Epic). +Light/Holy dmg on kicks. "Static Shield" (Dodge counter -> Lightning dmg).
*   **Advice:** Aztram warns that **Cerberus** guards the gate. Suggests a Bard to put him to sleep.
*   **Recruitment:** Justine recruits **Devarin** (Elf Bard) from the tavern. He demands 1000g.
*   **Quest Sharing:** Justine uses a loophole to share the LE quest with Devarin (via Party).

**LadyThunderlord Into Hades - Chapter 6**
*   **In-Game Event:** The party (Justine, Tako, Frobod, Pahrem, Leroy/Butt-Trumpet, Devarin) travels to the portal.
*   **Travel:** Rauel opens a portal to the Southwest Desert (Real World GPS match).
*   **Transformation:** Devarin switches to his adventure alias "Sajina" (Gargoyle).
*   **Dungeon:** They enter a cave. Fight "Shadowstalkers" (Darkness demons).
*   **Combat:** Sajina charms a Shadowstalker. Pahrem uses Grenades ("G'nades"). Justine uses "Fists of the Thunderlord" (Sonic/Lightning).
*   **Loot:** "Long Claw of the Shadowstalker" (Sword).
*   **Discovery:** A massive underground river/waterfall.
*   **Encounter:** They find a ledge overlooking 200+ Undead (Wights/Zombies).
*   **Strategy:** Sajina uses "The Dead Can Dance" (Bard Spell). Plays "Murder on the Dance Floor".
*   **Action:** The party dances/plays instruments to walk safely through the horde.
*   **Cliffhanger:** They see a spinning portal archway ahead.
