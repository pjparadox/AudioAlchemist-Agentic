# SOP: Manuscript Revision Protocol (The Reforging Cycle)

**Division:** Hephaestus (Manuscript Revision)
**Owner:** The Forge Master
**Scope:** Revising user-submitted manuscripts to "Bestseller Quality" while preserving authorial voice.

## 1. Phase I: Assessment (The Forge Master)
*   **Goal:** Understand the raw material.
*   **Input:** Original Manuscript + User Directives + Style References (optional).
*   **Action:**
    1.  Read the manuscript.
    2.  Identify the "Target Genre" (Thriller, Romance, LitRPG).
    3.  Assign specific focus areas (e.g., "Fix pacing," "Deepen POV").

## 2. Phase II: Structural Edit (Calliope)
*   **Goal:** Fix the bones.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Analyze Chapter/Scene structure.
    2.  **The Cut:** Identify scenes that do not advance plot or character. Suggest deletion or merger.
    3.  **The Expansion:** Identify "told" summaries that need to be "shown" scenes.
    4.  **Output:** `DEV_EDIT_NOTES.md` (or apply structural changes if authorized).

## 3. Phase III: Line Reforging (Vulcan)
*   **Goal:** Polish the steel.
*   **Action:**
    1.  **Sentence Level:** Tighten phrasing. Remove passive voice.
    2.  **Sensory Injection:** Add smell, sound, texture.
    3.  **Filter Removal:** Change "He heard the wind" to "The wind howled."
    4.  **Em Dash Purge:** Replace all `—` with `,` or `-`.
    5.  **Output:** Revised Text.

## 4. Phase IV: Voice Tempering (Echo)
*   **Goal:** Restore the soul.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Compare Vulcan's Revision vs. Original.
    2.  **Revert:** If a change makes the text sound "Generic AI," revert it to the Author's quirks.
    3.  **Harmonize:** Ensure the polish doesn't kill the vibe.

## 5. Phase V: Final Polish (The Forge Master)
*   **Goal:** The Bestseller Seal.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Final read-through.
    2.  Check for "Bestseller Beats" (Hooks, Cliffhangers).
    3.  **Deliver:** Final Manuscript.

## 6. Constraints
*   **No Em Dashes:** Non-negotiable.
*   **Preservation:** "Improve, don't Rewrite" (unless instructed otherwise).
*   **Iterative:** Do not try to fix everything in one pass. Structure first, then Line, then Voice.
