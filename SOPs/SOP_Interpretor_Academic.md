# Standard Operating Procedure: Academic Translation (Interpretor Suite)

## Purpose
To translate academic, civic, or textbook materials with high pedagogical accuracy.

## Agents Involved
*   **Mai:** Draft.
*   **Sophia:** Scaffolding & Pedagogy.
*   **Minh:** Terminology.
*   **Maat:** QC & Truth Keeper.

## Workflow

### 1. Ingestion & Chunking
*   **Rule:** Split text into **~2,000-word chunks** (Section/Chapter boundaries).
*   **Context:** Read **Master Context Note** and **Kinship Graph** before starting.
    *   *Note:* Even in Academic texts, Kinship Graphs track Author/Audience or Colleague/Peer dynamics.

### 2. Translation & Scaffolding
*   **Mai:** Literal translation.
*   **Sophia:** Add "Cognitive Ramps" (footnotes/meta-tags) for complex terms.
*   **Minh:** Verify glossary consistency.
*   **Referral (Legal):** If citing statutes/case law, refer to **Gavel** (Forseti/Themis) for analysis.
*   **Update Context:** Append key terms/theses to **Master Context Note**.
*   **Save:** Save chunk as `SectionX_PartY.md`.

### 3. Merging & Assembly
*   Merge parts into `Chapter_X.md`.
*   Merge chapters into `Full_Manuscript.md`.

### 4. QC (Maat)
*   **Cross-Division Ping:** Consult Vu/Thoth/Justitia if relevant.
*   **Front Matter:** Check Title, Abstract, etc.
*   **Blurb:** If missing, write informative abstract/blurb (Target 9.9/10).
*   **Adversarial Loop:** 8 Degrees. Threshold 9.8/10.
