
**Chapter 20: Resurrection and Reunion**
*   **Aftermath:** Melpomine (resurrected by Leroy) switches to **Scootiwutams** (Dwarf Paladin) to resurrect Frobod.
*   **Loot:** `Mangled Dolly` (Starts **Winter Dungeonland** quest - potential reward: Logout Re-enabled). `Dragon Heartstone` (Quest item). Epic gear distributed.
*   **Quest:** `Winter Dungeonland - Part 1` accepted. Requires returning the dolly to the North Pole in December.

**Chapter 21: Clutching Your Balzac, Gripping Your Longfellow**
*   **Quest:** `Charon's Library`. Leroy uses guildmates in Haven to buy real-world books from the cash shop and burn them, creating **Relics** in Hades.
*   **Reward:** Charon grants the party **Free Passage** forever and pays Leroy 60,000 Hades Marks.
*   **Travel:** Charon ferries them to the Gate of Hades instantly (blur speed).

**Chapter 22: Undying**
*   **Travel:** Melpomine uses `Thunder Bridge` to transport herself, Tako, and Mirae to **Durolan** (Haven).
*   **Ritual:** **Rauel** absorbs the `Book of Death and Rebirth`. The ritual to save Mirae causes a city-wide "Ahhhhh" chant and a vision of a Flying Serpent (Ouroboros).
*   **Transformation:** Mirae becomes **The Undying** (Mummy subrace). Cannot die (goes unconscious), free travel to Hades.
*   **Guild:** **Sajina** joins the **Toxic Muffins**.

**Chapter 23: Lady Thunderlord**
*   **Quest:** Melpomine offers the `Dragon Heartstone` to **Sune**.
*   **Event:** Sune speaks ("Rise my chosen"). A woman's voice interrupts ("J... Sal?"). Sune incinerates Mel (Fatal damage).
*   **Outcome:** Ariel resurrects Mel. Mel gains the title **Thunderlord** (Paladin of Sune).

**Chapter 24: Home Again**
*   **Location:** **Cupcake Keep** is upgraded (Ballistas, Gargoyles) following attacks. Defended by **Pehrom**, **SalemLilly** (Pehrom's wife), and **Justice** guild.
*   **Identities:** Real names revealed: Justine (Mel), Aaron (Frobod), Chris (Tako), Amy (Mirae), Leah (Pahrem), Mandeep (Sajina), Rob (Pehrom).
*   **NPCs:** Annette, Jason, James (rescued humans) demand to be turned into **Rodekin** (Wererats) to prevent being eaten by vampires (toxic blood).

**Chapter 25: The Final Turn In**
*   **Alliance:** Mel brings the humans to **Lord Zarentu** in **Dragonswood Keep**. Zarentu agrees to host/study them.
*   **Ending:** Mel (Lilly alias) infects Annette with the **Nativity Pestilence**. Book ends.
