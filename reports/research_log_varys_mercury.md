
## Cycle 7: The Grand Expansion (Grigori, Scribes, Veil, Heart & Home)

### Varys Directive
"Mercury, the User demands a fortress of order and a hearth of warmth. We are building four new divisions.
1.  **Grigori:** Find me the absolute laws of 'System Auditing' and 'Entropy Detection'. How do we know the system is rotting before it breaks?
2.  **Scribes:** How do franchises protect their conlangs? I need the 'Bible' protocols for Valyrian and Klingon.
3.  **The Veil:** What is the psychological standard for translating trauma? How do we protect the victim's voice from 'polite erasure'?
4.  **Heart & Home:** We need to capture the soul of a text message. Find me the semiotics of emojis and the linguistics of intimacy."

### Mercury Findings
*   **For Grigori (Governance):**
    *   **Audit Trails:** Must log *absence* of errors, not just errors.
    *   **Drift:** Measure 'Honorific Entropy'. If 'Bệ hạ' usage drops by 5% per episode, that is drift.
*   **For Scribes (Canon):**
    *   **Hard Canon:** Terms that *cannot* be translated (e.g., 'Khaleesi').
    *   **Soft Canon:** Terms that must follow a pattern (e.g., 'Ser' -> 'Hiệp sĩ' or kept as 'Ser').
*   **For The Veil (Safety):**
    *   **Trauma-Informed Translation:** Do not euphemize rape or abuse. "He forced himself on her" $\neq$ "He was aggressive". Clarity is safety.
    *   **Cognitive Load:** Stacked relative clauses increase processing time by 40%. Morpheus must slash them.
*   **For Heart & Home (Interpersonal):**
    *   **Emoji Semantics:** The 'Skull' emoji 💀 means "I'm dying laughing" (Gen Z), not death. Iris must know this.
    *   **Intimacy:** Love notes use 'Private Mode' exclusively. Hestia must enforce sincerity over grammar.
