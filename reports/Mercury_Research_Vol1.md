# Mercury Research Report: Volume 1 Assets

## 1. The Time Variance Authority (TVA)
*   **Aesthetic:** Retro-futuristic Bureaucracy.
    *   **Inspirations:** *Blade Runner* meets *Mad Men* meets *Brazil* (Terry Gilliam).
    *   **Colors:** Burnt orange, mustard yellow, wood paneling, CRT monitor greens.
    *   **Smell:** Ozone, old paper, floor wax, sterile air conditioning.
    *   **Sound:** Low hum of servers, clicking of typewriters/keyboards, muffled announcements.
*   **Mr. Paradox:**
    *   **Appearance:** Matthew Macfadyen. Sharp suit (TVA standard but tailored). Slicked back hair.
    *   **Vibe:** "Sexy, heroic" (in his own mind). Smug, helpful on surface, sociopathic underneath.
    *   **Environment:** His office is nicer than the bullpens. Maybe a view of the Time Loom (or similar energy stream).

## 2. Wolverine Variants (The Montage)
*   **Short Wolverine:** Comic accurate height (5'3"). Looks like Hugh Jackman digitally shrunk. Angry.
*   **Patch:** White tuxedo jacket, black pants, eyepatch. Sitting at a gambling table (Madripoor vibe). Smokes cigars.
*   **Cavillrine:** Henry Cavill. Muscular, reloading arm motion (Mission Impossible reference).
*   **Old Man Logan:** Grey hair, hat, shotgun on porch.
*   ** crucified Wolverine:** On the X-cross. Rain, skulls. (Unlikely to speak).
*   **Brown & Tan Suit:** Hulk fight tease.

## 3. The Void
*   **Aesthetic:** Mad Max Wasteland.
*   **Key Elements:**
    *   Ruins of 20th Century Fox logo (buried in sand).
    *   Alioth (purple cloud monster in sky).
    *   Scavengers.
*   **Sensory:** Heat, dust, metallic taste, silence broken by wind.

## 4. The Worst Wolverine
*   **Appearance:** Unshaven, drunk, wearing the yellow/blue suit under a dirty jacket.
*   **Smell:** Whiskey, urine, regret.
*   **Location:** A dive bar in a dying timeline.
