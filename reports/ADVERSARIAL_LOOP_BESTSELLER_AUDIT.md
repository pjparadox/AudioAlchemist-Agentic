## GLOBAL CRITIQUE

The manuscript is competent, but "competent" does not sell. It needs **teeth**. It needs to bleed. The voices are distinct, but they can be sharper. The sensory details are present, but they need to be *visceral* rather than just descriptive.

### CHAPTER 1: BOOT SEQUENCE

*   **Scene 0 (The Void):**
    *   *Critique:* "Gravity slammed into them like a freight train." -> **CLICHÉ.**
    *   *Fix:* Describe the sensation of organs settling, of lungs inflating for the first time. The *violence* of birth.
*   **Scene 1 (Print Bay):**
    *   *Critique:* The System's dialogue is good ("eating chips"), but the Operator feels generic.
    *   *Fix:* Give the Operator more anxiety. He's managing a catastrophe.
*   **Scene 8 (Rook's Landing):**
    *   *Critique:* The burp scene needs to be more than a joke. It needs to be a betrayal of his own biology.
    *   *Fix:* Emphasize the *expectation* of fire and the *reality* of bile.

### CHAPTER 2: DISCOVERY PHASE

*   **Scene 1 (Extraction):**
    *   *Critique:* The description of Sector 4 is standard cyberpunk. "Flickering neon."
    *   *Fix:* Specificity. What does the neon advertise? "Loans for kidneys." "Synth-meat (70% Real)."
*   **Scene 3 (Sunlight):**
    *   *Critique:* Yuki's fear is stated ("I will burn"). Make us feel it.
    *   *Fix:* The heat on her skin should feel like acid. The psychological phantom pain.
*   **Scene 4 (Arby's Setup):**
    *   *Critique:* Rook ordering sandwiches is funny, but let's lean into the *hunger*.
    *   *Fix:* Describe the smell of the roast beef as if it were a fresh kill.

### CHAPTER 3: THE QUEST FOR FIRE

*   **Scene 1 (Caca Fuego):**
    *   *Critique:* The bathroom scene is funny, but is it *Dinniman* funny?
    *   *Fix:* More internal monologue from Rook about the betrayal of the pepper. The "Mother of Dragons" line is good, keep it.
*   **Scene 5 (The Raid):**
    *   *Critique:* "Mei landed silently." "Rook landed like a meteor." Good contrast, but the combat needs more *impact*.
    *   *Fix:* Focus on the physics. The crunch of bone. The strain of muscle. Blackmore style.
    *   *Issue:* "Rook mounted the Elite." -> Phrasing.
    *   *Fix:* "Rook straddled the Elite." Or "Rook pinned the Elite."
*   **Scene 6 (The Pursuit):**
    *   *Critique:* "Sky-sharks." Good line.
    *   *Fix:* Ensure the drone threat feels lethal, not just annoying.
