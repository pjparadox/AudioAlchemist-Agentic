# CHAPTER 13: THE AMBUSH (ULTRA-DENSITY)

**TIMELINE: NULL (THE VOID)**
**LOCATION: THE CANYON**

The ambush didn't start with a sound. It started with a pressure wave.

We were driving through a narrow canyon. The walls were high, jagged cliffs of compressed trash—layers of geological history made of crushed cars, appliances, and broken dreams.

I was driving. The buggy (a Mad Max-style contraption we'd liberated from the Resistance) had no windshield, so the wind was slapping my face with the force of an angry lover.

"We're making good time," I shouted over the engine roar.

"Don't jinx it," Logan grumbled. He was cleaning his claws with a rag that was 90% grease.

Then... the air changed.

*BOOM.*

It wasn't a noise. It was a physical displacement of reality.

An explosion rocked the front axle.

The buggy didn't just stop; it pole-vaulted. The front end kicked up, and suddenly we were airborne.

*Time slows down.*

I saw the ground rushing up.
I saw Logan's cigar fly out of his mouth in slow motion, a tiny comet of embers spiraling into the dark.
I saw the sky spin. Purple. Grey. Purple. Grey.

*CRASH.*

We landed upside down.

The roll cage screamed as it ground against the rock. Sparks showered us like a celebratory fountain of pain.

I crawled out of the wreckage.

My left leg was bent at a 90-degree angle. The shin bone was sticking out through my suit, white and wet against the dirty red fabric.

"Motherfluffer!" I screamed.

I grabbed my ankle. I gritted my teeth.

*SNAP.*

I reset the bone. The pain was blinding. White hot. It tasted like biting on aluminum foil.

"Wade!" Logan yelled.

I looked up.

Logan was pinned. The chassis of the buggy—heavy steel, hot engine block—was resting on his chest. He was pushing up, his biceps bulging, veins popping like ropes on his forehead.

"Get... it... off!" he wheezed.

And then I saw him.

Standing on the ridge above us.

Sabretooth.

Not the Liev Schreiber one. He was too nuanced. This was the Tyler Mane one. The big one. The one who looked like a lion that had eaten a wrestler and then done a cycle of steroids.

He jumped down.

*THUD.*

The ground shook. He landed in a crouch, dust billowing around him. He stood up slowly, unfolding like a nightmare.

"Well, well," Sabretooth growled. His voice was a tectonic rumble. "The runt."

"Victor," Logan gasped.

"I've been waiting for this," Sabretooth said. "A long time."

He extended his claws. They weren't Adamantium. They were bone. Thick, yellow, serrated bone. They looked infected. They looked like they would give you tetanus just by looking at them.

I reached for my katanas.

"Hey, whiskers!" I yelled. "Pick on someone your own... well, not size, because you're huge. Pick on someone your own species!"

I lunged.

*FWOOSH.*

A wall of fire erupted between me and Sabretooth.

I stumbled back, shielding my face. The heat was intense. It singed my eyebrows instantly. It smelled of burnt hair and sulfur.

I looked up at the ridge.

Pyro.

He stood there, manipulating the flame like a conductor. He smirked.

"Stay back, freak," Pyro yelled.

I was cut off. I could only watch.

Sabretooth walked toward Logan. He took his time. He savored it.

"You look tired, Jimmy," Sabretooth grinned. His teeth were yellow points.

Logan strained against the car. "Victor... don't."

"Do you remember the last time we fought?" Sabretooth asked. "In the snow? You were weak then, too."

He raised a massive hand. The claws glinted in the firelight.

"Time to die, runt."

*Beat 1: Mechanics.*
Logan stopped pushing the car. He let the weight settle on his chest.
He took a breath.
Then... he exploded.
He didn't lift the car. He popped his claws *through* the metal door of the buggy.
*SNIKT.*
The Adamantium blades punched through the steel, through the window frame, and drove upward into Sabretooth's foot.
Sabretooth howled. It was a sound of pure animal shock.
He stepped back, hopping on one foot, blood spraying from his instep.
Logan used the distraction. He bench-pressed the chassis. *HNNNNG.* With a roar that tore his throat raw, he threw the buggy off him.
It rolled away with a metallic crash.
Logan stood up.
He was bleeding from his chest. His suit was torn. He was covered in oil and dust.
But he stood tall.

*Beat 2: Consequence.*
They collided.
It wasn't a martial arts fight. It wasn't a choreographed dance. It was two apex predators trying to unmake each other.
Claws met claws. *SPARK.*
Sabretooth swiped. His massive hand connected with Logan's shoulder.
*TEAR.*
He ripped a chunk of flesh out. Muscle, skin, suit—gone.
Logan didn't scream. He grunted. He drove his knee into Sabretooth's groin.
Sabretooth doubled over.
Logan stabbed him in the gut. Three claws deep.
They fell to the ground. Rolling. Biting. Snarling. A ball of fur and hate.

*Beat 3: Aftertaste.*
Logan got the upper hand.
He pinned Sabretooth to the ground. His claws were at Victor's throat.
Sabretooth laughed. Blood bubbled in his mouth.
"Do it!" Sabretooth hissed. "Do it, runt! You're a killer! Be a killer!"
Logan hesitated. His hand shook.
I saw it in his eyes. The memory. The brotherhood. The centuries of war.
"Do it!" Sabretooth screamed.
Logan's eyes went blank. The animal took over.
*SNICK-SLICE.*
He scissored his claws.
Sabretooth's head separated from his body.
It rolled.
It rolled across the dirt, leaving a trail of dark red. It stopped at my feet.
Sabretooth's eyes were still open. Frozen in a look of surprise.
The body twitched once. Then... still.

Logan stood up. He was panting. Heaving. Blood—his and Victor's—covered him like a second skin.
He looked wild. He looked terrifying.
He looked at me.
"Logan?" I whispered.
He blinked. The yellow faded from his eyes. The man came back.
He looked at the headless corpse.
He fell to his knees.
"Let's go," he whispered. "Before I eat him."
