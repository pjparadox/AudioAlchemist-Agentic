# CHAPTER 20: THE UNZIPPING

**LOCATION: THE TIME RIPPER CHAMBER**

The sound was the first thing.

It wasn't a mechanical noise. It wasn't gears grinding or turbines spinning.

It was the sound of screaming.

But not human screaming. It was the sound of *reality* screaming. It was the sound of atoms being torn apart, of history being ripped from the textbook pages.

*SKREEEEEEEEEEEEE.*

I fell to my knees. My ears bled. Not metaphorically. Warm, wet blood trickled down my neck.

"Wade!" Logan yelled.

He was fighting Cassandra. She was floating in the center of the room, her hands deep in the machine. She looked ecstatic. She looked like a conductor leading an orchestra of apocalypse.

"It's beautiful!" Cassandra laughed. "Can you feel it? The silence coming?"

The room was distorting. The walls were stretching like taffy. Colors were bleeding into each other. Orange became purple. Up became sideways.

*Sensory Overload:*
*   **Sight:** The air rippled like heat haze on asphalt. I saw my own hand age, wither to bone, then regenerate, then turn into a baby hand.
*   **Smell:** Burning film stock. Celluloid on fire. The chemical tang of developer fluid.
*   **Taste:** Ash. I tasted the ash of a billion lives.

"Stop it!" I yelled.

I charged.

Cassandra flicked a finger.

I flew backward. I hit a console. *CRACK.* My spine snapped.

"You can't stop the ending," Cassandra said. "The credits are rolling."

She plunged her hands deeper. The beam intensified.

The screen on the wall showed my world.

I saw Blind Al. She was fading. Turning into static.
I saw Peter. He was holding Muffin. He looked confused. Then he was gone.
I saw Vanessa.

She was walking down the street. She looked up. She saw the sky turning purple.

She didn't run. She didn't scream. She just looked... sad.

*No.*

"LOGAN!" I screamed.

Logan looked at the screen. He saw it too.

He saw the X-Men. Not his X-Men. *My* X-Men. Colossus. Negasonic. Yukio.

They were holding hands. Bracing for the end.

Logan roared.

It wasn't a battle cry. It was a sound of pure, distilled grief.

He launched himself at Cassandra.

She tried to push him back. But he was heavy. He was the Anchor. He was density incarnate.

He slammed into her.

They crashed into the energy stream.

*FZZEEEEEEEE.*

The room exploded in light.

# CHAPTER 21: THE CHOICE

I was floating.

White space. No floor. No ceiling.

Paradox stood there.

"Mr. Wilson," he said. He looked calm. Clean.

"Am I dead?" I asked. "Is this the lobby? Do you have magazines?"

"You are at the decision point," Paradox said. "The fulcrum."

He gestured.

Two doors appeared.

Door A: A golden door. Light spilled from it.
Door B: A wooden door. Rotting.

"Behind Door A," Paradox said, "is the Sacred Timeline. You join the Avengers. You become a legend. You matter. But your world dies. Your friends die."

"And Door B?"

"Door B is the hard way," Paradox said. "You go back. You fight. You probably die. And even if you win... you're just Wade Wilson. A clown in a red suit."

I looked at Door A.

I saw it. I saw me shaking hands with Thor. I saw me on a lunchbox. I saw me being *happy*.

I looked at Door B.

I saw the pain. The mess. The struggle.

I saw Vanessa.

"It's not a choice," I said.

"Of course it is," Paradox argued. "It's the only choice that matters. Self or Sacrifice."

"No," I said. "It's not a choice because I've already made it."

I walked to Door B.

"Hey, Paradox?"

"Yes?"

"Tell Thor to save his tears. I don't need them."

I kicked the door open.

***

**REALITY**

I woke up screaming.

I was in the chamber. The energy was tearing me apart. My skin was peeling off in strips. My muscles were cooking.

Logan was next to me. He was burning too. His skin was gone. He was a skeleton dipped in lava.

"WADE!" he screamed.

We were holding the cables. We were the circuit. We were grounding the power.

"Let go!" Cassandra shrieked. "You'll die!"

"That's the plan!" I yelled.

I looked at Logan.

He looked at me.

His eyes—the only part of him that wasn't burning—were clear.

"Maximum Effort?" he asked.

It was my line. He stole my line.

I laughed. My lips cracked and fell off.

"Maximum Effort."

We squeezed the cables.

*BOOM.*

The world went white.

And then... silence.
