# CHAPTER 20: THE UNZIPPING (ULTRA-DENSITY)

**TIMELINE: NULL (TIME RIPPER CHAMBER)**
**LOCATION: THE CORE**

The room didn't have geometry. It had suggestions.

The walls were white, but they were also vibrating. They were stretching like taffy, pulling apart to reveal the wireframe of the universe underneath.

The sound was the first thing that hit me.

*SKREEEEEEEEEEEEE.*

It wasn't mechanical. It wasn't the hum of a server farm.

It was the sound of screaming.

But not human screaming. It was the sound of *concepts* screaming. It was the sound of the year 2024 begging for mercy. It was the sound of history being scraped off the page with a razor blade.

I fell to my knees. My inner ear revolted. Up was down. Left was yesterday.

"Wade!" Logan yelled.

His voice was distorted. Slowed down. *Waaaaaaade.*

He was fighting.

Cassandra Nova was floating in the center of the room. Her hands were plunged deep into the energy stream of the Time Ripper. She looked ecstatic. She looked like a conductor leading a symphony of apocalypse.

"It's beautiful!" Cassandra laughed. Her voice echoed from everywhere at once. "Can you feel it? The silence coming? The peace of the blank page?"

I tried to stand. The floor rippled like water.

*Sensory Overload:*
*   **Sight:** Colors were bleeding. Orange became purple. Red became a sound. I saw my own hand age, wither to bone, then regenerate into a baby hand, then back to a scarred fist. *Time dilation.*
*   **Smell:** Burning film stock. Celluloid on fire. The chemical tang of developer fluid mixed with the copper of fresh blood.
*   **Taste:** Ash. I tasted the ash of a billion lives. It coated my tongue. Dry. Gritty. The taste of everyone I had ever loved turning into dust.

"Stop it!" I yelled.

I charged.

I tried to run, but the air was thick. Viscous. It was like running through clear jelly.

Cassandra didn't even look at me. She flicked a finger.

*THWACK.*

I flew backward. I hit a console. *CRACK.* My spine snapped.

"You can't stop the ending," Cassandra said. "The credits are rolling. The lights are coming up."

She plunged her hands deeper. The beam intensified.

A screen on the wall—the only thing that seemed solid—showed my world. Earth-10005.

I saw the apartment.
I saw Blind Al. She was knitting. The yarn turned to static. Then her hands. Then her face. She didn't scream. She just... ceased.
I saw Peter. He was holding Muffin. He looked confused. He looked at the camera.
"Mr. Wilson?"
Then he was gone.

And then... Vanessa.

She was walking down the street. She looked up. She saw the sky turning purple.

She didn't run. She didn't panic. She just looked... sad. She looked like she knew.

*No.*

The word ripped out of my throat. It wasn't a quip. It wasn't a joke. It was a plea.

"LOGAN!" I screamed.

Logan looked at the screen.

He saw it too.

He saw the X-Men. Not his X-Men. *My* X-Men. Colossus. Negasonic. Yukio.

They were standing in the Danger Room. They were holding hands. Bracing for the wave.

Logan roared.

It wasn't a battle cry. It wasn't the animal rage.

It was a sound of pure, distilled grief. It was the sound of a man who refused to bury another family.

He launched himself.

He didn't run. He *flew*. He used the unstable gravity of the room to propel himself.

He hit Cassandra.

She tried to push him back with her mind. But he was heavy. He was the Anchor. He was density incarnate. He was the weight of a hundred years of pain.

He slammed into her.

They crashed into the energy stream.

*FZZEEEEEEEE.*

The room exploded in light.

***

**THE VISION**

I was floating.

White space. No floor. No ceiling. Just infinite, sterile white.

Paradox stood there.

"Mr. Wilson," he said. He looked calm. Clean. His nose wasn't broken.

"Am I dead?" I asked. "Is this the lobby? Do you have magazines? Is *Highlights* still in print?"

"You are at the decision point," Paradox said. "The fulcrum."

He gestured.

Two doors appeared.

**Door A:** A golden door. Light spilled from it. I could hear applause. I could hear the Avengers theme.
**Door B:** A wooden door. Rotting. Splintered. It smelled of smoke.

"Behind Door A," Paradox said, "is the Sacred Timeline. You join the Avengers. You become a legend. You matter. You get the toy deal. You get the respect."

"And my world?"

"Gone. Pruned. A necessary sacrifice for your ascension."

"And Door B?"

"Door B is the hard way," Paradox said. "You go back. You fight. You probably die. And even if you win... you're just Wade Wilson. A clown in a red suit. A footnote."

I looked at Door A.

I saw it. I saw me shaking hands with Thor. I saw me on a lunchbox. I saw me being *happy*. I saw validation.

I looked at Door B.

I saw the pain. The mess. The struggle. I saw the rent being late. I saw the scars.

I saw Vanessa.

"It's not a choice," I said.

"Of course it is," Paradox argued. "It's the only choice that matters. Self or Sacrifice. Hero or Hack."

"No," I said. "It's not a choice because I've already made it."

I walked to Door B.

"Hey, Paradox?"

"Yes?"

"Tell Thor to save his tears. I don't need them. I prefer the laughter."

I kicked the door open.

***

**REALITY**

I woke up screaming.

I was in the chamber. The energy was tearing me apart.

My skin was peeling off in strips. My muscles were cooking. I could smell my own meat charring.

Logan was next to me.

He was burning too. His skin was gone. He was a skeleton dipped in lava. The Adamantium glowed cherry-red.

"WADE!" he screamed.

We were holding the cables. We were the circuit. We were grounding the power.

The pain was beyond pain. It was absolute. It was a white noise that drowned out every thought.

"Let go!" Cassandra shrieked. She was trapped in the beam, disintegrating. "You'll die! You'll both die!"

"That's the plan!" I yelled. My jaw was melting. "The plan is... *stupid*!"

I looked at Logan.

He looked at me.

His eyes—the only part of him that wasn't burning—were clear. They weren't yellow. They were blue.

He reached out. He grabbed my hand.

The connection snapped into place. Two anchors. One circuit.

"Maximum Effort?" he asked.

It was my line. He stole my line. The magnificent bastard.

I laughed. My lips cracked and fell off.

"Maximum Effort."

We squeezed the cables.

*BOOM.*

The world went white.

And then... silence.
