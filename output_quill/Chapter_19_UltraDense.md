# CHAPTER 19: THE POLITICS OF ETERNITY (ULTRA-DENSITY)

**TIMELINE: EARTH-616 (TVA HEADQUARTERS)**
**LOCATION: THE WAR ROOM**
**POV: HUNTER B-15**

*Side Story G: The Soft Mutiny*

The War Room didn't smell like war. It smelled like toner and stale air conditioning.

Hunter B-15 stood by the monitor wall. The orange light of the Sacred Timeline reflected in her eyes, but today, the light was wrong.

It flickered.

It wasn't a glitch. It was a pulse. A heartbeat. And it was irregular.

"Report," B-15 said. Her voice was calm, but her hand was gripping her baton so hard the leather grip creaked.

"Sector 4 is fluctuating," Analyst Kevin said. He was sweating. Kevin never sweated. He was a man made of anxiety and spreadsheets, but today, he looked like he was watching a car crash in slow motion.

"Define 'fluctuating'," B-15 ordered.

"The energy draw is... it's unprecedented, Ma'am. The Time Ripper is pulling power directly from the Loom. It's not just pruning branches. It's... it's eating the trunk."

B-15 looked at the screen.

The timeline wasn't just branching. It was fraying. The golden threads were turning grey. They were withering.

"Where is Paradox?"

"He's at the Void Outpost. He's locked the comms."

B-15 turned away from the screen. She looked at the room.

Dozens of analysts. Minutemen. Hunters.

They were all looking at her.

They knew.

In the TVA, rumors traveled faster than light. They knew about the rogue agent. They knew about the Anchor Being. They knew that Paradox had gone off-script.

"He says it's a 'radical pruning'," Kevin whispered. "He says the timeline is sick. He says mercy is inefficient."

B-15 felt a cold anger settling in her chest. It was a familiar feeling. It was the same feeling she had when she learned she was a Variant. When she learned her life was a lie.

"He's not pruning," B-15 said. Her voice carried across the silent room. "He's murdering. He's killing a universe because he doesn't like the ending. He's playing god with a cheat code."

She walked to the center of the room.

"Paradox thinks the TVA is a machine," she said. "He thinks we are cogs. He thinks we just follow orders."

She looked at a Minuteman by the door.

"Are you a cog, Miller?"

Miller straightened. "No, Ma'am."

"Are you a machine, Kevin?"

Kevin adjusted his glasses. "I... I prefer 'biological data processor', Ma'am."

A ripple of laughter. Nervous, but real.

"Paradox is trying to erase a timeline that is fighting to survive," B-15 said. "He is trying to erase a hero who gave everything. And he is doing it in our name."

She drew her baton.

*ZZZ-THWACK.*

It extended. The purple light hummed.

"I don't know about you," B-15 said. "But I'm tired of erasing things. I'm tired of being the bad guy."

She looked at the screen. The rot was spreading. The grey was encroaching on the gold.

"We have a job to do. Not for the Time Keepers. Not for the Sacred Timeline. For us. For the people we used to be."

She tapped her TemPad.

"Open a door," she ordered.

"To where?" Kevin asked.

"To the Void," B-15 said. "We're going to crash a party."

The room moved.

It wasn't a shout. It was a shift in gravity. Analysts typed faster. Minutemen checked their weapons.

The Soft Mutiny had begun.

It wasn't loud. It wasn't flashy. It was the sound of a thousand people deciding, all at once, to do the right thing.

"Kevin," B-15 said.

"Yes, Ma'am?"

"Bring the heavy ordinance. Paradox likes big guns. Let's show him ours."

**TIMELINE: NULL (THE VOID SECTOR)**
**LOCATION: THE APPROACH**

**POV: WADE WILSON**

We were running.

My lungs burned. My legs burned. My soul burned (but that might have been the chimichanga).

The outpost loomed ahead of us. A white blister on the black glass ground.

The beam of the Time Ripper was getting brighter. It was so bright it had a sound. A high-pitched *keening* that drilled into my skull.

"They're speeding it up!" Logan yelled.

He was running on all fours. Like an animal. It was terrifying. It was efficient.

"We need a distraction!" I shouted.

"We *are* the distraction!"

"We need a bigger distraction! We need a musical number! We need a cameo!"

*BOOM.*

The ground shook.

A portal opened above the outpost.

Not an orange portal. A purple one.

"Is that..." I squinted. "Is that Alioth?"

It was.

But Alioth wasn't attacking us. He was attacking the beam.

The cloud beast was wrapping itself around the pillar of light. It was feeding.

"The dog is eating the laser pointer!" I yelled. "Good boy!"

"It won't hold!" Logan roared. "We have to get inside!"

We hit the door.

It was blast-proof. Adamantium-reinforced. Locked.

"Open sesame!" I yelled, kicking it.

Nothing.

Logan stepped up.

"Allow me," he growled.

He didn't punch it. He didn't slash it.

He grabbed the edges of the frame. He dug his claws into the wall *around* the door.

*SCREEEEEEE.*

He pulled.

Muscles bunched. Veins popped. He roared—a sound that was half rage, half hernia.

The wall tore. The metal shrieked.

He ripped the entire doorframe out of the building.

He threw it behind him.

"After you," he said.

"Show off," I muttered.

We ran inside.

Into the belly of the beast. Into the machine that was killing my world.
