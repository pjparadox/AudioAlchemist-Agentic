# CHAPTER 5: OPENING

The courtroom was packed. Every seat in the gallery was filled. Reporters, curious locals, theology students, goth kids hoping for a show. It was a circus.
Judge Brewster banged his gavel.
"All rise."
The bailiff called the court to order.
Erin stood. She smoothed her skirt. She checked her notes one last time.
*Reasonable Doubt. Institutional Failure. Human Limits.*

"Mr. Thomas," Judge Brewster said. "Your opening."

Ethan Thomas walked to the jury box. He looked like a statue of Justice come to life. Solid. Dependable.
"Ladies and gentlemen," he began. His voice was a deep baritone that filled the room without shouting. "This is a simple case. It is a tragic case, but it is simple."
He held up a bottle of pills.
"This is Gambrolic. It is a medication used to treat epilepsy and psychosis. It controls seizures. It calms the mind."
He set the bottle down on the railing. *Clack.*
"Emily Rose had a medical condition. A serious, treatable condition. She went to doctors. She got a prescription. She was getting better."
He walked to the defense table and pointed a finger at Father Moore.
"Then she met this man."
The jury looked at Moore. Moore stared straight ahead.
"Father Richard Moore told her to stop taking the pills. He told her that her seizures were not a disease, but a gift. A curse. A possession."
Thomas paused.
"He told a sick girl to stop eating. He tied her to a bed. He watched her waste away until she weighed sixty-eight pounds. And when she died... he prayed."
Thomas looked at the jury, his eyes burning with righteous anger.
"We cannot allow superstition to override the law. We cannot allow negligence to masquerade as faith. We ask you to find the defendant guilty of negligent homicide."

Thomas sat down.
The jury looked shaken. It was a powerful narrative.
Erin stood up.
She didn't walk to the jury box. She walked to the witness stand. She touched the wood of the chair.
"Facts," she said. Her voice was cool, clear water. "Mr. Thomas has given you facts. Emily was sick. She stopped her meds. She died."
She turned to the jury.
"But facts are like bricks. You can build a wall with them. Or you can build a bridge. Mr. Thomas is building a wall. He wants to wall off the things we don't understand. He wants to tell you that everything in this world can be explained by a pill."
She walked to the center of the room.
"But we all know... in the quiet of the night... that isn't true."
She saw Juror 11 (The Nurse) nod slightly.
"We will show you that Emily Rose was not a victim of negligence. She was a victim of something science could not name. Something medicine could not cure. Father Moore didn't tell her to stop eating. *Something else* stopped her."
She looked at Moore.
"We will ask you to open your minds. To look past the wall of facts. To see the possibility... that Father Moore was the only one who truly tried to save her."
