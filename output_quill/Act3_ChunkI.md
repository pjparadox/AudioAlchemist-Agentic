# CHAPTER 22: EPILOGUE

**LOCATION: NEW YORK CITY (EARTH-10005)**
**TIME: THREE DAYS LATER**

The shawarma tasted like victory.

And garlic. Mostly garlic.

We sat on the curb. Me. Logan. Laura.

It was raining, but it was a nice rain. A cleansing rain.

Logan was eating a gyro with a ferocity that was frankly alarming. He ate like a man who had forgotten what food tasted like.

"Slow down, peanut," I said. "You'll get indigestion. And I don't want to be around for *that* biological event."

Logan grunted. He wiped sauce from his beard.

"It's good," he said.

"It's the best," I agreed.

I looked at Laura. She was wearing sunglasses. She looked cool.

"So," I said. "What now?"

"Now we live," Laura said.

"Living is hard," I noted. "Dying is easy. Comedy is hard. But living? Living is just... laundry and taxes."

"And family," Logan said.

He didn't look at me. He looked at the street. But I heard it.

*Family.*

"Yeah," I said. "And family."

Blind Al opened the window above us.

"Keep it down!" she yelled. "I'm listening to a podcast about serial killers! It's soothing!"

"Love you too, Al!" I shouted back.

I looked at my watch. Hello Kitty was still ticking.

I had a date. Vanessa was waiting.

"I gotta go," I said. "Don't wait up. Don't touch my stuff. Don't prune the plants."

I stood up.

"Wade," Logan said.

I turned.

"See you around, Bub."

It wasn't a threat. It was a promise.

I smiled.

"See you around, peanut."

I walked away.

The city was loud. Sirens. Honking. People yelling.

It was chaotic. It was messy. It was beautiful.

It was my world.

And it was still here.

*Fade to black.*

*Cue music.*

*Madonna. 'Like a Prayer'.*

*Because obviously.*

**THE END.**
