# CHAPTER 22: EPILOGUE (ULTRA-DENSITY)

**TIMELINE: EARTH-10005 (THREE DAYS LATER)**
**LOCATION: NEW YORK CITY**

The shawarma tasted like victory.

And garlic. Mostly garlic. It was greasy, salty, and perfect. It tasted like life.

We sat on the curb. Me. Logan. Laura.

It was raining, but it wasn't the miserable grey rain of the dealership window. It was a summer rain. Warm. Cleansing. It washed the soot of the Void off the pavement and out of our souls.

Logan was eating a gyro with a ferocity that was frankly alarming. He ate like a man who had forgotten what food tasted like. He wiped sauce from his beard with the back of his hand.

"Slow down, peanut," I said. "You'll get indigestion. And I don't want to be around for *that* biological event. My nose is sensitive. It's new."

Logan grunted. He swallowed a bite that was clearly too big.

"It's good," he said.

"It's the best," I agreed.

I looked at Laura. She was wearing sunglasses. She looked cool. Cooler than I would ever be. She was eating a falafel with a quiet dignity.

"So," I said. "What now?"

The question hung in the air, mixing with the smell of wet asphalt.

"Now we live," Laura said.

"Living is hard," I noted. "Dying is easy. Comedy is hard. But living? Living is just... laundry and taxes. And dentist appointments."

"And family," Logan said.

He didn't look at me. He looked at the street. At the people walking by. At a kid chasing a paper boat in the gutter.

*Family.*

"Yeah," I said. "And family."

Blind Al opened the window above us.

"Keep it down!" she yelled. "I'm listening to a podcast about serial killers! It's soothing! I need to know how they disposed of the bodies!"

"Love you too, Al!" I shouted back.

I looked at my watch. Hello Kitty was still ticking.

*Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock.*

It wasn't a countdown anymore. It was just time. Time we had earned.

I had a date. Vanessa was waiting.

"I gotta go," I said. "Don't wait up. Don't touch my stuff. Don't prune the plants."

I stood up. My knees popped. A reminder of the miles.

"Wade," Logan said.

I turned.

He looked up at me. His eyes were clear. The yellow was gone.

"See you around, Bub."

It wasn't a threat. It wasn't a dismissal. It was a promise.

I smiled.

"See you around, peanut."

I walked away.

The city was loud. Sirens. Honking. People yelling at each other in three different languages.

It was chaotic. It was messy. It was beautiful.

It was my world.

And it was still here.

*Fade to black.*

*Cue music.*

*Madonna. 'Like a Prayer'.*

*Because obviously.*

**THE END.**
