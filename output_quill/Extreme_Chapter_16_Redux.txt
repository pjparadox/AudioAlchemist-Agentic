**[INTERSTITIAL NARRATIVE]**

**DATE:** Current Timeline (The Trial).
**LOCATION:** Erin Bruner’s Condo.
**TIME:** 2:14 AM.

Erin sat on the floor of her living room. She was surrounded by islands of paper.
Transcripts. Medical records. Police reports.
She had a glass of wine in one hand and a highlighter in the other.
The only light came from the city outside and a small desk lamp that cast long, sharp shadows across the room.
She was looking for a crack.
A crack in the wall of "Medical Certainty" that Dr. Vogel had built.

She picked up the prescription records.
Exhibit 14.
She had looked at them a hundred times.
*Gambrolic. 200mg. 400mg. 600mg.*
A standard escalation. Titration.
But tonight... tonight something looked wrong.
She held the paper up to the light.
The handwriting.
Dr. Vogel's handwriting was precise. Block letters. Clinical.
But on the prescription for November 15th... the handwriting changed.
Just slightly.
The loop on the 'G' in Gambrolic was wider. The pressure on the pen was heavier. The ink was darker.
Erin frowned.
She pulled out the nursing logs from the same day.
*11/15/76. 14:00. Dr. Vogel visits patient. Patient restraints tightened.*
*11/15/76. 14:30. Dr. Vogel leaves.*
*11/15/76. 14:45. Patient screaming. Claims Dr. Vogel "fed her to the wolves."*

Erin looked back at the prescription.
She picked up a magnifying glass.
She looked at the signature.
*H. Vogel, MD.*
There was a tremor in the signature.
A small, jagged spike in the 'V'.
Erin traced it.
It looked like... a heartbeat. An EKG spike.
Or a flinch.
Why would he flinch?
He was writing a prescription for an epileptic girl. It was routine.
Unless he was afraid.
Unless he saw something in that room on November 15th that scared him so bad his hand shook.

Erin grabbed her notebook.
She started writing.
*Timeline:*
*Nov 15: Vogel doubles the dose.*
*Nov 15: Vogel's hand shakes.*
*Nov 15: Emily claims he "fed her to the wolves."*
*Hypothesis:* Vogel didn't increase the dose to help her. He increased it to *shut her up*.
He was scared.
He saw something.
What did he see?

Erin looked at the empty chair in the corner of her room.
The Eames chair.
It was empty.
But she felt the cold draft again. The smell of copper.
"What did you show him, Emily?" she whispered.
The air in the room seemed to thicken.
Erin looked back at the paper.
The ink on the prescription...
It looked wet.
She touched it.
It smeared.
Black ink on her finger.
Like soot.
Erin stared at her finger.
The record was six months old. The ink should be dry.
She smelled her finger.
Sulfur.
She dropped the paper.
It fluttered to the floor.
It landed face up.
And for a second, the handwriting didn't say *Gambrolic*.
It said *POISON*.
Erin blinked.
The word was gone. It said *Gambrolic* again.
She scrambled back, hitting the sofa.
"Okay," she breathed. "Okay. That's enough for tonight."
She stood up. She grabbed the wine bottle.
She walked to the bedroom and locked the door.
She left the papers on the floor.
But as she lay in bed, staring at the ceiling, she knew.
She had found the crack.
Vogel wasn't a doctor treating a patient.
He was a man trying to bury a secret.
And the secret was waking up.
