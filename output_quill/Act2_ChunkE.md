# CHAPTER 11: THE GAUNTLET - PART 1 (THE SETTLEMENT)

**LOCATION: THE SCAVENGER CAMP**

The settlement was built inside the ribcage of a Giant-Man variant.

It was impressive, in a grim way. Huts made of scrap metal and old billboards clung to the massive white bones like barnacles. People—mutants, humans, aliens—moved through the mud, trading scraps of food and water.

"We need supplies," Logan said. He was limping. The crash had done something to his hip that even his healing factor was struggling with.

"We need a new car," I added. "The Odyssey is... well, it's an Odyssey. It's not an Iliad."

We walked into the camp.

Heads turned. Eyes watched us from the shadows. Hungry eyes.

"Keep your head down," Logan muttered.

"Hard to do when you're wearing red spandex," I noted. "I'm basically a walking stop sign."

We found a trader. A Toad variant. He was sitting on a crate, eating a fly.

"We need fuel," Logan said. "And water."

Toad looked us up and down. He licked his lips. His tongue was uncomfortably long.

"What do you have to trade?" Toad asked.

I patted my pockets. "I have a half-eaten chimichanga. A picture of Bea Arthur. And... oh, look! A bullet!"

Toad sneered. "Not enough."

"What do you want?" Logan asked.

Toad pointed.

At the edge of the camp, there was a cage. Inside was a child. A mutant kid. Glowing blue eyes.

"The boss wants the kid," Toad said. "He's an energy source. You bring me the kid, I give you fuel."

I looked at the kid. He looked terrified.

I looked at Logan.

Logan's face was stone.

"No," Logan said.

"Then no fuel," Toad shrugged.

We walked away.

"We take the fuel," Logan whispered.

"Steal it?"

"Take it."

"And the kid?" I asked.

Logan stopped. He looked at the cage. He looked at his hands—hands that had killed thousands.

"We're not heroes, Wade," Logan said. "We're survivors."

"We can be both," I said.

That night, we didn't just take the fuel.

We took the camp apart.

*Beat 1: Mechanics.*
Logan moved like a shadow. He snapped the neck of the guard by the fuel depot. *CRACK.* Clean. Efficient.
I went for the cage. I sliced the lock. *CLANG.*
The kid looked up. "Are you here to save me?"
"I'm here to make a bad decision," I said. "Come on."

*Beat 2: Consequence.*
The alarm went off.
Suddenly, the camp was awake. Mutants poured out of the huts. Pyro. Sabretooth. A guy with quills.
We fought back to back.
Logan was a whirlwind. Claws flashing. Blood spraying.
I was... less graceful. I was shooting and quip-ing.
"Sorry! Excuse me! Just passing through! Love what you've done with the place!"

*Beat 3: Aftertaste.*
We got the fuel. We got the kid to the edge of the camp.
"Run," Logan told the boy.
The boy ran into the dark.
We jumped into a stolen dune buggy. We sped away.
I looked back. The camp was burning.
"We did a good thing," I said.
Logan wiped blood from his claws.
"We made enemies," he said. "That's not the same thing."

# CHAPTER 12: THE GAUNTLET - PART 2 (THE RUINS)

**LOCATION: THE FOX LOT**

We drove for hours.

The landscape changed. The sand gave way to concrete. Broken slabs. Rebar.

We stopped at a ruin.

It was a statue. Or part of one. A giant torch.

"Lady Liberty?" Logan asked.

"Planet of the Apes," I corrected. "Or... wait."

I wiped the dust off a plaque.

*20th CENTURY FOX.*

It was the logo. The monolith. The fanfare structure.

I sat down on the letter 'X'.

"This is it," I said. "This is where I came from."

Logan looked at the ruins. "It's just rocks."

"It's not just rocks!" I shouted. "It's history! It's *X-Men*. It's *Daredevil*. It's *Elektra*! Okay, maybe not *Elektra*. But it's my home!"

I felt a lump in my throat.

*It’s gone. All of it. The studio. The universe. The stories.*

"Everything ends," Logan said. He sat down next to me. He didn't look at me. He looked at the horizon.

"Does it have to?" I asked. "Why does it have to end? Why can't we just... keep going?"

"Because nothing lasts," Logan said. "Not even us."

He pulled out a cigar. He lit it. The smoke smelled of comfort.

"I miss them," I whispered. "Vanessa. Blind Al. Even Weasel. I miss my world."

"Then fight for it," Logan said.

"I am," I said. "But I'm tired, Wolvie. I'm so tired."

He looked at me. He put a hand on my shoulder. It was heavy. Warm.

"Me too, kid," he said. "Me too."

We sat there for a long time, watching the sun set over the ruins of a dead studio. Two relics of a bygone era, smoking in the dark.

# CHAPTER 13: THE GAUNTLET - PART 3 (THE AMBUSH)

**LOCATION: THE CANYON**

We were ambushed in the canyon.

It was a good spot for an ambush. High walls. Narrow path. Classic.

*BOOM.*

An explosion rocked the buggy. We flipped.

I crawled out of the wreckage. My leg was backwards. I snapped it back into place. *CRUNCH.* "Motherfluffer!"

"Wade!" Logan yelled.

He was pinned. A piece of the chassis was on his chest.

And standing over him was Sabretooth.

Not the Liev Schreiber one. The Tyler Mane one. The big one. The one who looked like a lion that did steroids.

"Well, well," Sabretooth growled. "The runt."

"Victor," Logan wheezed.

"I've been waiting for this," Sabretooth said. He extended his claws. They were long. Dirty.

I tried to run. I tried to help.

But Pyro was there. He blasted me with fire.

"Hot! Hot! Hot!" I rolled. I burned. My skin bubbled.

I watched, helpless, as Sabretooth raised his hand.

"Time to die, Jimmy," Sabretooth grinned.

*Beat 1: Mechanics.*
Logan roared. He didn't try to lift the car. He popped his claws *through* the car door, stabbing Sabretooth in the foot.
Sabretooth howled. He stepped back.
Logan shoved the car off. It took every ounce of strength. Muscles tore. Veins popped.
He stood up.

*Beat 2: Consequence.*
They collided. It was primal. No technique. Just weight and hate.
Claws met claws. Sparks showered them.
Sabretooth bit Logan's shoulder. Logan stabbed Sabretooth's gut.
They were a ball of fur and rage.

*Beat 3: Aftertaste.*
Logan won.
He took Sabretooth's head off. *SNICK-SLICE.*
The head rolled to my feet.
Logan stood there, panting, bleeding from a dozen wounds.
He looked wild. He looked like an animal.
He looked at me. His eyes were blank.
"Logan?" I whispered.
He blinked. The man came back.
He fell to his knees.

# CHAPTER 14: THE AFTERMATH

We camped in the canyon.

We didn't talk. We just sat by the fire, healing.

The smell of burning mutant flesh (Sabretooth's corpse) hung in the air.

I looked at Logan. He was staring at the fire. He looked older. Smaller.

"You saved me," I said.

"I saved myself," he grunted.

"No," I said. "You could have run. You stayed."

He didn't answer.

I realized something then.

He wasn't the Worst Wolverine because he was weak. He was the Worst Wolverine because he cared too much. And it broke him.

"Thank you," I said.

He grunted. "Shut up."

"Okay," I said. "But for the record? That decapitation was top tier. Solid 9 out of 10. You lost a point for the landing."

He smirked. Just a little.

It was the first time I'd seen him smile.

It looked like hope.
