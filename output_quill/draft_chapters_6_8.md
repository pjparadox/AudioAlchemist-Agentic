# CHAPTER 6: THE HUNT

The multiverse, it turned out, smelled like ozone and desperation.

Wade fell out of the portal and landed face-first in mud. He spat out a mouthful of dirt.

"Okay. Universe 1. Let's see what we got."

He stood up. He was in a forest. It was raining. But not normal rain. It was movie rain. The kind that was backlit perfectly and made everyone look tragic.

He saw him.

Wolverine.

But not *his* Wolverine. This one was... short. Like, really short. He looked like a frustrated hobbit who hit the gym too hard.

"Hey, peanut. You look... compact."

The short Wolverine growled. It sounded like a chainsaw stuck in a log. "Walk away, Bub."

"I can't. I need you. You're the Anchor. You're the... wait, are you standing in a hole? Or are you just..."

*SNIKT.*

The claws popped.

"Okay, okay! Size doesn't matter! It's how you use the... oh god, he's lunging for my ankles!"

*PORTAL.*

Wade fell through.

***

Next stop.

A casino. Madripoor. Smoke hung in the air, thick and blue. It smelled of expensive tobacco and cheap morals.

There he was. Sitting at a card table. White tuxedo jacket. Eyepatch.

"Patch," Wade whispered. "Classic. Very Sean Connery meets a pirate."

He walked up to the table.

"Deal me in."

Patch looked up. The one eye was cold. "This is a private game."

"I'm a private guy. I just need you to come with me. Save a world. Be a hero. Maybe stab some people."

Patch took a puff of his cigar. He blew the smoke in Wade's face.

Then he retracted his claws *through* the table, skewering Wade's hand to the felt.

"OW!" Wade screamed. "That is a mahogany table! Do you have no respect for wood?"

*PORTAL.*

***

Next stop.

A dark alley. Rain again.

A figure on a cross. An X-shaped cross.

Wade stopped.

This wasn't funny. This was... iconic. It was the cover of *Uncanny X-Men* #251 brought to life in horrifying 4K resolution.

The Wolverine was crucified. Dying. Amidst a pile of skulls. The stench of decay was overpowering.

"Whoa. Okay. This is... heavy. Heavy metal album cover heavy."

The Wolverine looked up. His eyes were glazed. He was barely breathing.

"Help... me..."

"I... I can't." Wade's voice dropped. "I need a Wolverine who can fight. Not one who needs a tetanus shot and a hug."

He backed away. He felt dirty.

*PORTAL.*

***

Next stop.

A gym. Or a sterile room.

A man stood there with his back turned. Huge muscles. Wide shoulders.

"Logan?"

The man turned.

It was Henry Cavill. With a cigar.

Wade gasped. "The Cavillrine! You were the one who promised? The legends are true!"

Cavillrine pumped his arms—the reload motion. *Click-click.*

Claws popped.

"This feels right! This feels like efficient casting!"

Cavillrine punched him. Wade flew through a wall.

"Worth it!" Wade yelled as he fell into the orange void.

# CHAPTER 7: THE BAR

The bar smelled like a mistake.

It was the kind of place where the floor was sticky, the lights were dim to hide the stains, and the patrons looked like they were one bad song choice away from a felony.

Wade walked in. He adjusted his mask.

He saw him.

Sitting at the end of the bar. A bottle of whiskey in front of him. He was wearing a jacket that looked like it had been dragged behind a truck. But under the jacket... yellow.

Wade walked over.

"Is this seat taken? Or did the last guy die of alcohol poisoning?"

The Wolverine didn't look up. "Get lost."

"Classic. The brooding intro. I love it. Look, I've had a day. I've met a tiny you, a pirate you, and a Superman you. I'm tired. I need an Anchor."

"I'm not an anchor." Logan swirled the amber liquid in his glass. "I'm a drunk."

"You can be both. Functioning alcoholism is a superpower. Ask my dad. Wait, don't. He's dead."

Wade reached out and touched the Wolverine's shoulder.

"Come on, Wolvie. My world is dying. My Vanessa... she's gonna be gone. I need you."

The Wolverine turned.

His face was a roadmap of scars and stubble. His eyes were bloodshot. But there was something else there.

Pain.

Real, deep, bottomless pain.

"I said." He slammed the glass down. "Get lost."

He extended a claw. Just the middle one.

"Cute. Very mature."

"I'm not going anywhere with you. I'm done. I failed. Everyone is dead because of me."

"Everyone?"

"Everyone. The X-Men. The humans. The dog. I let them down."

"Well. That's a bummer. But guess what? In my world, you're a hero. You died a hero. You have a statue and everything. It's a little generous with the bulge, but it's tasteful."

Logan chugged the whiskey.

"I'm coming with you. Whether you like it or not."

He pulled out his Desert Eagle.

"I'm gonna shoot you. And then I'm gonna drag you."

Logan laughed. A dry, hacking sound. "You think you can hurt me?"

"I think I can annoy you into compliance."

*BANG.*

He shot Logan in the head.

Logan's head snapped back. He slumped onto the bar.

"See?" Wade said to the terrified bartender. "Conflict resolution."

# CHAPTER 8: PRUNED

Wade dragged the unconscious Wolverine into the TVA office.

"I found him!" Wade dropped Logan's heavy body onto the expensive rug. "I found the Anchor! He smells like a distillery, but he's breathing!"

Mr. Paradox looked up from his desk. He looked horrified.

"What is that?"

"That is the Wolverine. The Worst Wolverine, apparently. But he'll do. We'll fix him in post."

Logan groaned. He started to stir. The bullet hole in his forehead was already closing, pushing the metal slug out with a wet *plop*.

"You brought a Variant *here*? To the TVA? Do you have any idea the paperwork this creates?"

"Just put him in the timeline! Swap him out! Plug him in! Save my world!"

"It doesn't work like that!" Paradox shouted. "You can't just replace an Anchor Being! It's not a lightbulb, you idiot! It's a metaphysical constant!"

Logan sat up. He looked around. He looked at Paradox. He looked at Wade.

"Where the hell am I?"

"Space DMV. Don't ask."

"I told you." Paradox's voice turned cold. "Your world is dying. Mercy is a swift death."

He reached for a device on his desk. A pruning stick. But a big one. A Pruning Cannon.

"No!" Wade lunged.

Paradox fired.

But he didn't fire at Wade's head. He fired at the floor.

The floor dissolved.

Orange energy swirled. A hole opened up beneath them. A chute to hell.

"Enjoy the Void."

Wade fell.

Logan fell.

"YOU SON OF A BITCH!" Wade screamed as he tumbled into the darkness. "YOU OWE ME A VALIDATION STICKER!"

They fell for a long time.

They hit the ground hard. Sand. Dust.

Wade sat up, spitting out grit.

"Well. That went well."

Logan stood up. He popped his claws. All six of them.

"I'm gonna kill you."

"Get in line."
