# CHAPTER 9: THE WASTELAND

The Void didn't just look like the end of the world; it felt like it.

It was a landscape of discarded ideas. Broken monuments. Half-buried skyscrapers. The sky was a bruise—purple and angry, dominated by a cloud entity that roared like a hurricane made of lions.

Wade stood up. His suit was coated in grey dust.

"Okay. So. We're in the trash can. The cosmic landfill. Where bad plotlines go to die."

He looked at Logan.

The Wolverine was pacing. He looked like a caged tiger. He slashed a piece of rebar with his claws. *CLANG.*

"Where are we?"

"The Void. It's where the TVA sends things they don't like. Like us. And apparently..." He pointed to a giant stone head half-buried in the sand. "Is that a giant stone head of... me? No. Too handsome."

"I don't care." Logan snarled. "How do we get out?"

"We don't. That's the point. It's a one-way trip."

Logan stopped pacing. He turned to Wade.

"You did this. You shot me. You dragged me here."

"I was trying to save my world! I was trying to be a hero!"

"You're not a hero. You're a joke. A clown in a red suit."

"And you're a disappointment! The Worst Wolverine! That's what he called you! What did you do, huh? Did you forget to tip? Did you scratch the furniture?"

Logan roared. He lunged.

He tackled Wade into the sand.

*SNIKT.*

The claws came down. Wade caught Logan's wrists.

"Whoa! Easy, tiger! Let's use our words! Or a mediator!"

Logan headbutted him. *CRACK.*

Wade's nose broke. Then healed.

"Okay. We're doing this."

He kicked Logan in the chest. Logan flew back.

Wade drew his katanas.

"Dance off, bro!"

They charged.

The clash of Adamantium on steel rang out like a bell. Sparks flew. Logan swiped low, gutting Wade. Wade slashed high, taking off Logan's cheek. They healed. They fought. They were trapped in a loop of violence, fueled by hate and regeneration.

# CHAPTER 10: THE HONDA ODYSSEY

The fight had lasted for an hour. Or maybe a day. Time was weird here.

They were both exhausted. They were both bleeding. They were both healing.

"Call it a draw?" Wade panted. He was missing an ear. It was growing back, itchy and pink.

Logan was sitting on a rock, pulling a katana out of his thigh. "I hate you."

"The feeling is mutual. But look on the bright side. We have transportation."

He pointed.

Over the dune, a vehicle approached.

It was a Honda Odyssey. 2011. Gold.

"You have got to be kidding me."

"It's destiny! The Odyssey! The Chariot of the Gods! Or at least, the Chariot of Soccer Moms!"

The van stopped. A scavenger jumped out. He had a spear.

"Give me your boots!"

Wade shot him.

"Sorry. I need the van."

They got in.

The interior smelled of old Goldfish crackers, spilled milk, and resignation. It was the scent of a thousand school runs and family vacations gone wrong.

"Shotgun!"

"I'm driving."

"Fine. But I pick the music."

Wade turned the key. The engine sputtered to life. The radio turned on.

*Avril Lavigne. 'I'm with You'.*

"Yes!" Wade sang along. "It's a damn cold night! Trying to figure out this life!"

Logan slammed his head against the steering wheel. "Kill me. Again."

# CHAPTER 11: THE BORDERLANDS

They drove for miles across the wasteland.

They passed the ruins of the 20th Century Fox logo. Wade saluted it. "Rest in power, you magnificent corporate entity."

They approached a settlement. It was built inside the ribcage of a giant skeleton. Giant-Man? Galactus? Someone big.

"That looks ominous. Let's go there."

"It's a trap."

"It's a plot point. We have to go. It's how we meet the villain."

They drove the Honda into the camp.

Scavengers surrounded them. Mutants. Variants.

Wade saw faces he recognized from the *X-Men* movies. Toad. Sabretooth. Pyro.

"Holy cameo. It's the Brotherhood of Evil Background Actors."

A woman stepped out.

She was bald. She wore a long coat. She moved with a grace that was terrifying.

Cassandra Nova.

"Well, well." Her voice was like velvet wrapped around a razor blade. "What do we have here? A talker. And a walker."

She looked at Logan. She licked her lips.

"I like the walker. He feels... heavy."

She raised a hand.

Wade felt a pressure in his head. Not just a headache. It felt like fingers—cold, slimy fingers—sliding *inside* his brain tissue. Rumaging through the filing cabinets of his mind.

"Get out of my head, Charles!"

"I'm not Charles. I'm his twin. The one who strangled him in the womb."

She flicked her wrist.

Wade and Logan went flying. They crashed into the sand.

"Welcome to the Borderlands. You're going to feed Alioth."

# CHAPTER 12: THE FIRST CLASH

Cassandra's mind-fingers were disgusting.

Wade could feel them. *She's looking for my PIN number. Joke's on her, I don't have a bank account.*

"You're empty." Cassandra looked at Wade with disdain. "Just noise. And cancer."

She turned to Logan.

"But you..." She stepped closer. She placed her fingers on Logan's temples. "You are full of tragedy. Let's see."

Logan screamed.

"Get off him!" Wade tried to stand, but his legs felt like jelly.

"Quiet, pet." She didn't even look at him. She telekinetically slammed him into the ground.

Logan fell to his knees. He was weeping.

"I wasn't there," Logan sobbed. "I was drinking. They called me. And I wasn't there."

"Delicious. The guilt. It's like a fine wine."

Suddenly, a roar cut through the air.

Not Alioth. Not Logan.

An engine.

A car jumped over the bone-wall of the camp. It was a custom hot-rod. Fantastic Four style? No. Something else.

"Johnny? Is that Johnny Storm?"

A man jumped out of the car.

"Flame On!"

"Evans! Captain America! Oh my god, language!"

The man burst into flames. He flew at Cassandra.

"Run!" Wade grabbed the Wolverine. He dragged him toward the Honda Odyssey.

"We're leaving! We're escaping via the convenient distraction!"

They jumped in the van. Wade slammed it into reverse.

They sped out of the camp, leaving the chaos behind.

"Who was that?" Logan asked, wiping tears/blood from his face.

"That was the budget increasing."

He looked at Logan.

"You okay, peanut?"

Logan didn't answer. He just stared out the window at the purple sky.

"We have to kill her."

"Cassandra?"

"Yeah." Logan popped a claw. "She made me remember."

Wade nodded.

"Okay. Revenge mission. I like it. It's classic. It's simple. Let's do it."

He cranked the radio.

*Grease. 'You're the One That I Want'.*

"Really?"

"It's a banger. Now shut up and drive."
