# CHAPTER 14: EMILY: THE SPIRAL

*Flashback. December 1976.*

The pills were orange.
Small, oval, innocent-looking things.
*Gambrolic. 200mg.*
Emily held the bottle in her hand. It rattled. *Ch-ch-ch.*
It sounded like a rattlesnake. Or a baby rattle made of bones.

She was back in her dorm room. The university had let her return, "pending stabilization."
Stabilization.
That's what they called it.
Emily called it "The Fog."
She took the pill. She swallowed it dry. It tasted of chalk and chemical bitterness. It scraped down her throat.
Within twenty minutes, the fog rolled in.
It wasn't a visual fog. It was a sensory one.
The world lost its sharp edges. Colors became muted, pastel washes of grey and beige. Sounds were muffled, as if she were underwater.
Alice's voice, usually sharp and funny, became a drone.
"You okay, Em? You're staring at the wall again."
"I'm fine," Emily said.
But her voice sounded wrong. Slow. Thick.
Her tongue felt huge in her mouth. A slab of meat she couldn't control.
*Slurring,* Dr. Vogel had said. *A temporary side effect. Ataxia. Difficulty with muscle coordination.*
She tried to stand up. Her legs felt heavy, like she was wading through molasses.
She stumbled.
"Whoa," Alice said, catching her. "Easy there, space cadet."
"I'm fine," Emily repeated. It was the only sentence she could form.

She went to the cafeteria for lunch.
The smell hit her at the door.
Usually, the cafeteria smelled of floor wax and pizza.
Today, it smelled of... slaughter.
The scent of the meatloaf was overpowering. It smelled of raw blood. Of guts. Of something dead that had been left in the sun.
Emily gagged.
She walked to the line. She grabbed a tray. Her hands were shaking.
*Tremors,* Vogel had said. *Benign.*
She looked at the food.
The mashed potatoes looked like grey paste. The green beans looked like worms.
And the meat...
It was pulsing.
Just a little. A rhythmic throb. *Thump. Thump.*
Emily stared at it.
*"Eat,"* a voice whispered.
It wasn't the lunch lady.
It was the voice.
*"Eat the flesh. Take it into you."*
Emily dropped the tray.
*CLANG.*
The sound echoed in the cavernous room. Silence rippled out from the crash site.
Everyone looked at her. Hundreds of eyes.
"I'm sorry," Emily whispered. "I'm sorry."
She ran.
She ran out of the cafeteria, ignoring the stares, ignoring the whispers.
*She's crazy. That's the girl who freaked out in the library. She's on drugs.*
*Yes,* Emily thought. *I am on drugs. And they aren't working.*

She went to her class. *Introduction to Anthropology.*
She sat in the back row.
Professor Arndt was talking about shamanism. About possession cults in Haiti.
"In these cultures," Arndt said, pacing the stage, "the possessed individual is not seen as sick. They are seen as a vessel. A horse for the spirits to ride."
Emily gripped her desk.
*A horse.*
That's what she felt like. A beast of burden. Being ridden by something heavy and cruel.
The Gambrolic made her body heavy, but it didn't stop the rider. It just made the horse too tired to buck.
The rider was digging its spurs in.
She felt a pain in her side. Sharp. Hot.
She looked down.
She was digging her pen into her hand. The ink was bleeding into her skin, mixing with blood.
She hadn't even felt it.
She stopped.
She looked up at the professor.
Professor Arndt had stopped talking. He was looking at her.
But his face...
His face was melting.
His eyes were sliding down his cheeks. His mouth was a vertical slit.
He opened his mouth to speak.
But instead of words, a swarm of flies flew out.
*Buzzing.*
The sound filled the room.
Emily stood up.
"Stop it!" she screamed. "Stop looking at me!"
The class turned.
They didn't have faces.
They were mannequins. Blank, smooth plastic heads.
And they were all laughing.
A dry, scratching sound.
Emily grabbed her bag and ran.

She ran to the park. The shortcut.
It was snowing. Big, wet flakes that stuck to her eyelashes.
She sat on a bench, shivering.
She looked at the bottle of pills in her hand.
*Gambrolic.*
Dr. Vogel said it would fix the wires. He said it would stop the storm.
But the storm was inside the pill.
The pill locked the doors. It boarded up the windows.
But it locked the monster *inside* with her.
She couldn't pray.
She tried.
"Our Father..."
Her mind went blank. The words were gone. Erased.
*We ate them,* the voice whispered. *We ate your prayers.*
*You are empty. You are a house swept clean.*
*And we are moving in.*

A dog trotted by. A stray mongrel.
It stopped. It looked at her.
It whined.
Then it snarled.
It bared its teeth, backing away, its hackles raised.
It wasn't looking at Emily.
It was looking at the thing sitting on the bench next to her.
Emily didn't want to look.
But she had to.
She turned her head.
The bench was empty.
But the snow...
The snow on the empty seat was depressed.
A perfect imprint of a large, heavy body.
And the smell...
Sulfur. And burning hair.
The thing was sitting right next to her.
Waiting.
*"You are tired,"* it whispered. *"Sleep. Let us take the wheel."*
Emily closed her eyes.
She felt a hand on her shoulder.
A cold, heavy hand.
She didn't fight it.
She was too tired. The Gambrolic made her so tired.
She leaned into the cold.
And let the darkness take her.
