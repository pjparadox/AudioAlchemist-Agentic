# Tone Audit Mapping Strategy

## Principles
1. **Downgrade Intensity:** If EN is "Damn/Stupid", VN should not be "Chó chết/Khốn nạn".
2. **Contextual Slang:** "Vãi" is okay for high-energy gamer slang, but not for quiet shock ("Dude...") or awkward laughter unless the character is consistently vulgar.
3. **Sexual Profanity:** Avoid "buồi" (dick) unless EN explicitly says "dick/cock". "What the hell" != "Thằng đầu buồi".
4. **Narration vs Dialogue:** Narration should be cleaner unless it's deep POV of a vulgar character.
