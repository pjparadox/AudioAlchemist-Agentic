# REVISION PROTOCOL: ULTRA-DENSITY

## OBJECTIVE
Expand "Part I" from 6,400 words to 15,000+ words.
**Strategy:** "The Fractal Expansion". Do not just add scenes; dive deeper into existing moments.

## THE RULES
1.  **The "Stephen King" Rule:** Do not just say "She drank coffee." Describe the mug (chipped, souvenir from a trip she didn't enjoy), the taste (burnt, metallic), and the sound of the swallow. History anchors objects.
2.  **The "Jun Prince" Rule:** Dialogue must have subtext. No "on the nose" exchanges. Every conversation is a power struggle or a seduction.
3.  **The "Blackmore" Rule:** Pain is specific. Not "it hurt," but "it felt like a wet screw turning in the bone."
4.  **The "Ghost Injection" Rule:** If a scene feels short, inject a memory. When Erin looks at the jury, she shouldn't just see jurors; she should remember a jury she lost five years ago.

## TARGET DENSITY
*   **Prologue:** 1,500 words.
*   **Chapters:** 2,000 - 2,500 words each.
*   **Total Part I:** ~15,000 words.
