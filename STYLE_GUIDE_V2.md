# STYLE_GUIDE_V2.md: The Three-Engine Strategy

## 1. The Three Engines (Structure)
1.  **The Trial:** Present tense urgency (written in past). Casework, chess, pressure.
2.  **The Haunting:** Flashback engine. Slow collapse, desperation.
3.  **The Contagion:** Supernatural bleed. The infection spreads to Erin.

## 2. The POV Plan
*   **Erin Bruner (Primary):** Legal thriller propulsion + personal haunting.
*   **Father Moore (Secondary):** Faith under siege + guilt.
*   **Emily (Tertiary):** The fuse. Shorter, sensory, ambiguous.

## 3. The Skill Set (Technique)
### Jun Prince (Erin's Voice)
*   **Strengths:** Voice, relational tension, stinging levity.
*   **Application:** Socially sharp narration. Banter. "Ordinary life" beats (laundry, wine) that make horror invasive.

### Neal Shusterman (Plot & Ethics)
*   **Strengths:** Ethical dilemmas, clean escalation, braided structure.
*   **Application:** Every witness forces a worse choice. Tight "if/then" plotting.

### Keith Blackmore (The Horror)
*   **Strengths:** Physicality, momentum, survival-horror realism.
*   **Application:** Restraints, bruises, dehydration, the *smell* of the room. Adrenaline.

### Stephen King (The World)
*   **Strengths:** Place, secondary characters, tastable dread.
*   **Application:** The farm, town, campus as characters. Side characters with tiny lives.

## 4. Expanded Arcs
*   **Erin:** From Control to Surrender. Has a private wound (failed past case).
*   **Moore:** Not a saint. Fears being wrong. Crushed by institutional hesitation.
*   **Emily:** Funny, hardworking, independent. Preserve ambiguity.
*   **Owen Thomas:** Not a villain. Believes he is protecting the world from superstition.
