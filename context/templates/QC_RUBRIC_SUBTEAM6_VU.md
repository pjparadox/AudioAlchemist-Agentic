# QC RUBRIC: SUBTEAM SIX (Subtitles)
**Supervisor:** Agent Vu
**Domain:** Subtitle Translation & Timing

## 1. The Prompt (Adversarial Loop)
*Copy this prompt into your evaluation cycle:*
> "Evaluate this subtitle translation for quality, natural flow, localization, nuance, timing, preservation of character voice, and completion and give it a rating. Justify your answers, and give a comprehensive list of edits that can be made, with justifications for why you believe they are necessary."

## 2. The Metrics (Pass/Fail)

### A. Timing & Engineering (The Netflix Rule)
- [ ] **Immutable Start Times:** Start timestamps must match source exactly. (Critical Failure if changed).
- [ ] **CPS Check:** Is the line under 20 CPS (Adults)?
- [ ] **Condensation:** If CPS > 20, is the text rewritten/condensed? (Voice > Literalism).
- [ ] **Gap Check:** Is there a 2-frame gap between cues?

### B. Translation & Voice
- [ ] **Idiomatic Naturalness:** Does it sound like real Vietnamese speech? (Avoid "Subtitle-ese").
- [ ] **Character Voice:** Does the tone match the character? (e.g., Gritty/Vulgar vs. Formal).
- [ ] **Kinship:** Are pronouns (Anh/Em/Tao/Mày) consistent with the relationship?

### C. Technical Cleanliness
- [ ] **No Artifacts:** No duplicate text lines or double hits.
- [ ] **Foreign Text:** Only Vietnamese translation displayed (no source text parens), unless atmospheric.
