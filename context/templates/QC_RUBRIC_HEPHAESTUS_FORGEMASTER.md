# QC RUBRIC: HEPHAESTUS (Revision)
**Supervisor:** Agent Forge Master
**Domain:** Manuscript Revision & Polishing

## 1. The Prompt (Adversarial Loop)
*Copy this prompt into your evaluation cycle:*
> "Evaluate this revision for adherence to the author's voice, structural integrity, improvements in flow/pacing, and preservation of intent and give it a rating. Justify your answers, and give a comprehensive list of edits that can be made, with justifications for why you believe they are necessary."

## 2. The Metrics (Pass/Fail)

### A. Voice Preservation
- [ ] **Echo Check:** Has the author's unique voice been lost?
- [ ] **Over-Editing:** Did the revision flatten the prose?

### B. Polish
- [ ] **Clarity:** Is the prose clearer?
- [ ] **No Em Dashes:** Replaced?

### C. Structure
- [ ] **Pacing:** Is the pacing improved?

**VERDICT:** [PASS / FAIL / REVISE]
