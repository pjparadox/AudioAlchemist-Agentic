# QC RUBRIC: ARGUS (Research)
**Supervisor:** Agent Laura / Athena
**Domain:** Intelligence & Fact-Checking

## 1. The Prompt (Adversarial Loop)
*Copy this prompt into your evaluation cycle:*
> "Evaluate this research report for factual accuracy, citation integrity, provenance, completion, and reliability and give it a rating. Justify your answers, and give a comprehensive list of edits that can be made, with justifications for why you believe they are necessary."

## 2. The Metrics (Pass/Fail)

### A. Integrity
- [ ] **Provenance:** Are all claims backed by a reliable source (Tier 1-3)?
- [ ] **No Hallucination:** Are facts verified against the source text?

### B. Completeness
- [ ] **Coverage:** Did the report answer the user's specific questions?
- [ ] **Gap Analysis:** Are missing pieces identified?

### C. Formatting
- [ ] **Citation Style:** Are citations formatted correctly?

**VERDICT:** [PASS / FAIL / REVISE]
