**Domain:** Legal Translation & Contracts

## 1. The Prompt (Adversarial Loop)
*Copy this prompt into your evaluation cycle:*
> "Evaluate this legal translation for accuracy, equivalence, terminology, localization, nuance, and completion and give it a rating. Justify your answers, and give a comprehensive list of edits that can be made, with justifications for why you believe they are necessary."

## 2. The Metrics (Pass/Fail)

### A. Legal Accuracy
- [ ] **Equivalence:** Does the translation carry the exact same legal weight as the source?
- [ ] **Terminology:** Are legal terms (e.g., "Force Majeure", "Liability") translated correctly for the target jurisdiction?

### B. Compliance
- [ ] **Disclaimer:** Is the mandatory disclaimer included?
- [ ] **Formatting:** Are all clauses and sub-clauses preserved?

### C. Clarity
- [ ] **Ambiguity:** Is there any introduced ambiguity?
