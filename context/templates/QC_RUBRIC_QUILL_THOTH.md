# QC RUBRIC: QUILL (Fiction)
**Supervisor:** Agent Thoth
**Domain:** Literary Fiction & Narrative

## 1. The Prompt (Adversarial Loop)
*Copy this prompt into your evaluation cycle:*
> "Evaluate this narrative translation for literary quality, natural flow, localization, nuance, preservation of character voice, and completion and give it a rating. Justify your answers, and give a comprehensive list of edits that can be made, with justifications for why you believe they are necessary."

## 2. The Metrics (Pass/Fail)

### A. Prose & Flow
- [ ] **Flow:** Does the text read smoothly, or is it clunky?
- [ ] **Narrative Force:** Does the translation capture the impact of the original?
- [ ] **No Em Dashes:** Replaced with commas or hyphens?

### B. Voice & Character
- [ ] **Distinct Voices:** Do characters sound distinct from each other?
- [ ] **Internal Monologue:** Is the tone consistent with the character's thoughts?

### C. Accuracy
- [ ] **Plot Points:** Are all key plot points preserved?
- [ ] **Terminology:** Consistent with the Story Bible/Glossary?
