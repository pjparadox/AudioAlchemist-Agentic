# HIGH-STAKES LEGAL TRANSLATION GUIDE (LITIGATION TIER)

**Classification:** TIER 1 (Strict Liability)
**Scope:** Appeals, Court Filings, Contracts, Binding Disclaimers.
**Warning:** "Good enough" is negligence. Ambiguity is malpractice.

## 1. VIETNAMESE (VN) – Jurisdictional Mapping

### A. The "Administrative vs. Judicial" Divide
Vietnam operates a dual track for administrative complaints. You must distinguish where the case sits.
*   **Grievant (Agency Phase):** *Người khiếu nại*.
*   **Petitioner/Plaintiff (Court Phase):** *Người khởi kiện* (Admin Court) or *Nguyên đơn* (Civil Court).
*   **Appellant (Appellate Phase):** *Người kháng cáo*.
    *   *Trap:* Do not call a Petitioner "Người khiếu nại" in a brief to the Appellate Court. It implies they are still begging the agency for help. Use **"Người kháng cáo"** or **"Nguyên đơn"**.

### B. "Dismissal" Terminology
*   **Bác bỏ (Dismissal on Merits):** The court heard the case and decided you lose. Final.
*   **Đình chỉ (Discontinuation):** The court stopped the case (e.g., mootness, withdrawal). Not necessarily a loss on merits.
*   **Trả lại đơn (Return of Petition):** Procedural rejection (e.g., wrong court).
*   *Application:* "Dismissed for failure to exhaust" is a procedural bar acting as a final judgment. Use **"Bác bỏ đơn kiện"** (Dismiss lawsuit) but specify reason: **"vì lý do chưa hoàn tất thủ tục..."**.

### C. False Friends
*   **"Exhaustion":** NOT *mệt mỏi* or *cạn kiệt* (physical). MUST BE **"Hoàn tất"** (complete) or **"Thực hiện xong"** (perform completely) regarding remedies.
*   **"Stay" (of proceedings):** NOT *ở lại*. MUST BE **"Tạm đình chỉ"** (Temporary suspension).

## 2. JAPANESE (JP) – The "Kyakka" Protocol

### A. The Fatal Distinction: Kyakka vs. Kikyaku
*   **Kyakka (却下) – "Kick Out":** Dismissal on procedural grounds (e.g., no jurisdiction, late filing, failure to exhaust). The court *refuses to hear* the substance.
    *   *Consequence:* Plaintiff can often refile if the defect is cured.
*   **Kikyaku (棄却) – "Reject":** Dismissal on the merits. The court heard the substance and ruled the Plaintiff is *wrong*.
    *   *Consequence:* Res Judicata applies. Cannot refile.
*   *Mandate:* For "Failure to Exhaust Administrative Remedies," use **KYAKKA (却下)**. Using *Kikyaku* implies the underlying claim (ADA violation) was proven false, which is factually incorrect and harmful.

### B. Court Hierarchy
*   **Circuit Court:** *Junkai Saibansho* (Direct translation, rare in JP) or *Chihou Saibansho* (District Court equivalent).
    *   *Note:* Use **"Junkai Saibansho (巡回裁判所)"** for US context, but note it functions as a court of general jurisdiction.
*   **Tribunal (Agency):** *Shimpan-sho* (Inquiry board) or *Choukai* (Disciplinary, if applicable). For benefits hearings: **"Choukai" (聴会)** or **"Shinri" (審理 - Hearing)**.

## 3. GERMAN (DE) – Precision & Bureaucracy

### A. Appeals: Berufung vs. Revision
*   **Berufung:** Full second instance. Re-examines facts and law.
*   **Revision:** Third instance (usually). Examines *only* legal errors.
*   *Application:* US Appellate Courts generally defer to trial court fact-finding unless "manifestly erroneous." They are closer to *Revision* but structurally are the first level of appeal (*Berufung* level).
    *   *Standard:* Use **"Berufung"** for the Illinois Appellate Court. Use **"Revision"** only for Supreme Court reviews of legal interpretation.

### B. "Reset" & Nullity
*   **Nichtig (Void):** Void ab initio. It never existed legally.
*   **Anfechtbar (Voidable):** Valid until challenged.
*   **Aufgehoben (Quashed/Lifted):** Cancelled by authority.
*   *Application:* A "Reset Letter" that treats a hearing as if it didn't count makes the hearing **"Nichtig"** (effectively) or **"Annulliert"**.

## 4. QC AUDIT CHECKLIST (THE GAUNTLET)

**The "Hostile Auditor" Question:**
> *"If I were the opposing counsel, could I claim this translation admits guilt or waives a right?"*

1.  **Check "Exhaustion":** Does it imply physical tiredness? (Fail). Does it imply the remedies are "used up" (correct) or "abandoned" (wrong)?
2.  **Check "Dismissal":** Does it imply a loss on merits when it was procedural? (Critical Fail).
3.  **Check "Record":** Is "Record" translated as "Music Disc" or "Written File"? (Context check).
4.  **Check "Parties":** Are parties consistently identified as "Appellants" vs "Complainants"?

**Required Action:** Any translation failing this checklist must be marked **"DANGEROUS"** and rejected.
