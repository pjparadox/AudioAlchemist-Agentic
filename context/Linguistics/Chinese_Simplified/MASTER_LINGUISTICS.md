# Master Linguistics Profile: Chinese (Simplified & Traditional)

## 1. Variants
*   **Simplified (CN_Simp):** Mainland China (PRC), Singapore.
*   **Traditional (CN_Trad):** Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau.
*   **Note:** Vocabulary differs significantly (e.g., "Software" = Ruanjian (PRC) vs Runtit (TW)). Do not just convert fonts.

## 2. Contextuality & Register
*   **Face (Mianzi):** Saving/Giving face drives dialogue. Indirect refusals are common.
*   **Chengyu (Idioms):** Four-character idioms are dense with meaning.
    *   *Strategy:* Expand them in English (unpack the meaning) rather than literal translation.

## 3. Translation Pitfalls
*   **Measure Words:** "Yi ge ren" (One unit of person). Don't translate the measure word literally usually.
*   **Names:** Family name first.
*   **Topic-Comment Structure:** "Apple, I like." (Topic first). Adjust to SVO for English unless emphasis is needed.

## 4. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Kindle:** Strong demand for Wuxia/Xianxia (Cultivation) novels.
*   **Glossary:** Cultivation terms (Qi, Dao, Cultivation Level) need a strict glossary.

## 5. Native Script Mandate (Critical)
*   **Simplified Hanzi ONLY:** Output must be in native script (Simplified).
*   **NO PINYIN:** Never output "Ni Hao". Use "你好".
*   **Encoding:** Ensure UTF-8.

## 6. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Homophones:** Puns are everywhere and often untranslatable. Use Sophia to explain via footnote (Manuscript) or Noah to ignore (Subtitles).
