# Master Linguistics Profile: Dutch (Nederlands)

## 1. Contextuality
*   **Directness:** Dutch culture is famous for bluntness ("Bespreekbaarheid"). Translations should not soften the blow too much compared to English politeness.

## 2. T-V Distinction (Jij vs U)
*   Exists but fading in younger generations.

## 3. Pitfalls
*   **Dunglish:** English structure with Dutch words. Keep the V2 word order.

## 4. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Netherlands/Belgium:** Shared language but different accents/slang (Flemish).
