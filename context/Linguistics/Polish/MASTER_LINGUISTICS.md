# Master Linguistics Profile: Polish (Polski)

## 1. Morphology
*   **Cases:** 7 grammatical cases. Nouns change endings.
*   **Gender:** Masc/Fem/Neut.

## 2. Contextuality
*   **Pan/Pani:** Mandatory formal address (Sir/Madam) used with 3rd person verb. "Pan idzie?" (Is Sir going?) instead of "Are you going?".
    *   *Translation:* Map to "You" (Formal) or "Sir".

## 3. Market
*   Strong fantasy/sci-fi tradition (Witcher).
