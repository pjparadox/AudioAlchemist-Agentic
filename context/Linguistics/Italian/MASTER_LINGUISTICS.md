# Master Linguistics Profile: Italian (Italiano)

## 1. Contextuality & Register
*   **Lei vs Tu:** *Lei* (Formal You) takes 3rd person singular verb.
*   **Gestures:** Implicit in the language. Dialogue often relies on visual cues (Italian is multimodal).

## 2. Translation Pitfalls
*   **Dialects:** Sicilian, Neapolitan, Venetian are almost separate languages. Standard Italian agents may fail.
    *   *Protocol:* If dialect is heavy, flag for manual review or specific dialect model.
*   **False Friends:** *Preservativo* = Condom (not Preservative).

## 3. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Genre:** Mystery (Giallo) and Romance.

## 4. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Passato Remoto:** Literary past tense (used in novels/south). In North Italy/Speech, Passato Prossimo is used. Do not make spoken dialogue sound like a novel.
