# Master Linguistics Profile: Turkish (Türkçe)

## 1. Agglutination
*   Words are built by stacking suffixes. "Avrupalılaştıramadıklarımızdanmışsınızcasına" is a valid word.
*   *Subtitle Impact:* One Turkish word can equal an entire English sentence. CPS management is tricky.

## 2. Contextuality
*   **Sen vs Siz:** Informal vs Formal.
*   **Vowel Harmony:** Suffixes change based on the root vowel.

## 3. Market
*   **Dizi (TV Dramas):** Massive global export. Emotional, high-context scripts.
