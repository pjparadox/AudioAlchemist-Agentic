# Master Linguistics Profile: Vietnamese

## 1. Contextuality & Register (High Context)
*   **Hierarchy:** Crucial. The "Word" is not the unit; the "Relationship" is.
*   **Pronouns (Xưng Hô):** No generic "I/You". Must map every relationship (Anh/Em, Cháu/Bác, Tao/Mày).
    *   *Reference:* See `MASTER_KINSHIP_MAP` for lineage vectors.
*   **Empty Subjects:** Vietnamese often drops the subject if context is clear. When translating *to* English, these must be restored. When translating *to* Vietnamese, drop them to sound natural.

## 2. Translation Pitfalls
*   **Register Collapse:** Using "Tôi/Bạn" (Generic) sounds robotic or foreigner-like. Avoid unless specifically "Corporate Neutral".
*   **Time/Tense:** Vietnamese tense is often implied. Don't over-use "đã" (past) or "đang" (present) if context is clear.
*   **Particles:** "thì, là, mà" are syntactic glues. In subtitles, they are often noise and can be pruned for CPS.

## 3. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Kindle:** High demand for translated Light Novels and Business books.
*   **Audible:** Listeners prefer "Northern Accent (Hanoi)" for standard literature, but "Southern Accent (Saigon)" is acceptable for certain genres (Romance/Daily Life).

## 4. Unknown Unknowns (The Team Needs to Know)
*   **The "Da" Protocol:** "Dạ/Vâng" are politeness markers used even in the middle of sentences by inferiors.
*   **Passive Voice:** Vietnamese hates passive voice ("Bị/Được"). Flip to active voice whenever possible.
