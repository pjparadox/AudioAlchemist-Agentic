# Master Linguistics Profile: Spanish (Español)

## 1. Variants (The Big Split)
*   **Peninsular (Spain):** Uses *Vosotros* (Plural Informal You). Lisped 'c/z'.
*   **Latin American (LatAm):** Uses *Ustedes* for all Plural You. Uses *Vos* in Argentina/Uruguay.
*   **Directive:** You MUST identify the region. Translating a Mexican drug drama with "Vosotros" is a critical failure.

## 2. Contextuality
*   **T-V Distinction (Tú vs Usted):**
    *   *Usted:* Respect/Distance.
    *   *Tú:* Intimacy.
*   **Gender:** Romance language gender.

## 3. Translation Pitfalls
*   **False Friends:** *Embarazada* = Pregnant (not Embarrassed). *Constipado* = Cold (not Constipated).
*   **Sentence Length:** Spanish sentences run long. For English subs, split them.
*   **Question Marks:** Inverted `¿` at start.

## 4. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Mexico vs Spain:** Two distinct Amazon stores (.com.mx vs .es). Localization must match.

## 5. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Subjunctive Mood:** Expresses doubt/emotion. English often loses this nuance. Ensure the *uncertainty* is conveyed.
