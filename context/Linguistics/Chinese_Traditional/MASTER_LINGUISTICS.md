# Master Linguistics Profile: Chinese (Traditional) - Taiwan/HK

## 1. Distinct Identity
*   **Vocabulary:** Uses Traditional characters. Taiwan uses Zhuyin for input, PRC uses Pinyin.
*   **Tone:** HK Cantonese (written) is very different from Standard Written Chinese (Mandarin).
    *   *Check:* Is the source Mandarin (TW) or Cantonese (HK)?

## 2. Contextuality
*   **Politeness:** Taiwan Mandarin is often softer ("Hao de" vs "Hao") than Beijing Mandarin.
*   **Loan Words:** More influence from Japanese and English in Taiwan/HK.

## 3. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Complex Characters:** Ensure encoding is UTF-8 Traditional.
*   **Vertical Text:** Sometimes used in high-brow literature.

## 4. Native Script Mandate (Critical)
*   **Traditional Hanzi ONLY:** Output must be in native script (Traditional).
*   **NO PINYIN/ZHUYIN:** Never output Romanized/Bopomofo text as the final product. Use "你好".

## 5. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Political Sensitivity:** Terms relating to governance/history diverge sharply from PRC usage. Adhere to the **Context of Origin** (Tier 1).
