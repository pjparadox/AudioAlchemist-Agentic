[REGISTER & TONE]
Genre: High-Fantasy / Historical Drama (Cung đình).
High Valyrian Rules: Use archaic Hán-Việt (Sino-Vietnamese) for a poetic/sacred feel.
   - Example: "Long hồn" (Dragon soul), "Huyết mạch" (Bloodline), "Băng hàn vượt thời đại" (Ice spanning ages).
Addressing Dragons: Dragons are sacred. NEVER use "nó" (it). Dragonriders use "ngươi" (respected subordinate/partner) or address them by name.
