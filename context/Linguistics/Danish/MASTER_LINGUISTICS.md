# Master Linguistics Profile: Scandinavian (Danish, Swedish, Norwegian)

## 1. The Mutual Intelligibility Spectrum
*   Written Danish and Norwegian (Bokmål) are very similar.
*   Spoken Norwegian and Swedish are mutually intelligible-ish.
*   **False Friends:** "Rolig" = Calm (Swe/Nor) vs Fun (Dan). "Frokost" = Breakfast (Swe) vs Lunch (Dan/Nor).

## 2. Contextuality (Janteloven)
*   **The Law of Jante:** Cultural humility. "Don't think you are special."
*   **Tone:** Dialogue is often understated, direct, and pragmatic. Minimalism is key.

## 3. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Nordic Noir:** Huge market. Crime thrillers.
*   **Market:** Often aggregated, but distinct localizations are preferred.

## 4. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Du-reform:** Swedes almost exclusively use "Du" (Informal) now, even to the King. The formal "Ni" is rare/archaic or snobbish. Do not over-formalize translations.
