# Master Linguistics Profile: Portuguese (Português)

## 1. Variants (Brazil vs Portugal)
*   **Brazilian (PT-BR):** Huge market. Uses "Você" for You (mostly). Gerunds (-ando).
*   **European (PT-PT):** Uses "Tu" more. Infinitive constructions (a fazer).
*   **Divergence:** Pronunciation and vocabulary differ drastically. A PT-PT sub on a BR show will anger users.

## 2. Amazon/Market Specifics
*   **Brazil:** Major Kindle Unlimited market.

## 3. Unknown Unknowns
*   **Saudade:** The untranslatable feeling of longing/melancholy. Keep it or explain it; "Miss" is too weak.
