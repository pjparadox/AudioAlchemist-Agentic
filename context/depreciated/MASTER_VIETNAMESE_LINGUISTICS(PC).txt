Register: High-Fantasy/Historical Drama (Cung đình).

High Valyrian: Use archaic Hán-Việt (Sino-Vietnamese) for a poetic/sacred feel.

Examples: "Long hồn" (Dragon soul), "Huyết mạch" (Bloodline), "Băng hàn vượt thời đại" (Ice spanning ages), "Hỏa long cũng hóa tro" (Fire dragons turn to ash).

Social Positioning (Xưng hô):

King/Queen Regnant: Bệ hạ / Trẫm (Imperial Self).

Queen Dowager/Mother: Thái hậu / Nương nương.

Queen Consort: Vương hậu.

Prince/Princess: Hoàng tử / Công chúa.

Husband/Wife: Phu quân / Thê tử (Formal); Chàng / Thiếp (Intimate).

Siblings: Older Brother (Đại huynh), Younger Brother (Đệ/Hiền đệ), Older Sister (Tỷ tỷ), Younger Sister (Muội muội).

Uncle: Thúc thúc (Daemon to Rhaenyra’s kids).

Class/Insult Markers:

Bastard: "Con hoang" (spiteful), "Con riêng" (neutral), "Long chủng" (Dragonseed - noble tone).

Smallfolk: Tiện dân / Dân đen. Use "Tao/Mày" (crude/aggressive).

Traitors: Kẻ tiếm quyền (Usurper), Kẻ cướp ngôi, Nghịch tặc (Classical traitor).

The "Strong" Pun: Use "Những chàng trai dòng họ Strong" and emphasize "mạnh mẽ."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VIETNAMESE KINSHIP GUIDE FOR TRANSLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Royal Titles:
Your Grace (King/Queen): Bệ hạ
Your Grace (Dowager): Nương nương / Thái hậu
Prince: Hoàng tử
Princess: Công chúa
My Lord: Đại nhân / Lãnh chúa

Sibling/Relative Terms (Formal):
Older Brother: Đại huynh
Younger Brother: Hiền đệ (formal) / Em trai (casual)
Older Sister: Tỷ tỷ
Younger Sister: Muội muội
Uncle (Father's younger brother): Thúc thúc (This applies to Daemon's blood relation to Rhaenyra).
Uncle (Father's older brother): Bá phụ / Bác.

Marital Terms:
Husband: Phu quân (formal) / Chàng (intimate).
Wife: Thê tử (formal) / Thiếp (intimate).

Power Hierarchy Overriding Age:
The King/Queen Status: When Aegon II becomes King, his older sister Rhaenyra would never be addressed as Tỷ tỷ (Older Sister) by his supporters. They would use "Công chúa" (Princess) to demote her status or "Kẻ phản nghịch" (Rebel) to strip her of title.
Aemond and Aegon: Even though Aemond is more competent, he must use "Thần" (Your Servant/Subject) when speaking to Aegon in a formal court setting, and "Đệ" (Younger Brother) only in private.

The Nuance of Bastardy (The "Strong" Boys):
Con hoang: This is the most common and insulting translation for bastard. It implies "wild child" or "fatherless." This is what Aemond would use to provoke Jace and Luke.
Con riêng: A more neutral term for a child from another relationship.
Long chủng (Dragonseed): This is a specific term you should use for the bastards of Valyrian descent in Season 2 (Hugh, Ulf, Addam). It translates to "Dragon Seed" and sounds much more formal and legendary than a common slur.

The "Queen Who Never Was" (Rhaenys):
Best translation: "Vị Nữ Hoàng Chưa Từng Lên Ngôi."
Nuance: In Vietnamese culture, "vị" is a respectful classifier for a person of high status. Using "vị" distinguishes her as someone who should have been Queen.

High Valyrian vs. Common Tongue:
In High Valyrian, use words like "Long hồn" (Dragon soul) instead of "linh hồn của rồng." This gives the audience a sense that the language they are speaking is ancient and sacred.

The "Small Council" and Official Titles:
Small Council: Tiểu Hội đồng.
Hand of the King: Bàn tay của Vua (Literal) or Thủ tướng/Tẻ tướng (Historical equivalent). Most Vietnamese fans prefer "Cánh tay của Vua."
Master of Whisperers (Larys Strong): "Trưởng quan Mật báo".
Kingsguard: Thiết vệ (Iron Guard) or Ngự lâm quân.

Gender and the Iron Throne:
Nữ vương: A Queen who rules in her own right (Rhaenyra).
Vương hậu: A Queen who is merely the wife of a King (Helaena).
Thái hậu: The Queen Mother / Dowager (Alicent).

Addressing Dragons:
Characters shouldn't just refer to dragons as "it" (nó).
Dragonriders should refer to their dragons with a sense of partnership or even reverence, using "ngươi" (a form of 'you' used for close subordinates or respected non-humans) rather than the derogatory nó.

Prophecy and "A Song of Ice and Fire":
The Dream: "Bản tình ca của Băng và Lửa."
The Promised Prince: "Vị hoàng tử được hồi báo."

The Concept of "The Realm":
Use "Giang sơn" (Rivers and Mountains) or "Thiên hạ" (All under Heaven).

Key Insults:
Usurper: Kẻ tiếm quyền / Kẻ cướp ngôi.
Pretender: Kẻ mạo danh / Kẻ tự xưng.
Traitor: Kẻ phản nghịch / Nghịch tặc.
Kinslayer: Kẻ sát nhân cốt nhục / Kẻ giết người thân.
Whore / Harlot: Điếm / Ả lăng loàn.
The Realm’s Delight: Niềm vui của Vương quốc / Viên ngọc của Giang sơn.

Crucial Verbs:
To Claim a Dragon: Thu phục rồng / Cưỡi rồng.
To Bend the Knee: Quỳ gối / Quy phục.
Treason: Đại tội phản quốc.
The Succession: Sự kế vị.
