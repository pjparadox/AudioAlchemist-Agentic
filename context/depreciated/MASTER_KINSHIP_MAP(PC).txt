TEAM BLACK (Rhaenyra):

Rhaenyra: Rightful Queen (Nữ vương). Wife/Niece of Daemon (Thiếp).

Daemon: King Consort (Hoàng thân/Phu quân). Uncle to Rhaenyra's children (Thúc thúc).

Jacaerys (Jace): Heir. "Đại huynh" to his brothers.

Corlys (Sea Snake): Lãnh chúa / Đại nhân.

Rhaenys: "Vị Nữ hoàng chưa từng lên ngôi" (The Queen Who Never Was).

Addam/Alyn of Hull: Bastard sons of Corlys (Long chủng).

TEAM GREEN (Aegon):

Aegon II: The King (Bệ hạ).

Alicent: Queen Dowager (Thái hậu). Mother to Aegon, Helaena, Aemond.

Aemond: Second son. Uses "Ta" (arrogant). Must use "Bệ hạ/Đại huynh" for Aegon in public.

Helaena: Queen Consort (Vương hậu). Dreamer/Seer.

Otto Hightower: Hand (Cánh tay của Vua). Father of Alicent. Uses "Ngươi" to scold Aegon.

Criston Cole: Hand/Commander. Alicent's secret lover (Chàng/Thiếp in private).

DETAILED CHARACTER & KINSHIP INFO

THE BLACK FACTION (TEAM RHAENYRA) Based in Dragonstone.

Rhaenyra Targaryen
Role: The central protagonist, rightful Queen.
Translation Note: She is the "Nữ vương." In private with Daemon, she would use "Thiếp" (wife) while he uses "Chàng."

Daemon Targaryen
Role: The King Consort and Commander.
Translation Note: To the public, he is "Hoàng thân." To Rhaenyra, he is "Phu quân."

Jacaerys (Jace) Velaryon
Role: Eldest son of Rhaenyra and heir to the Throne.
Translation Note: As the eldest brother, he is "Đại huynh" to his siblings.

Corlys Velaryon ("The Sea Snake" - Rắn Biển)
Role: Lord of Driftmark and Hand of the Queen.
Translation Note: Addressed as "Lãnh chúa" (Lord) or "Đại nhân."

Rhaenys Targaryen ("The Queen Who Never Was" - Vị Nữ hoàng chưa từng lên ngôi)
Role: Dragonrider of Meleys.
Translation Note: As a royal woman of the older generation, she is a "Công chúa" or "Vương phi."

Baela Targaryen
Role: Eldest daughter of Daemon and Laena. Dragonrider of Moondancer.

Rhaena Targaryen
Role: Younger daughter of Daemon and Laena.

Mysaria ("The White Worm" - Sâu Trắng)
Role: Information broker and advisor to Rhaenyra.

THE GREEN FACTION (TEAM AEGON) Based in King’s Landing.

Aegon II Targaryen
Role: The crowned King.
Translation Note: Addressed as "Bệ hạ" (Your Majesty).

Alicent Hightower
Role: Queen Dowager.
Translation Note: Addressed as "Thái hậu" (Queen Mother/Dowager).

Aemond Targaryen
Role: The second son and protector of the realm.
Translation Note: He must address Aegon with respect as "Đại huynh" or "Bệ hạ."

Helaena Targaryen
Role: Queen Consort.
Translation Note: She is "Vương hậu."

Otto Hightower
Role: Former Hand of the King.

Criston Cole
Role: Lord Commander of the Kingsguard and Hand of the King.

Larys Strong ("The Clubfoot" - Larys Chân Thọt)
Role: Lord of Harrenhal and Master of Whisperers.

Gwayne Hightower (Introduced Season 2)
Role: Knight of the Hightower. Son of Otto.

Tyland Lannister
Role: Master of Coin.

THE DRAGONSEEDS
Addam of Hull & Alyn of Hull: Bastard sons of Corlys.
Hugh Hammer: Blacksmith, claims Vermithor.
Ulf White: Tavern storyteller, claims Silverwing.
