# EVIDENCE LOG: The Klingenberg Case

## Audio Tapes
*   **Item:** 42 Cassette Tapes.
*   **Content:** Recordings of the 67 exorcism sessions.
*   **Key Segments:**
    *   "Demons arguing" (Voices overlapping).
    *   Anneliese speaking in deep, guttural voices ("The Demon Voice").
    *   Dialogues with "Hitler" and "Nero".
*   **Status:** Excerpts played in court. Full transcripts exist in German (not currently in archive).

## Letters
*   **Item:** Letters from Anneliese to Father Alt (1974-1975).
*   **Content:** Descriptions of her "torment", "smell of burning", "faces".
*   **Quote:** "I am nothing; everything about me is vanity. What should I do? I have to improve. You pray for me."

## Medical Reports
*   **Item:** EEG Scans (1969-1973).
*   **Finding:** Consistent with Temporal Lobe Epilepsy (focal seizures).
*   **Item:** Autopsy Report (July 1976).
*   **Finding:** Cause of death: Starvation. Knees broken. Pneumonia present.

## Legal Documents
*   **Item:** Indictment (Aschaffenburg Prosecutor).
*   **Charge:** Negligent Homicide.
*   **Item:** Verdict (April 1978).
*   **Ruling:** Guilty.

## Photographs
*   **Item:** Photos of Anneliese during the exorcism (Spring 1976).
*   **Description:** Show extreme emaciation, bruises on face (from "genuflecting" or hitting floor), black eyes.
*   **Contrast:** Often compared with photos of her as a healthy university student in 1973.
