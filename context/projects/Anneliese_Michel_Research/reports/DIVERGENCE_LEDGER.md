# DIVERGENCE LEDGER: Anneliese Michel vs. Emily Rose

## 1. Structural Shifts

| Element | Real Case (Anneliese Michel) | Film (The Exorcism of Emily Rose) | Narrative Function of Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Setting** | 1970s Bavaria, West Germany. Rural, Catholic, Pre-Modern vibes. | Modern Day (2005) American City. Snowy, urban, secular. | Makes the horror immediate/relatable to US audience; removes "foreignness". |
| **The Defendant** | Two Priests (Renz & Alt) + Two Parents. | Single Priest (Father Moore). | Focuses the conflict on a single hero/martyr figure. Simplifies liability. |
| **The Lawyer** | Erich Schmidt-Leichner (Famous Nazi-hunter/defender). Male, older. | Erin Bruner (Laura Linney). Female, agnostic, career-driven. | Creates a "Believer vs. Skeptic" arc. Erin "converts" to open-mindedness; Schmidt-Leichner was just doing a job. |
| **The Prosecution** | Negligent Homicide (Duty of Care). | Negligent Homicide. | Kept identical. |

## 2. The Horror Elements

| Horror Motif | Real Case | Film |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Eating** | Ate spiders, flies, coal. Drank urine from floor. | Ate bugs (cockroaches). |
| **The Body** | Broken knees, black eyes, 30kg skeleton. | Contorted poses, but actress (Jennifer Carpenter) remains physically intact. |
| **The Voices** | 6 Demons: Lucifer, Cain, Judas, Nero, Hitler, Fleischmann. | 6 Demons: Lucifer, Cain, Judas, Nero, Legion, Belial. (Hitler removed). |
| **The 3:00 AM** | No specific 3 AM motif in records. | "The Witching Hour" is a central scare mechanic. |

## 3. Adaptation Opportunities (Bridging the Gap)
1.  **The "Hitler" Problem:** The film removed Hitler to avoid absurdity. A novel *must* address it. Is it Anneliese's subconscious manifesting the ultimate German evil?
2.  **The Tape Realism:** The film's tapes are scary but clear. The real tapes are chaotic, guttural, and often boring (hours of prayer). A novel can capture the *monotony* of the horror.
3.  **The "Fleischmann" Character:** The disgraced Frankish priest demon is unique to the real case. He adds local color (a demon from *their* specific history) that the generic "Legion" lacks.
4.  **The Parents:** The film treats the parents as background. In reality, they were co-defendants. Their guilt/fanaticism is a rich vein for drama.
