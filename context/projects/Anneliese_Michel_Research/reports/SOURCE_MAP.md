# SOURCE MAP: The Anneliese Michel Case

## 1. Reliability Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Primary / Near-Primary (The "Gold" Layer)
*   **Court Verdict (Urteil):** *Landgericht Aschaffenburg, Urteil vom 21.04.1978*. (Not currently in database, but definitive source).
*   **Autopsy Report (Obduktionsbericht):** Dr. Kroner & Dr. Schleip. (Summary confirmed: Malnutrition/Dehydration).
*   **The Exorcism Tapes (Original-Tonbänder):** 42 cassettes recorded by Renz/Alt.
    *   *Reliability:* High for *what was said*, but subject to interpretation (possession vs. psychosis).
*   **Contemporary Press (1976-1978):**
    *   *The Washington Post* ("Cries of a Woman Possessed", Apr 21, 1978).
    *   *Time Magazine* ("A Phenomenon of Fear", Sept 6, 1976).
    *   *The New York Times* ("2 Priests Charged", July 14, 1977).
    *   *Reliability:* High for public mood and trial facts; Med-High for quoting witnesses; Low for deep medical nuance.

### Tier 2: Academic & Investigative Reconstructions
*   **Felicitas D. Goodman, *The Exorcism of Anneliese Michel* (1981):**
    *   *Contribution:* Goodman (anthropologist) had access to court records and tapes.
    *   *Bias:* Argues for a "Religious Altered State of Consciousness" rather than pure pathology.
    *   *Utility:* Best source for detailed English translations of the tapes.
*   **Uwe Wolff, *Der Teufel ist in mir* (2006):**
    *   *Contribution:* German biography.
    *   *Utility:* Cultural context of 1970s German Catholicism.

### Tier 3: Popular & Media Retellings (Use with Caution)
*   **Wikipedia / Web Articles:** Good for dates, bad for nuance.
*   **The Exorcism of Emily Rose (Film):** *FICTION.* Useful only for the "Divergence Ledger".
*   **Ghost/Paranormal Sites:** *UNRELIABLE.* Often invent "demonic" details not in the transcripts.
