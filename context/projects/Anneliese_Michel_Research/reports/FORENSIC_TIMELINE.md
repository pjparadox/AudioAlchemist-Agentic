# FORENSIC TIMELINE: The Anneliese Michel Case

## 1. Onset & Medical Phase (1968-1973)
*   **1968 (Age 16):**
    *   **Event:** First "blackout" seizure at school. Anneliese bites her tongue.
    *   **Medical:** Diagnosed with "Grand Mal" epilepsy (Temporal Lobe Epilepsy).
    *   **Treatment:** No immediate medication? (Conflicting reports).
*   **1969:**
    *   **Event:** Second seizure.
    *   **Medical:** Dr. Lüthy (Neurologist) prescribes *Zentropil*.
*   **1970 (June):**
    *   **Event:** Third seizure. Anneliese stays at a psychiatric clinic in Mittelberg.
    *   **Symptoms:** Claims to see "devil faces" (Fratzen) during prayer. Hearing "knocking" sounds.
    *   **Medical:** Prescribed *Aolept* (Periciazine) - a phenothiazine neuroleptic used for schizophrenia/psychosis.
*   **1973 (November):**
    *   **Event:** Anneliese becomes "intolerant" of sacred objects.
    *   **Medical:** Dr. Lüthy prescribes *Tegretol* (Carbamazepine) - a mood stabilizer and anti-convulsant.
    *   **Note:** By this point, she has been on anti-convulsants for 5 years with no cessation of "voices".

## 2. Religious Escalation (1974-1975)
*   **1974 (San Damiano Pilgrimage):**
    *   **Event:** Anneliese travels to San Damiano, Italy with a group.
    *   **Observation:** She cannot enter the shrine. She refuses to drink the holy water. Claims the ground "burns" her feet.
    *   **Witness:** Thea Hein (Tour guide) reports this to the family. This is the "Pivot Point" where the explanation shifts from Medical to Spiritual.
*   **1974 (Late):**
    *   **Event:** Father Ernst Alt begins counseling Anneliese. He observes she doesn't "look" epileptic.
    *   **Action:** Alt writes to Bishop Stangl for permission to exorcise. Stangl refuses, urging more medical care.
*   **1975 (July):**
    *   **Event:** Anneliese's condition worsens. She sleeps on the stone floor to "atone". eats insects.
    *   **Action:** Father Alt petitions Bishop Stangl again.

## 3. The Exorcism Phase (Sept 1975 - July 1976)
*   **1975, Sept 24:**
    *   **Event:** **SESSION 1.** Bishop Stangl grants permission for the *Rituale Romanum*. Father Arnold Renz (Salvatorian) assigned as exorcist.
    *   **Note:** Stangl orders "Strict Secrecy" (*Stillschweigen*).
*   **1975, Late:**
    *   **Pattern:** Sessions occur 1-2 times per week. Anneliese often has to be physically restrained by her father and fiance (Peter).
    *   **Medical:** She continues to take Tegretol sporadically.
*   **1976, May:**
    *   **Event:** Anneliese predicts her death/deliverance for July 1st.
    *   **Physical:** She weighs under 40kg. Knees are ruptured from compulsive genuflection (600/day reported).
*   **1976, June 30 (The Final Night):**
    *   **Event:** Last Exorcism.
    *   **Condition:** Anneliese has pneumonia and a high fever. She cannot stand.
    *   **Last Words:** "Beg for absolution" (to Renz). "Mother, I'm afraid" (to Anna).
    *   **Weight:** 30-31 kg (68 lbs).

## 4. Death & Trial (1976-1978)
*   **1976, July 1:**
    *   **Event:** Anneliese is found dead in bed.
    *   **Action:** Father Alt informs the prosecutor.
*   **1978, March-April:**
    *   **Trial:** *Landgericht Aschaffenburg*.
    *   **Defense:** "Religious Act protected by Constitution."
    *   **Prosecution:** "Negligent Homicide - death was preventable by forced feeding."
    *   **Verdict:** Guilty. 6 months suspended.

## 5. Critical Divergence Points (The "What Ifs")
1.  **The Pilgrimage (1974):** If the tour guide hadn't framed her anxiety as "demonic aversion", would the family have sought a new psychiatrist?
2.  **The Bishop's Letter (Sept 1975):** If Stangl had required a doctor's presence (as per modern rules), Renz would have been forced to hospitalize her when she hit 40kg.
3.  **The Last Week (June 1976):** Dr. Roth (General Practitioner) saw Anneliese but did not order involuntary commitment. Why? (He later claimed he thought she was under "specialist care" of the exorcists).
