# RITUAL MECHANICS: 1970s Context

## 1. Institutional Flowchart: Getting Permission

1.  **The Application (Petitio):**
    *   Family/Parish Priest (Father Alt) approaches the Bishop (Stangl).
    *   Must provide evidence of *Infestatio* (Obsession/Possession).
    *   *Requirement:* Medical clearance. (This was the point of failure/ambiguity in the Klingenberg case).
2.  **The Discernment (Discernimento):**
    *   Bishop reviews the evidence.
    *   Criteria from *Rituale Romanum* (1614) Titulus XII:
        *   *Unknown Languages:* Speaking/Understanding languages the subject never learned.
        *   *Hidden Things:* Revealing distant or hidden events.
        *   *Supernatural Strength:* Displaying power beyond physical age/condition.
        *   *Aversion to the Sacred:* Violent reaction to relics, holy water, or the Eucharist.
3.  **The Authorization (Mandatum):**
    *   If criteria are met, Bishop issues the *Mandatum*.
    *   In Michel's case: Permission for *Exorcismus Maior* (Major Exorcism) granted in Sept 1975.
    *   *Condition:* "Sub secreto" (Under secrecy).

## 2. The Ritual: Rituale Romanum (1614)

### The Setup
*   **Location:** Usually a church or chapel. In Michel's case, her bedroom (a deviation, but permitted for "secrecy").
*   **Attire:** Priest wears purple stole (penitence/authority) over cassock/surplice.
*   **Items:** Crucifix, Holy Water, Relics (St. San Damiano medal used).

### The Liturgy (Observable Actions)
1.  **Litany of Saints:** Priest calls upon saints. (Michel often screamed in pain here).
2.  **Psalm 53:** "God, save me by your name..."
3.  **The Commands (Imperatives):**
    *   *Praecipio tibi:* "I command you."
    *   *Exi ab ea:* "Depart from her."
4.  **The Interrogations:**
    *   "Dic mihi nomen tuum" (Tell me your name).
    *   "Quando exies?" (When will you leave?).
    *   *Note:* The demon is only supposed to answer these two questions. Any other conversation is discouraged as a trap of the devil (but Renz engaged in extensive theological debates with the "demons").
5.  **The Physicality:**
    *   Sprinkling holy water (Subject burns/recoils).
    *   Laying on of hands (Subject fights/biting).
    *   Breathing (Insufflation) on the face (Symbol of the Holy Spirit).

## 3. Historical Friction: 1970s vs. Vatican II
*   **The Conflict:** Vatican II (1962-1965) had modernized the Church, moving away from "medieval superstitions" like demons.
*   **The Reactionaries:** Fathers Alt and Renz (and the Michel family) were traditionalists who felt the modern church had lost its way.
*   **The Stakes:** Proving the possession was real wasn't just about saving Anneliese; it was about proving the *existence of the Devil* to a skeptical, modernizing Church. This motivation likely fueled the decision to record the tapes.
