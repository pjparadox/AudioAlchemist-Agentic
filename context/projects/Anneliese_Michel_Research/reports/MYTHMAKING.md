# MYTHMAKING: From Case to Legend

## 1. The Grave Pilgrimage
*   **Location:** Klingenberg Cemetery.
*   **The Pilgrims:** Small groups of traditionalist Catholics who view Anneliese not as a victim of negligence, but as a "Saint of the Counter-Reformation."
*   **The Narrative:** They believe she accepted the possession to "atone for the sins of the modern Church" (specifically Vatican II).
*   **The Paradox:** The Church officially considers it a tragic failure (and suppressed the rite), yet the "folk religion" honors her as a martyr.

## 2. The Fire of 2013
*   **Event:** On June 6, 2013, the sawmill owned by the Michel family (managed by Anneliese's sister) burned down.
*   **Cause:** Official police report: Arson.
*   **The Myth:** Local rumors immediately attributed it to the "Curse of Klingenberg" or demonic residue, showing how the town remains haunted by the 1976 event.

## 3. Motif Genealogy (Evolution of the Story)

| Motif | Reality (Source) | Legend (Mutation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incorruptibility** | Body exhumed in 1978; showed normal decay. | Rumors persist that she was "perfectly preserved" but the authorities lied to cover it up. |
| **The "Sign"** | Stigmata claims were vague redness. | Retold as full bleeding wounds matching Christ's. |
| **The Demons** | 6 specific names (including Hitler). | Retold as "Legion" or generic ancient evils to strip the specific German historical context. |

## 4. Institutional Incentives
*   **The Church:** Needs to distance itself from the "Medieval" image. Used the case to mandate medical presence in future exorcisms.
*   **The Skeptics:** Use the case as the ultimate proof that "Religion Kills."
*   **The Media:** *Emily Rose* revitalized interest, transforming a tragic epileptic girl into a "Cool Monster" for entertainment.
