# TIMELINE: The Anneliese Michel Case

## Phase 1: Onset (1952 - 1973)
*   **1952, Sep 21:** Anneliese Michel is born in Leiblfing, Bavaria.
*   **1968:** Anneliese suffers her first "grand mal" seizure at age 16. Diagnosed with Temporal Lobe Epilepsy.
*   **1969:** Second severe seizure. Begins taking medication (Zentropil).
*   **1970:** Third seizure. Prescribed *Aolept* (Periciazine). Reports seeing "fritzen" (grimacing faces) while praying.
*   **1973:** Graduates high school (Gymnasium) and enters University of Würzburg (Pedagogy/Theology).
*   **1973, Nov:** Begins treatment with *Tegretol* (Carbamazepine).

## Phase 2: Escalation (1973 - 1975)
*   **1973:** Anneliese complains of knocking sounds in her room. Medical exams find no cause.
*   **1974:** Anneliese participates in a pilgrimage to San Damiano, Italy. She is unable to enter the shrine, refuses to drink holy water, and claims the soil "burns".
*   **1974, Late:** Father Ernst Alt begins counseling Anneliese. He concludes she is not epileptic but possessed.
*   **1975, July:** Father Alt requests permission for a "Major Exorcism" from Bishop Josef Stangl.
*   **1975, Sept:** Bishop Stangl grants permission to Father Arnold Renz to perform the *Rituale Romanum* under strict secrecy.

## Phase 3: The Exorcism (Sept 1975 - July 1976)
*   **1975, Sept 24:** First exorcism session performed.
*   **1975 - 1976:** 67 sessions performed over 10 months. Anneliese's behavior becomes erratic: eating spiders/coal, drinking urine, screaming for hours, performing hundreds of genuflections daily (rupturing knee ligaments).
*   **1976, May:** Anneliese predicts her "deliverance" will occur in July.
*   **1976, June 30:** Final exorcism session. Anneliese is weak, weighing 30kg. Her last words to her mother: "Mother, I'm afraid." To the priests: "Beg for absolution."
*   **1976, July 1:** Anneliese dies in her sleep.

## Phase 4: The Aftermath (1976 - 1978)
*   **1976, July:** Investigation begins. Parents and priests charged with negligent homicide.
*   **1978, Feb 25:** Body exhumed. Found to be naturally decayed (refuting claims of incorruptibility).
*   **1978, Mar 30:** Trial begins in Aschaffenburg.
*   **1978, Apr:** Verdict delivered. All defendants found guilty. Sentenced to 6 months (suspended).
