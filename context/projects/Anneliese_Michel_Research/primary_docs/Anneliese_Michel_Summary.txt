SUMMARY:
Anna Elisabeth "Anneliese" Michel (21 September 1952 – 1 July 1976) was a German woman who underwent 67 Catholic exorcism rites during the year before her death. She died of malnutrition, for which her parents and the priest who performed the exorcism were convicted of negligent homicide.

KEY ENTITIES:
- Anneliese Michel (Subject)
- Josef & Anna Michel (Parents)
- Father Ernst Alt (Applicant Priest)
- Father Arnold Renz (Exorcist)
- Bishop Josef Stangl (Authority)
- Dr. Lüthy & Dr. Schleip (Medical Doctors - referenced in general context)
- Erich Schmidt-Leichner (Defense Attorney)

MEDICAL HISTORY:
- 1968: First seizure (Grand Mal). Diagnosed with Temporal Lobe Epilepsy.
- Treatment: Dilantin, Aolept (Periciazine), Tegretol (Carbamazepine).
- Symptoms: "Devil faces", voices saying she was "damned", intolerance to sacred objects (crucifix, holy water at San Damiano).

THE EXORCISM:
- Authorization: Bishop Stangl granted permission for "Rituale Romanum" in 1975 to Father Renz.
- Duration: 10 months (Sept 1975 - July 1976).
- Frequency: 1-2 sessions/week, up to 4 hours.
- Total Sessions: 67.
- Demons Identified: Lucifer, Cain, Judas Iscariot, Nero, Hitler, Fleischmann (a disgraced Frankish priest).

DEATH:
- Date: July 1, 1976.
- Cause: Malnutrition and dehydration.
- Weight: 30 kg (66 lb).
- Physical state: Broken knees (genuflection), pneumonia.

TRIAL (The Klingenberg Case):
- Charge: Negligent Homicide (Fahrlässige Tötung).
- Defendants: Parents, Alt, Renz.
- Verdict: Guilty.
- Sentence: 6 months suspended (probation), reduced to 3 years probation.
- Defense Argument: Protected by religious freedom (German Constitution).
- Prosecution Argument: Death prevented by forced feeding/doctor visit as late as 1 week prior.

POST-MORTEM:
- Exhumation: Feb 25, 1978. Body showed normal decay (refuting "incorruptible" claims).
