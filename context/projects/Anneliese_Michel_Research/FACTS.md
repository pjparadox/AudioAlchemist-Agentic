# FACTS: The Klingenberg Case (Anneliese Michel)

## 1. Subject Identity
*   **Name:** Anna Elisabeth "Anneliese" Michel
*   **DOB:** September 21, 1952
*   **DOD:** July 1, 1976 (Age 23)
*   **Location:** Klingenberg am Main, Bavaria, West Germany
*   **Cause of Death:** Malnutrition and Dehydration (Autopsy Findings)
*   **Weight at Death:** 30 kg (66 lbs)

## 2. Medical History
*   **Diagnosis:** Temporal Lobe Epilepsy (Grand Mal seizures).
*   **Secondary Diagnosis:** Depressive neurosis, potential schizophrenic patterns (visual/auditory hallucinations).
*   **Medications:**
    *   *Zentropil* (Anti-convulsant) - Early treatment.
    *   *Aolept* (Periciazine) - Phenothiazine neuroleptic.
    *   *Tegretol* (Carbamazepine) - Mood stabilizer/Anti-epileptic.
*   **Medical Timeline:** Treatment began in 1968 after first seizure. Continued until 1975/1976 when she refused further medical intervention.

## 3. The Exorcism (The Rites)
*   **Authorization:** Granted by Bishop Josef Stangl (Würzburg) in September 1975.
*   **Rite Used:** *Rituale Romanum* (1614).
*   **Exorcists:** Father Arnold Renz (Salvatorian) and Father Ernst Alt.
*   **Sessions:** 67 recorded sessions over 10 months.
*   **Audio Evidence:** 42 tapes were recorded. (Often referred to as "The Klingenberg Tapes").

## 4. The "Demons"
According to the session transcripts/tapes, the entities identified themselves as:
1.  **Lucifer**
2.  **Judas Iscariot**
3.  **Cain**
4.  **Nero**
5.  **Adolf Hitler**
6.  **Fleischmann** (A disgraced Frankish priest from the 16th century).

## 5. The Trial (1978)
*   **Charge:** *Fahrlässige Tötung* (Negligent Homicide).
*   **Defendants:**
    *   Josef Michel (Father)
    *   Anna Michel (Mother)
    *   Father Arnold Renz
    *   Father Ernst Alt
*   **Defense:** Erich Schmidt-Leichner. Argued religious freedom and the "reality" of possession.
*   **Prosecution:** Argued medical negligence; death was preventable by forced feeding up to one week prior.
*   **Verdict:** All four defendants found guilty.
*   **Sentence:** 6 months imprisonment, suspended for 3 years probation.
*   **Significance:** Resulted in the German Bishops' Conference tightening rules on exorcism and eventually suppressing the *Rituale Romanum* in favor of a reformed rite.
