# PROFILES: Key Players

## Anneliese Michel (Subject)
*   **Role:** The Possessed / Victim.
*   **Profile:** Deeply religious, anxious, prone to hyper-scrupulosity. Intelligent (University student).
*   **Psychological Note:** Diagnosed with epilepsy. Doctors suggested "doctrinal neurosis" - her psychosis adopted the religious framework of her upbringing.
*   **Key Trait:** Submission to suffering as "atonement" for the sins of others (wayward priests, drug addicts).

## Father Arnold Renz (The Exorcist)
*   **Role:** Lead Exorcist.
*   **Affiliation:** Salvatorian Order.
*   **Personality:** Ascetic, traditionalist. Believed firmly in the literal reality of demons.
*   **Action:** Conducted the 67 sessions. Recorded the tapes.
*   **Outcome:** Convicted of negligent homicide.

## Father Ernst Alt (The Applicant)
*   **Role:** Pastor / Spiritual Director.
*   **Personality:** Convinced that medication was failing Anneliese. Saw her aversion to sacred objects as the definitive diagnostic sign of possession (*infestatio*).
*   **Action:** Petitioned Bishop Stangl for the rites.
*   **Outcome:** Convicted of negligent homicide.

## Bishop Josef Stangl (The Authority)
*   **Role:** Bishop of Würzburg.
*   **Action:** Approved the exorcism but ordered "strict secrecy". Did not visit Anneliese personally during the rites.
*   **Outcome:** Investigated but not charged (or charges dropped due to lack of direct oversight/health). Died in 1979.

## Josef and Anna Michel (The Parents)
*   **Role:** Caregivers.
*   **Personality:** Devout, old-world Catholics. Trusted the priests over the doctors once the "spiritual" diagnosis was made.
*   **Action:** Stopped medical consultation. Facilitated the rites in their home.
*   **Outcome:** Convicted but received no sentence (the court ruled they had "suffered enough" by losing their daughter).

## The "Demons" (Personality Profiles from Tapes)
*   **Lucifer:** Arrogant, calm, claims total ownership.
*   **Judas:** Despairing, suicidal ideation.
*   **Nero:** Aggressive, violent.
*   **Cain:** Sullen, rejected.
*   **Hitler:** Speaks with distinct Franconian accent (mocked by skeptics as Anneliese's natural dialect), shouts Nazi slogans.
*   **Fleischmann:** Lewd, vulgar, tormented by his priestly vows.
