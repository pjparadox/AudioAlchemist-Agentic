# EXPANSION OUTLINE: THE 70K PROTOCOL
**Goal:** Expand ~25k script to ~75k Novel.
**Ratio:** 3.0x Expansion.

## ACT 1: THE ANCHOR & THE SALESMAN (Target: 20,000 Words)

### Chapter 1: The Grave (Expansion: 3k words)
*   **Script:** Wade digs up Logan. TVA arrives. Fight.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Cold:* 500 words on the North Dakota winter (King style).
    *   *The Dig:* 1000 words on the physical labor, the blisters, the healing factor fighting the cold.
    *   *The Monologue:* Extended internal rant about the Disney/Fox merger as a corporate war.
    *   *The Fight:* 1500 words of anatomical violence (Blackmore). Every broken bone is cataloged.

### Chapter 2: The Interview (Expansion: 3k words)
*   **Script:** 2018 Flashback. Happy Hogan rejection.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Commute:* Wade getting to Stark Tower. The smell of the lobby. The intimidation.
    *   *Happy's Office:* Deep description of the Avengers memorabilia. Wade's feeling of inferiority.
    *   *The Rejection:* Slow down the dialogue. Analyze every micro-expression on Happy's face (Prince style).

### Chapter 3: The Salesman (Expansion: 4k words)
*   **Script:** Wade selling cars.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Grind:* Wade's daily routine. The alarm clock. The toupee application ritual (horror style).
    *   *The Lot:* The sociology of used car sales. The specific smell of the Kia Carnival.
    *   *The Test Drive:* Extended scene with the Chipman family. Wade's internal desire to kill them vs. his need for commission.

### Chapter 4: The Locker Room & Peter (Expansion: 3k words)
*   **Script:** Peter supports Wade.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *Peter's World:* POV shift? or deep observation of Peter. Why is he so happy?
    *   *The Suit:* The tragedy of the suit in the locker. It's a corpse.

### Chapter 5: The Birthday Party (Expansion: 4k words)
*   **Script:** Surprise party. Breakup talk with Vanessa.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Guests:* Mini-biographies of everyone in the room. What is Buck doing? What is Shatterstar's life like?
    *   *Vanessa:* The agony of the "Friend Zone." The "Dermot" revelation treated as a physical wound.

### Chapter 6: The Abduction (Expansion: 3k words)
*   **Script:** TVA takes Wade. Paradox offer.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The TVA:* Description of the Retro-Future aesthetic (Dinniman style - "The texture of the bureaucracy").
    *   *The Offer:* Wade watching the monitors. The "Marvel Jesus" delusion taking root.

## ACT 2: THE VOID (Target: 30,000 Words)

### Chapter 7: The Search for Logan (Expansion: 4k words)
*   **Script:** Montage of Wolverines. The Bar.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Montage:* Expand *each* variant encounter into a full scene. The Short King. The Patch. The Cavillrine.
    *   *The Bar:* The smell of the whiskey. The despair of this specific Logan.

### Chapter 8: Arrival in the Void (Expansion: 3k words)
*   **Script:** Pruned. Fight. Johnny Storm.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Fall:* The sensation of being pruned.
    *   *The Fight:* Wade vs. Logan Round 1. Visceral, pointless violence.
    *   *Johnny:* The hope. The realization.

### Chapter 9: The Fortress of Bone (Expansion: 4k words)
*   **Script:** Capture. Cassandra Nova. Johnny's Death.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Skull:* King-style atmospheric horror. The acoustics of a dead god's head.
    *   *Cassandra:* Her fingers inside minds. The sensation of telepathy (like wet spiders).
    *   *The Skinning:* Blackmore-style anatomical horror. 1000 words on the sound and look of Johnny's death.

### Chapter 10: The Odyssey (Expansion: 5k words)
*   **Script:** Escape. Driving the Honda.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Landscape:* The geography of the Void. Ruined landmarks.
    *   *The Car Fight:* The "Gore-gasm." Two immortals destroying each other in a confined space. 2000 words of blood and upholstery.

### Chapter 11: The Resistance (Expansion: 5k words)
*   **Script:** Meeting Blade, Gambit, Elektra, X-23.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Bunker:* Detailed setting.
    *   *The Legends:* Give Blade a monologue. Describe Gambit's cooking. Deep dive into their "Forgotten" status (Meta-commentary on the Fox merger).
    *   *The Campfire:* Laura and Logan. The emotional core.

### Chapter 12: The Assault (Expansion: 5k words)
*   **Script:** Attacking the base. Juggernaut.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Plan:* Dinniman-style "Raid Planning."
    *   *The Action:* Split POV? Or Wade observing the chaos. Gambit's card tricks described in particle-physics detail.

## ACT 3: THE CLIMAX (Target: 25,000 Words)

### Chapter 13: The Corps (Expansion: 8k words)
*   **Script:** 100 Deadpools.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Army:* Describe the variants.
    *   *The Fight:* "Like a Prayer." Choreography. Every kill is unique. The "Long Take" translated to prose.
    *   *Peter's Arrival:* The divine intervention.

### Chapter 14: The Ripper (Expansion: 5k words)
*   **Script:** Paradox. The Machine. The Sacrifice.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *The Machine:* How it works. The sound. The heat.
    *   *The Pain:* The unmaking of the self. The connection between Wade and Logan.

### Chapter 15: The Aftermath (Expansion: 4k words)
*   **Script:** Epilogue.
*   **Novelization:**
    *   *Shawarma:* The taste of victory.
    *   *Reunion:* The party again. The feeling of belonging.
    *   *Final Monologue:* Wade's thesis statement on "Mattering."
