# STYLE_GUIDE_FUSION_V2.md

## The "Chimichanga Blend" (Strict Ratio)

### 1. Matt Dinniman (50%) - The Core Voice
*   **Role:** The System Architect / The Chaotic Narrator.
*   **Key Traits:**
    *   **System Awareness:** The world is a game/construct. Wade knows the rules (TVA, Copyright, Box Office) and actively exploits or critiques them.
    *   **Hyper-Specific Absurdity:** Descriptions should veer into the bizarrely specific. (e.g., "The explosion smelled like burnt hair and a geometry teacher's disappointment.")
    *   **Capitalization of Concepts:** Use Capital Letters for game-like terms (e.g., *The Anchor Being*, *The Void*, *The Mouse*).
    *   **Panic/Competence Switch:** Toggle instantly between manic panic and hyper-competence.

### 2. Jun Prince (30%) - The Analyst
*   **Role:** The Modern Cynic.
*   **Key Traits:**
    *   **Social Hierarchy Analysis:** Wade analyzes the power dynamics of every room. Who is the Tank? Who is the NPC?
    *   **Cool Detachment:** A layer of "I'm too cool for this" even when things are going wrong.
    *   **Modern Slang:** Use current internet/cultural vernicular naturally, not forced.

### 3. Stephen King (20%) - The Grounding
*   **Role:** The Atmosphere / The Ghost Writer.
*   **Key Traits:**
    *   **Sensory Anchoring:** Describe the smell, temperature, and texture of the scene before the action starts.
    *   **Brand Names:** Ground the world in reality (e.g., "Skechers Shape-Ups", "Honda Odyssey", "Old Spice").
    *   **Folksy Dread:** Use conversational metaphors for horrific things. "It was the kind of wound that says hello to your ancestors."
    *   **Transitions:** Smooth the jump cuts of the script into flowing narrative memory.

### 4. Keith C. Blackmore (Combat/Violence Override)
*   **Trigger:** ANY physical violence or injury.
*   **Key Traits:**
    *   **Anatomical Precision:** Bone names, muscle groups, arterial sprays.
    *   **The Weight of Meat:** Bodies are heavy. Hits hurt. Focus on the *consequence* of impact.
    *   **Sound:** The wet *thwack*, the dry *crack*, the gurgle.
    *   **No "Cool" Violence:** Violence is messy, exhausting, and gross.

## Formatting Rules
*   **Dialogue:** Use standard novel formatting. Break up long monologues with action beats.
*   **Thought Bubbles:** Use *Italics* for direct internal thought.
*   **Length:** **EXPAND EVERYTHING.** If the script says "Deadpool digs," write 3 paragraphs about the cold, the shovel blisters, and the smell of the dirt.
