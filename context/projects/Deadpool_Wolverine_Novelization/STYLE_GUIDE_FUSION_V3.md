# STYLE_GUIDE_FUSION_V3 (THE 70K MANDATE)

## 1. The Expansion Ratio (2.5x - 3.0x)
*   **Rule:** For every line of script dialogue, generate **three paragraphs** of prose.
*   **Technique:** "The Iceberg Method." The dialogue is the tip; the prose is the submerged mass of thought, sensory detail, and history.

## 2. Stylistic Directives

### A. Matt Dinniman (The System & The Absurd)
*   **Scope:** World-building and Internal Monologue.
*   **Mandate:** Treat the TVA/Void as a broken RPG system. Use capitalized terms (*The Anchor*, *The Void*, *The Admin*).
*   **Example:** "The pain wasn't just physical; it was a debuff icon flashing in the corner of my vision, promising -50% Dexterity for the next six rounds."

### B. Stephen King (The Atmosphere & The Dread)
*   **Scope:** Setting the Scene and Character Backstory.
*   **Mandate:** Ground the absurdity in dirt, brand names, and weather.
*   **Example:** "The wind sounded like a dead man trying to whistle through a hole in his throat. It smelled of ozone and the kind of cheap pine air freshener you buy at a gas station in 1998."

### C. Keith C. Blackmore (The Meat & The Bone)
*   **Scope:** Violence and Injury.
*   **Mandate:** **NO SKIPPING.** If a bone breaks, describe the sound, the heat, the nausea. Violence is heavy, wet, and exhausting.
*   **Example:** "The radius snapped with a sound like a dry branch wrapped in wet leather. The pain was a white spike, hot and jagged."

### D. Jun Prince (The Cynic & The Mask)
*   **Scope:** Social Dynamics and Meta-Commentary.
*   **Mandate:** Analyze the "Scene" as a performance.
*   **Example:** "He was playing the 'Reluctant Hero' card, but he was over-acting. You could see the desperate need for validation in the way he held his chin."

## 3. The "Ghost Writing" Protocol
*   **Invent Details:** If the script says "Wade looks sad," write a page about how the specific shade of beige in the carpet reminds him of a hospital waiting room in 2016.
*   **Expand Time:** Slow down the action. A 10-second fight scene should take 3 pages to read.
