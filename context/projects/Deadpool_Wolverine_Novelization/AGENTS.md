# AGENTS.md: Deadpool & Wolverine Novelization

## Project Scope
Novelization of the *Deadpool & Wolverine* screenplay.
**Target Audience:** Mature, Fanbase, Bestseller Market.

## 1. Style Mandates (The "Chimichanga Blend")
*   **Narrative Voice:** **Jun Prince** (Cynical/Modern) + **Matt Dinniman** (Chaotic/System-Aware).
*   **Action Scenes:** **Keith C. Blackmore** (Visceral/Grim/Impact-Heavy).
*   **Atmosphere/Filler:** **Stephen King** (Conversational/Deep Character/Slow Dread).
*   **POV:** First Person (Wade Wilson). Voiceovers become internal monologue or direct address to the reader ("Constant Reader" -> "Constant Viewer/Nerd").

## 2. Process Rules
1.  **17th Degree Adversarial Loop:** Every segment must pass 17 rounds of internal critique before finalization.
    *   *Note:* Agents must log the "Audit Trail" of these loops.
2.  **No Direct Quotes:** Do not use direct quotes from the style reference texts.
3.  **Embellishment:** Allowed and encouraged. Expand on the "Off Screen" moments.

## 3. Agent Assignments
*   **Varys:** Style Analysis & Fusion Strategy.
*   **Elara:** Primary Drafter (The Writer).
*   **Lyra:** Prose Stylist (The King/Blackmore filter).
*   **Thoth:** The Harsh Critic (Enforces the 17th Loop).
*   **Deadpool (Meta-Agent):** The voice itself.

## 4. Specific Mappings
*   **TVA:** Treated as "The System" (Dinniman style bureaucracy).
*   **The Void:** Treated as a King-esque wasteland (The Langoliers/Dark Tower vibes).
*   **Combat:** Blackmore-esque anatomically precise violence.
