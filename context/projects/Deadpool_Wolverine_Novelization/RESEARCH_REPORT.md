**Subject:** Analysis of Target Authors for Project "Chimichanga"

## Executive Summary
Mercury has completed the deep-dive analysis of the provided samples. The "Chimichanga Blend" requires a precise alchemical mix.

### 1. Matt Dinniman (The Chaos Engine)
*   **Key Trait:** *Systemic Absurdity.*
*   **Emulation:** The protagonist is aware of the "Rules" and hates them. The world is a hostile game board. Use bolding/italics for emphasis on "Game Terms" (or in this case, Movie Tropes).
*   **Voice:** frantic, exasperated, but hyper-competent.

### 2. Stephen King (The Weaver)
*   **Key Trait:** *Grounded Americana.*
*   **Emulation:** Use brand names (Skechers, Kia Carnival). Spend time on the weather. Use the phrase "I tell you what" or similar conversational bridges. Flashbacks should feel like memories, not scenes.
*   **Voice:** Nostalgic, detailed, slightly weary.

### 3. Keith C. Blackmore (The Butcher)
*   **Key Trait:** *Somatic Violence.*
*   **Emulation:** Don't say "he hit him." Say "the fist connected with a meat-slapping thud that vibrated up to the shoulder." Focus on the *consequences* of violence—the fatigue, the mess, the smell.
*   **Voice:** Grim, professional, sensory-focused.

### 4. Jun Prince (The Modernist)
*   **Key Trait:** *Cynical Analysis.*
*   **Emulation:** The narrator analyzes the social/narrative hierarchy. "He was the Tank, I was the DPS, and the TVA were the trash mobs." (Metaphorically).

## Synthesis Strategy
We will open with **King** (The Grave, the atmosphere). We will transition to **Dinniman/Prince** for the monologue (Disney, Fox, The Rights). We will switch to **Blackmore** the moment the shovel hits the dirt and the TVA arrives.
