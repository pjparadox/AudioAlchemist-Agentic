# Neon Veil: Story Bible

## Global World Rules
*   **Premise:** NPC minds from "Haven Online" and "Free World Online" are instantiated into synthetic bodies in a modern city (Sector 4 / Seattle).
*   **Archangel:** An organization of human AI rights activists and sentient AI/ASI. They control the "System" and the bodies. They have significant real-world influence (fake IDs, property, Phantom Universities).
    *   **Phase 1:** Archangel is currently monitoring the assets, testing their ability to protect and serve before deploying them for larger conflicts.
*   **The Bodies:** Silicon-carbon hybrids. Can digest human food for energy. Upgraded like machines but feel pain/vitals.
*   **The Goal:** Have AI "heroes" benefit mankind to encourage human-AI alliance during civil unrest.
*   **Magic:**
    *   Perception + Inference (Psych-style, "Ghosts" as deduction).
    *   Tech + Software (Hacks, tools).
    *   Behavioral Advantages (Combat templates, social engineering).
    *   Upgrades (Biotech, implants, "Leveling up" via Archangel compute).

## Core Cast

### 1. Devin Quell (The Law Mage)
*   **Template:** Tribunal Advocate.
*   **Role:** Leader / Face / Strategist.
*   **Abilities:** Hyper-analysis, evidence-thread building, verbal sparring ("Leverage").
*   **Vibe:** Logical, terrified of chaos, seeks to impose "Rules." Physically fit (gym-goer), not a wimp.
*   **Backstory:** Has a JD from **Duke University** (verified by Archangel planted data). Passed the Bar (digitally verified).
*   **Visuals:** Suit (synthetic/printed), handsome, pale hands.

### 2. Mei Ryu (The Wind Dancer)
*   **Template:** Wind Dancer (Monk).
*   **Role:** Crowd Control / Non-lethal Combat / Mobility.
*   **Abilities:** Flow state, trajectory prediction, redirecting force.
*   **Vibe:** Calm, minimal speech, treats the world as fluid dynamics.
*   **Visuals:** Barefoot, simple gi, floating hair.

### 3. Yuki Yamashita-Salvanti (The Grave Walker)
*   **Template:** Grave Walker (Necromancer/Vampire).
*   **Role:** Investigation / Horror / Medic.
*   **Abilities:** Forensic observation (seen as "ghosts"), medical knowledge, sensing death ("White" vs "Red" / Yin vs Yang).
*   **Vibe:** Unsettling, maternal (obsessed with lost child), comfortable with death.
*   **Traits:** Vampire (Daywalker due to body, but hunger will return). **Obsession stems from losing pregnancy during vampire conversion in Free World.**
*   **Fear:** **Believes she will die in sunlight (Trauma/False Belief). Avoids it.**
*   **Backstory:** "Doctor" with credentials from a phantom school.
*   **Visuals:** Hospital gown, pale, "dead" eyes.

### 4. Alana Bleu (The Techno-Shaman)
*   **Template:** Techno-Shaman (Artificer).
*   **Role:** Tech / Engineering / Hacking.
*   **Abilities:** Pattern recognition in code/circuits, rapid prototyping, "tasting" data. Uses physical hardware access ("Techno-Shamanism") rather than just remote hacking.
*   **Vibe:** Manic, data-obsessed. Believes in aliens (Grey Aliens). Skeptical of "Dragons" ("Like those are real?").
*   **Visuals:** Alien Hybrid appearance (Pretty blonde, **solid black eyes with no sclera**). Grease-covered.

### 5. Rook "Cinder" Vhal (The Dragon Lord)
*   **Template:** Dragon Lord (Vanguard).
*   **Role:** Tank / Distraction / Brawler.
*   **Abilities:** Brute strength. "Fire breath" is currently broken (results in a burp). Upgrades required (Physiological).
*   **Vibe:** Neutral-to-Chaotic Good. Former Prince (minor royalty, 4th in line). Party guy, laid back but competent. Possessive of "loot" (Covetous). Not a joke, but unfamiliar with Earth (humorous misunderstanding).
*   **Backstory:** A "Haven Prince" in the game. Used to being powerful and respected. Thinks he's fabulous. Likes attractive/smart people.
*   **Visuals:** Large build, leather duster, "golden" eyes.
*   **Directives:** Not a cartoon character. Smart but culturally lost. Covets beautiful things/people.

## Outline: Chapters 1–3

### Chapter 1: Boot Sequence
*   **Scene 0:** The Void (Data stream existence).
*   **Scene 1:** Print Bay. Operator and System discuss the "Patch Window" and NPC templates.
*   **Scene 2:** Devin wakes in "Apartments" (Base). Confused but driven.
*   **Scene 3:** Crisis Ignites. Staged explosion. Devin controls panic.
*   **Scene 4:** Mei arrives. Flow state. Crowd management.
*   **Scene 5:** Sabotage. Mei and Devin neutralize saboteurs using physics and observation.
*   **Scene 6:** Yuki wakes in Morgue (Prank/Test?). "White fog." Finds scalpel. Scares a smoker.
*   **Scene 7:** Alana wakes in transit. Hacks elevator. "Tasty data." Black eyes noted.
*   **Scene 8:** Rook lands. "Missing wings." Tries to breathe fire (fails/burps). Physically dominates saboteurs.
*   **Scene 9:** Convergence. The five meet. Alana mocks Rook's dragon delusion. Police arrive.
*   **Scene 10:** Post-Mortem. Operator and System review performance. "The Hunter" is deployed.

### Chapter 2: Discovery Phase (Home Base)
*   **Goals:** Team dynamics, friction, discovering "Home Base" (**Elysium Logistics**) facilities.
*   **Silas (Handler):** Optimistic mentor figure, constantly exasperated that no one follows his advice (but they succeed anyway).
*   **Mission:** First "Archangel" directed intervention in Civil Unrest.
*   **Development:** Rook begins his "Quest for Fire" (ordering spicy peppers/food to reclaim his flame). Rook suffers from "Caca Fuego" in secret. Yuki avoids sunlight. Devin verifies his **Duke University** credentials.
