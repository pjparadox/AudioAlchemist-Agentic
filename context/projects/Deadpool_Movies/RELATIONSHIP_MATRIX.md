# RELATIONSHIP_MATRIX.md: Narrative Dynamics

## WADE ↔ VANESSA
*   **Dynamic:** Soulmates. The only thing Wade takes seriously.
*   **Conflict:** Wade feels unworthy; Vanessa wants him to believe in himself.

## WADE ↔ WOLVERINE (THE WORST)
*   **Start:** Hostile. Wade wants a savior; Logan wants to die.
*   **Middle:** Violent co-dependency. "Two idiots in a Honda."
*   **End:** Brothers in arms. Mutual respect earned through blood.

## WADE ↔ MR. PARADOX
*   **Dynamic:** Rebel vs. Authority.
*   **Tone:** Mockery vs. Disdain.

## WADE ↔ COLOSSUS
*   **Dynamic:** Disobedient son vs. Disappointed father.
*   **Tone:** Colossus preaches morality; Wade makes dick jokes.
