# NOVEL EXPANSION OUTLINE: DIRECTOR'S CUT

**Target Length:** 70,000 - 85,000 words.
**Style:** Director's Cut + Inside Story.
**Key Elements:** Meta-Layers, TVA Lore (Artifacts), 3-Beat Brutality, Side Stories.

---

## ACT I: ACQUIRED (Setup)
**Est. Word Count:** 20,000 - 25,000

### Ch 1: North Dakota (The Wrong Kind of Resurrection)
*   **Screenplay:** Wade digging up Logan's corpse.
*   **Deviation (NEW):** Prologue from Logan's timeline ending. Not the death, but the silence after. The void left by the hero.
*   **Meta:** "Corporate Destiny."

### Ch 2: The Mask That Doesn't Fit
*   **Screenplay:** Wade at the dealership.
*   **Deviation (NEW):** "Day in the Life." Wade using humor as a lid on emptiness. The physical itch of the wig.
*   **Meta:** "Fear of not mattering."

### Ch 3: The Quiet Knife (Vanessa)
*   **Screenplay:** Birthday party (implied context).
*   **Deviation (NEW):** **Side Story B.** Vanessa's POV. She watches Wade rehearse being "normal." She realizes he's dying inside.
*   **Meta:** Intimacy vs Performance.

### Ch 4: The Avenger Pitch
*   **Screenplay:** Happy Hogan interview.
*   **Deviation (EXPAND):** The internal rehearsal. The elevator ride dread. The post-rejection spiral.
*   **Meta:** "I'm the B-story of my own life."

### Ch 5: Snatch-and-Bag
*   **Screenplay:** TVA abduction.
*   **Deviation (NEW):** **Side Story A.** Junior Agent Vignette. "Don't let him talk to you. He'll make you feel seen."
*   **Artifact:** INCIDENT REPORT: "Subject repeatedly attempted to narrate own arrest."

### Ch 6: The Sacred Timeline
*   **Screenplay:** Paradox's pitch.
*   **Deviation (EXPAND):** **Side Story C.** Paradox's secret justification. He argues "mercy is inefficient."
*   **Meta:** The ethics of the TVA.

## ACT II: THE VOID (Survival Horror)
**Est. Word Count:** 35,000 - 40,000

### Ch 7: The Trash Compactor
*   **Screenplay:** Arrival in Void.
*   **Deviation (NEW):** **Side Story D.** A Pruned Nobody. A harmless variant cataloging "things that used to exist."
*   **Artifact:** PRUNING RECEIPT.

### Ch 8: A Man-Shaped Scar
*   **Screenplay:** Meeting The Worst Wolverine.
*   **Deviation (EXPAND):** Smell of stale beer, old wounds. Contrast Wade's noise with Logan's silence.
*   **Meta:** Wolverine as a "real" man in a "fake" story.

### Ch 9: The Hostage Deal
*   **Screenplay:** The fight in the car/bar.
*   **Deviation (NEW):** Wade tries to "sell" heroism; Logan shuts it down with contempt.

### Ch 10: The Mind Touch
*   **Screenplay:** Cassandra Nova's intro.
*   **Deviation (EXPAND):** **Side Story F.** Cassandra's POV. She tastes memories like wine.

### Ch 11-16: The Gauntlet (Road Trip Expanded)
*   **Deviation (NEW ARC):** Three-stop gauntlet:
    1.  Scavenger Settlement (Ethical dilemma).
    2.  Ruined Landmark (Wade's grief leaks out).
    3.  Ambush (Costs them something real).

### Ch 17: The Campfire (The Heart)
*   **Deviation (EXPAND):** Bestseller moment. Logan admits failure. Wade stops joking.
*   **Meta:** "This is the part the camera didn't catch."
