# LINGUISTICS_GUIDE.md: Director's Cut Standards

## 1. The Four Meta-Layers
Deadpool's fourth-wall breaking must be formatted specifically to denote the *type* of commentary.

### A) Spoken Aloud (Performance)
*   **Format:** Standard Quotes.
*   **Usage:** Wade performing for an audience (even if they don't get it).
*   **Example:** "If you’re wondering how much blood is too much blood, the answer is: Disney doesn’t know either."

### B) Private Aside (Confidential)
*   **Format:** Italics, No Quotes.
*   **Usage:** A whisper to the reader. Vulnerable, cynical, or revealing.
*   **Example:** *Okay. This part looks heroic. It is not. This is what panic looks like when it puts on eyeliner.*

### C) Embedded Commentary (Seamless)
*   **Format:** Normal Paragraph Text.
*   **Usage:** Wade's brain is the narrator. Stephen King-style intimacy.
*   **Example:** The shovel hit something solid. That’s the thing about closure: you always imagine it’s soft.

### D) Footnotes (Director's Commentary)
*   **Format:** Superscript number + Bottom of page (or end of section).
*   **Usage:** Petty corrections, accidental emotional reveals. No trivia dumps.
*   **Example:** He’d practiced this speech in the mirror.¹
    *   *¹ Yes, I know that’s sad. No, you may not tweet about it.*

## 2. TVA Artifacts (Lore Injection)
Use these as palate cleansers between chapters.
*   **Formats:** Case Files, Incident Reports, Transcripts, Pruning Receipts.
*   **Tone:** Bureaucratic horror, dry humor.

## 3. Dialogue Mechanics
*   **"Said" Removal:** Mandatory. Use action beats.
*   **Interruptions:** Em-dashes (—).

## 4. Profanity
*   **Deadpool:** Creative, high frequency.
*   **Logan:** Blunt, angry.
*   **General:** R-Rated.
