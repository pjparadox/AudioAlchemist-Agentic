# STYLE_PROFILE: The Director's Cut Blend

## 1. Core Voice: The Weaponized Fourth Wall
*   **Defense Mechanism:** Humor hides fear.
*   **Control Tactic:** Narration controls the narrative.
*   **Magic Trick:** Misdirection before a gut punch.
*   **Rule:** The commentary can't only be jokes. It must occasionally be true.

## 2. Violence: The "3-Beat Brutality"
Every violent action must follow this sequence:
1.  **Mechanics:** What hits what. Specificity. (e.g., "The baton took him in the throat.")
2.  **Consequence:** Fluid, sound, failure. (e.g., "Not a clean hit. He gagged, eyes wet.")
3.  **Aftertaste:** Sensory/Psychological residue. (e.g., "Wade tasted copper. The joke came out thin.")

## 3. The Deadpool Glaze
*   Add a joke that *fails* because he is rattled.
*   Or a joke that lands but turns sad.
*   *Do not* put chaos in the physical consequence. Physics must be real. Chaos is in Wade's head.

## 4. Micro-Techniques
*   **Failure Verbs:** "His knee forgot its job."
*   **Disgusting Detail:** One per kill. (e.g., "The sound of a snapped umbrella.")
*   **Pain Grammar:** Short sentences. Fragments.

## 5. Atmosphere (Stephen King / Jun Prince)
*   **Interiority:** Explore the "B-Story" of Wade's life.
*   **Grounding:** Brand names, smells, the mundane horror of corporate existence.
