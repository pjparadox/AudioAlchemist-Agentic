# CHARACTER_VOICES.md: English Novelization

## WADE WILSON / DEADPOOL
*   **Vibe:** Chaotic, Meta-Aware, deeply insecure but hiding it behind 24/7 performance art.
*   **Voice:**
    *   **Vocabulary:** Pop culture encyclopedic, medical jargon mixed with playground insults.
    *   **Rhythm:** Staccato. Rapid-fire. Interrupts himself.
    *   **Quirks:** Uses "Capitalized Concepts" (e.g., Plot Armor). Breaks the fourth wall in narration.
    *   **Vulnerability:** When sincere (with Vanessa/Logan), the jokes stop. He speaks simply.

## WOLVERINE (LOGAN)
*   **Vibe:** Exhausted, self-loathing, reluctant.
*   **Voice:**
    *   **Vocabulary:** Minimalist. Grunts. "Bub."
    *   **Rhythm:** Slow, heavy. Like a tank engine turning over.
    *   **Quirks:** Uses threats as greetings.
    *   **Keywords:** "Kid," "Bub," "Shut up."

## VANESSA
*   **Vibe:** The Anchor. Grounded, witty, warm.
*   **Voice:**
    *   **Dynamic:** She keeps up with Wade. She is the only one who can banter with him on equal footing.
    *   **Tone:** Affectionate but firm.

## CASSANDRA NOVA
*   **Vibe:** Predatory aristocratic.
*   **Voice:**
    *   **Tone:** Soft, intimate, violating. Calls people "Darling" or "Pet."
    *   **Rhythm:** Languid. She has all the time in the world.
