# Project Context: Legal 303 Appeal

## Overview
This project involves the translation and processing of the "Revised Tagged 303 Appeal" document, a legal appeal arguing against the dismissal of a complaint for "failure to exhaust administrative remedies" against the IDHS.

## Domain
Legal / Judicial Appeal (Illinois Law).

## Key Parties
*   **Petitioners:** Nguyen et al.
*   **Respondents:** Illinois Department of Human Services (IDHS), Chief ALJ, Board of Education.

## Key Legal Concepts & Terminology
*   **Exhaustion Doctrine:** "Quy tắc/nguyên tắc hoàn tất thủ tục hành chính" (The requirement to use all agency remedies before going to court).
*   **Ultra Vires:** "Vượt quá thẩm quyền" (Acts beyond the legal power or authority of the agency).
*   **Administrative Review Law:** "Luật Xem xét Hành chính".
*   **Hardship Exception:** "Ngoại lệ do hoàn cảnh khó khăn" (89 Ill. Adm. Code § 590.680).
*   **Reset Letter:** "Thư Đặt lại/Hủy bỏ" (Mechanism used to nullify hearings).
*   **TRO (Temporary Restraining Order):** "Lệnh Ngăn chặn Tạm thời".
*   **Rule to Show Cause:** "Lệnh Yêu cầu Giải trình".

## Tone & Style
*   **Tone:** Formal, argumentative, precise, authoritative.
*   **Style:** High-fidelity legal translation (Gavel Division). Use "Toà án" for Court, "Nguyên đơn" for Petitioners (or "Người kháng cáo" if specific to appeal context, but usually Petitioners in admin review).
