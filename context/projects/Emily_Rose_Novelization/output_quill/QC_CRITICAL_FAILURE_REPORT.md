## CRITICAL FAILURE: DENSITY & LENGTH
*   **Current Word Count:** 8,670 words.
*   **Target Word Count:** 40,000+ words (Standard Novel).
*   **Deficit:** ~32,000 words.

### Analysis of Failure
1.  **Summary Syndrome:** The previous draft summarized scenes (e.g., "The trial dragged on") rather than inhabiting them. A novel must show the dragging—the smell of wet wool in the courtroom, the specific boredom of the bailiff, the sound of the clock.
2.  **Missing Beats:** While all *scenes* were covered, the *beats* within scenes were compressed. Dialogue was stripped of its silence.
3.  **Style Dilution:** The "King" atmosphere and "Blackmore" violence were present but fleeting. They need to be sustained.

### Remediation Plan (The 17th Loop)
*   **Mandate:** 1 Script Chunk (1000 words) = 1 Novel Chapter (1500-2000 words).
*   **Ghost Writing:** We must invent sensory details that do not exist in the script. We must write the internal monologues that the camera implies but cannot show.
*   **No Skipping:** Every line of dialogue in the script must be evaluated. If it's kept, it must be surrounded by prose. If it's cut, it must be replaced by narrative equivalence.

**VERDICT:** REJECTED. INITIATE DEEP RE-DRAFT.
