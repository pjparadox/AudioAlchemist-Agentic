# RELATIONSHIP_MATRIX.md: The Exorcism of Emily Rose (Novelization)

## Core Dynamic: The Trial
* **Erin Bruner (Attorney) <-> Father Richard Moore (Defendant)**
    * *Formal:* Attorney/Client.
    * *Emotional:* Skeptic vs. Believer. Erin starts viewing him as a "job" (Prince: Cynical), but slowly becomes his "protector" against the system.
    * *Arc:* Transactional -> Respect -> Shared Trauma.

* **Erin Bruner <-> Ethan Thomas (Prosecutor)**
    * *Formal:* Opposing Counsel.
    * *Emotional:* Intellectual Rivals. Ethan is "Old School/Church Goer" vs. Erin's "Modern Ambition".
    * *Dynamic:* Respectful but cutthroat. (Prince style banter).

## The Haunting (The Past)
* **Emily Rose <-> Father Moore**
    * *Formal:* Priest/Parishioner.
    * *Emotional:* Shepherd/Lamb. Moore failed to save her life but is trying to save her soul/story.
    * *Dynamic:* Intimate, tragic, paternal.

* **Emily Rose <-> The Demon (The Entity)**
    * *Formal:* Possessor/Vessel.
    * *Emotional:* Predator/Prey.
    * *Dynamic:* (Blackmore Style) Violent, intrusive, absolute domination.

## The Family
* **Emily <-> Her Parents (Nathaniel & Maria)**
    * *Formal:* Child/Parents.
    * *Emotional:* Loving but overwhelmed. They are simple people caught in a cosmic war. (King Style: salt-of-the-earth, helpless).

## Character Voices
1. **Erin:** Sharp, clipped, internal monologue full of legal strategy and doubt.
2. **Moore:** Soft-spoken, resolute, biblical cadence.
3. **Thomas:** Theatrical, righteous, "folksy" but sharp.
4. **Emily:** Normal college girl -> Fragmented, guttural, panicked.
