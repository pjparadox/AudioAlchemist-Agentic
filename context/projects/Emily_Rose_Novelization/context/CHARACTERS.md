# CHARACTERS.md: The Exorcism of Emily Rose

## Protagonists

### Erin Bruner
* **Role:** Defense Attorney. Rising Star.
* **Appearance:** Sharp suits, tired eyes masked by expensive makeup. "A shark that hasn't slept in a week."
* **Motivation:** Making Partner. Winning.
* **Internal Conflict:** She trades conscience for victory. This case forces her to confront the void.
* **Style Note (Prince):** Cynical, drinks too much coffee, checks her Blackberry constantly.

### Father Richard Moore
* **Role:** The Defendant. Parish Priest.
* **Appearance:** Worn collar, hands rough from work, eyes that have seen the abyss.
* **Motivation:** Telling Emily's story. He doesn't care about jail; he cares about the Truth.
* **Internal Conflict:** Guilt. Did he kill her by stopping the medication? Or did he save her soul?
* **Style Note (King):** Smells of incense and stale fear. Quiet intensity.

## Antagonists

### Ethan Thomas
* **Role:** Prosecutor. "God's Lawyer."
* **Appearance:** Sunday best, choir singer demeanor, razor-sharp mind.
* **Motivation:** Convict Moore to protect the rational world (and the Church's reputation, ironically).
* **Style Note:** Uses "folksy" charm to hide a steel trap.

### The Six (The Demons)
* **Role:** The Occupiers.
* **Manifestation:** Voices, shadows, smells (burning hair, sulfur).
* **Style Note (Blackmore):** When present, prose becomes jagged, violent, sensory-overloaded.

## Supporting

### Emily Rose
* **Role:** The Victim (Flashbacks).
* **Appearance:** Starts as a fresh-faced country girl. Devolves into a bruised, emaciated vessel.
* **Style Note:** Tragic innocence lost.

### Judge Brewster
* **Role:** The Arbiter.
* **Style Note:** No-nonsense, tired of theatrics.
