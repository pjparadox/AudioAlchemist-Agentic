# STYLE_GUIDE.md: The Emily Rose Novelization Project (Revised)

## 1. The Voice Fusion Mix
To achieve the "Bestseller Quality" requested, the narrative must blend four distinct voices.

*   **Stephen King (50% - The Foundation):**
    *   *Atmosphere:* Use sensory details to ground the horror. The smell of ozone, the specific brand of coffee, the texture of the snow.
    *   *Interiority:* Deep dives into character thought processes. Use *italics* for direct internal thought.
    *   *Dread:* The horror should feel like it's seeping out of the landscape itself.
    *   *Structure:* Conversational but ominous.

*   **Jun Prince (30% - The Modern Lens):**
    *   *Cynicism:* Applied to Erin Bruner and the legal system. The "billable hour," the shark-like nature of the firm.
    *   *Modernity:* References to technology and the absurdity of modern life vs. ancient evil.
    *   *Constraint:* **NO LITRPG ELEMENTS.** No stats, no system boxes, no "leveling up" notifications. The influence is strictly tonal (cynicism/modernity).

*   **Keith C. Blackmore (Violence/Horror - The Spike):**
    *   *Trigger:* Use strictly for **Exorcism Scenes**, **Possession Manifestations**, and **Violent Deaths**.
    *   *Style:* Visceral, kinetic, gross.
    *   *Keywords:* Wet, grinding, tearing, meat, bone, popping, sulfur, rot.

*   **PirateAba (20% - The Scope):**
    *   *Worldbuilding:* Treat the courtroom and the farm as distinct "realms."
    *   *Epic Pacing:* Allow scenes to breathe. Build the "System" of the trial.
    *   *Constraint:* **NO LITRPG ELEMENTS.** Focus on the emotional "leveling up" (growth) of characters through narrative arc, not mechanics.

## 2. The "Ghost Writing" Mandate
*   **Expansion:** A single scene from the script must be expanded into a full chapter.
*   **Sensory Fill:** Describe the cold, the wind, the sound of the dry cornstalks, the smell of the coming snow.
*   **Internal Monologue:** Provide the *thought* before the dialogue.

## 3. Formatting
*   **POV:** Third Person Past (Limited).
*   **Tense:** Past.
*   **Dialogue:** Standard double quotes.
