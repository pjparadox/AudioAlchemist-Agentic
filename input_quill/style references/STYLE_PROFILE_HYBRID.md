# STYLE_PROFILE_HYBRID.md: The Chimichanga of Horror

## 1. The Blend
This novelization utilizes a "Poly-Voice" strategy to maximize impact across different narrative modes.

### A. Stephen King (Atmosphere & Interiority) - 40%
*   **Role:** Grounding the supernatural in the mundane.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **Brand Names as Anchors:** Use specific products (e.g., "drinking a lukewarm Pepsi", "the hum of the GE refrigerator") to create reality.
    *   **The "Constant Reader" Voice:** Folksy, conversational narration that occasionally breaks the fourth wall subtly ("You might think you know fear, but...").
    *   **Italics for Thought:** *Oh god, oh jesus, it's inside me.*
    *   **Sentence Structure:** Run-on sentences connected by conjunctions to build anxiety, followed by a short, sharp shock.

### B. Jun Prince (The Cynical Analyst) - 30%
*   **Role:** The Legal and Intellectual Framework.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **Cold Logic:** dissecting emotional moments with surgical precision.
    *   **Status Awareness:** Hyper-focus on who has the power in the room.
    *   **No Fluff:** Short, punchy declarations. "Erin didn't believe in God. She believed in billable hours."

### C. Neal Shusterman (Pacing & Scope) - 20%
*   **Role:** Narrative Drive and "Unwinding" Tension.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **Present Tense Immediacy:** (Used in high-tension moments).
    *   **Shifting Perspectives:** Seamlessly moving from the macro (society/courtroom) to the micro (a fly on the wall).
    *   **Philosophical Resonance:** Ending chapters with a lingering moral question.

### D. Keith C. Blackmore (Visceral Violence) - 10% (100% in Horror Scenes)
*   **Role:** The Exorcism and Physical Trauma.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **Anatomical Precision:** "The sound of the patella snapping was like a dry branch stepping on a wet sponge."
    *   **Sensory Overload:** Focus on smell (urine, sulfur, copper blood) and taste.
    *   **Brutality:** No looking away. The camera zooms *in* on the wound.

## 2. Formatting Rules
*   **Dialogue Tags:** ELIMINATE "said" wherever possible. Use action beats.
    *   *Bad:* "I don't know," she said.
    *   *Good:* Erin clicked her pen, the sound echoing in the silent room. "I don't know."
*   **Sensory Expansion:** Every scene must engage at least 3 senses.
*   **Paragraphing:** Vary length. Use single-line paragraphs for impact.
